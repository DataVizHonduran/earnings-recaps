## Sector Consensus

Demand across the Communication Services sector is generally robust, though activity is bifurcated by technology cycle. A strong consensus exists regarding the acceleration of AI-driven services and wireless connectivity, with Alphabet (GOOGL), Meta (META), T-Mobile (TMUS), and AT&T (T) reporting robust growth in AI infrastructure, ad impressions, and postpaid account additions. Conversely, a moderate softening is evident in legacy product lines; Charter (CHTR), Comcast (CMCSA), and Warner Bros. Discovery (WBD) all report declining demand for traditional linear video and residential broadband. Capital spending remains robust across the majority of firms, as companies like Alphabet, Meta, and Verizon (VZ) aggressively expand data center capacity and 5G/fiber footprints to support next-generation connectivity.
