## Sector Consensus

Activity across the energy sector is generally robust, though characterized by a sharp divergence between liquids and natural gas. Upstream production and midstream throughput are expanding, with firms such as **ExxonMobil**, **Chevron**, **Diamondback Energy**, and **EOG Resources** reporting robust production growth, particularly in the Permian Basin and Guyana. Midstream activity is similarly robust, as evidenced by record volumes at **Targa Resources** and **ONEOK**. However, a moderate to slight contraction is evident in U.S. natural gas, where **APA Corp**, **Diamondback Energy**, and **Occidental Petroleum** report production curtailments and negative realized prices driven by infrastructure constraints at the Waha hub. Demand signals further diverge by end-market: while traditional transportation fuels show stable to softening demand (**Chevron**, **Phillips 66**), there is robust emerging demand for power infrastructure and data center energy supply (**Williams Companies**, **Baker Hughes**, **EQT Corp**).

Demand is bifurcated between traditional fuels and new power infrastructure. **Chevron** and **Phillips 66** report softening demand for gasoline and diesel, while **Williams Companies** and **EQT Corp** cite robust growth in natural gas demand driven by data center development in Ohio and Appalachia. **Baker Hughes** reports record orders for industrial energy technology linked to power generation for AI data centers.

**Labor Markets & Wages**

Firms are experiencing robust pricing for crude oil but persistent inflationary pressure on inputs. **Occidental Petroleum** and **EOG Resources** report robust realized crude prices, while **Baker Hughes** cites persistent inflationary costs within its Oilfield Services Equipment segment. To counter these pressures, **ExxonMobil** and **Chevron** are implementing aggressive structural cost-saving programs to protect margins.

To mitigate future bottlenecks, **Williams Companies** is making cash purchases of long-lead Power Innovation equipment, and **ExxonMobil** is actively rerouting global product shipments to optimize its portfolio amid Middle East disruptions.
