## Sector Consensus

Activity across the energy sector is generally robust, though characterized by a sharp divergence between liquids and natural gas. Most firms, including **ExxonMobil**, **Chevron**, **Devon Energy**, and **Diamondback Energy**, report strong production growth and record throughput in oil and liquids.
