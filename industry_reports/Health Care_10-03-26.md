## Sector Consensus

Demand across the Health Care sector is generally **robust**, though highly bifurcated by product lifecycle and geography. A majority of firms, including **AbbVie**, **Amgen**, **Bristol Myers Squibb**, and **Biogen**, report strong growth in "growth portfolios" or new product launches (e.g., immunology and rare disease) while experiencing a **moderate** to **sharp** decline in legacy portfolios due to biosimilar competition and generic impacts. ## Key Thematic Headings

**Consumer/Business Demand**
Demand is robust for specialized therapies and high-tech devices, such as **Dexcom's** CGM and **Edwards Lifesciences'** TAVR systems. Conversely, legacy pharmaceutical lines are softening, with **Amgen** reporting accelerated erosion for Prolia and Xgeva due to biosimilar launches. In the services sector, **Quest Diagnostics** reports robust growth in Alzheimer’s and cardiometabolic testing.

**Labor Markets & Wages**
Labor signals are largely absent across the sector, though specific pressures exist in high-touch care. **DaVita** reports persistent upward pressure on patient care costs driven by increased compensation expenses. **Centene** is pursuing moderate headcount reductions through "enterprise optimization" and contract exits, incurring significant severance costs.

**Price & Cost Pressures**
Pricing power is mixed and often regulatory-driven. **Cigna** and **Centene** are implementing premium rate increases to offset medical cost trends, while **Amgen** reports a 22% lower net selling price for Enbrel due to Medicare Part D price setting. Input cost pressures remain a headwind for **GE HealthCare**, which cites significant inflation in memory chips, oil, and freight.

**Inventory & Logistics**
Inventory levels are generally stable but vary by product. **Quest Diagnostics** and **Dexcom** report moderate increases in inventory levels, while **Biogen** notes that inventory dynamics are creating short-term volatility in reported sales for Vumerity. Logistics risks are highlighted by **Align Technology**, which cites potential freight disruptions stemming from geopolitical instability in the Middle East.
