## Sector Consensus

The Utilities sector is experiencing robust long-term demand growth, primarily catalyzed by the rapid expansion of data centers and hyperscale technology users. This trend is most pronounced in Texas and the Southeast, as reported by **American Electric Power (AEP)**, **CenterPoint Energy (CNP)**, and **Southern Company (SO)**. While long-term signals are strong, short-term retail activity remains sensitive to weather anomalies; for instance, **Ameren Corp (AEE)** and **NRG Energy** noted softening retail sales due to warmer-than-normal winter temperatures. Capital expenditure remains robust across the sector, with firms like **Exelon Corp (EXC)** and **Sempra (SRE)** executing multi-billion dollar modernization plans to enhance grid resilience and capacity. Pricing power is generally moderate to stable, as firms such as **Eversource Energy (ES)** and **American Water Works (AWK)** successfully utilize regulatory channels to implement rate increases to recover infrastructure costs.
