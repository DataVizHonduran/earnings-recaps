## Sector Consensus

Demand across the Consumer Discretionary sector is generally robust, though activity is increasingly bifurcated by product line and geography. Travel and leisure services—including **Carnival Corp**, **Hilton**, **Marriott**, and **Booking Holdings**—report strong underlying demand and record bookings, though this is tempered by slight softening in EMEA and Asia Pacific due to geopolitical volatility.
