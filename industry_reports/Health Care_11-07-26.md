A majority of firms, including **AbbVie**, **Amgen**, **Bristol Myers Squibb**, and **Biogen**, report strong growth in "growth portfolios" or new product launches (e.g., immunology and rare disease), while simultaneously experiencing **softening** demand for legacy portfolios due to biosimilar competition and generic erosion. ## Key Thematic Headings

**Consumer/Business Demand**
Demand is robust for high-innovation products, such as **AbbVie’s** Skyrizi and **Amgen’s** Imdelltra, but softening for legacy lines like **Amgen’s** Prolia. In the services sector, **Quest Diagnostics** reports robust requisition volume growth driven by an aging population and preventative care. Conversely, **Abbott Laboratories** is seeing a moderate contraction in worldwide nutrition sales.

**Labor Markets & Wages**
Labor signals are largely absent for most firms, but **DaVita Inc.** reports that increased compensation expenses are driving up patient care costs. **Centene Corp** is pursuing enterprise optimization and contract exits, resulting in estimated severance costs between $355 million and $405 million. **Elevance Health** notes that its operating expense ratio was driven upward by targeted investments in its workforce.

**Price & Cost Pressures**
Pricing power is mixed; **Cigna** and **Centene Corp** are implementing premium rate increases to offset medical cost trends. However, **Amgen** reports a 22% lower net selling price for Enbrel due to U.S. Medicare Part D price setting under the Inflation Reduction Act. **Cardinal Health** and **Baxter International** both cite tariffs and higher manufacturing costs as persistent pressures on margins.

**Inventory & Logistics**
Inventory levels are generally stable, though **Quest Diagnostics** and **Dexcom** report moderate year-over-year increases in inventory values. **Biogen** notes that short-term revenue for Vumerity and Spinraza has been impacted by inventory dynamics and stocking for high-dose regimens. **Align Technology** identifies potential logistics risks and shipping freight disruptions stemming from military action in the Middle East.
