## Sector Consensus

Demand across the Information Technology sector is robust, primarily catalyzed by the global build-out of AI infrastructure and the integration of agentic AI capabilities. This momentum is evident across diverse industry groups, including Technology Hardware & Equipment (AAPL, CSCO, ANET), Semiconductors (AMAT, AVGO, AMD), and Software & Services (ADBE, CRM, CDNS). Growth is broad-based geographically, with particular strength noted in the Americas and Asia Pacific. However, signals diverge by end-market: while AI-driven data center and enterprise networking demand is accelerating, legacy segments—such as AMD’s gaming business and AKAM’s delivery services—are softening. Similarly, consumer-facing hardware shows a divergence where revenue is growing due to premium AI-enabled devices, but overall unit volumes are declining.

## Key Thematic Headings

**Consumer/Business Demand**
Business demand is robust, with Broadcom and AMD reporting massive growth in AI accelerators and data center revenue. Enterprise networking is seeing a "supercycle," as evidenced by Cisco’s record revenue and HPE’s 148% increase in networking revenue. Consumer demand is mixed; Apple reports record iPhone 17 demand, while HP Inc. notes a significant decline in consumer printing and personal systems unit volumes.

**Labor Markets & Wages**
Labor signals are generally stable, though some firms are prioritizing efficiency over expansion. CDW reports moderate upward pressure on compensation expenses, while Cognizant is implementing "Project Leap" to properly size its workforce, incurring significant severance costs. Cisco reports its highest productivity metrics in 30 years per employee, and Salesforce continues to execute restructuring initiatives.

**Price & Cost Pressures**
Pricing power is robust for premium AI offerings, with Salesforce seeing bookings for premium SKUs more than double. Autodesk is actively improving price realization by reducing multi-year discounts. Conversely, HP Inc. is navigating rising commodity costs, and Cisco is incorporating the estimated impact of tariffs into its future guidance.

**Inventory & Logistics**
Inventory levels are increasing moderately as firms buffer for AI growth, with Apple and Arista Networks reporting notable stock build-ups. Applied Materials has intentionally increased inventory and logistics capacity to ensure operational readiness. Supply constraints remain a bottleneck for some, with AMD noting that data center growth is tied to the ability to scale supply to meet demand.
