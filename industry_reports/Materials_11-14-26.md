## Sector Consensus

Demand across the Materials sector is generally moderate, though characterized by significant bifurcation across end-markets. A robust trend is evident in high-technology and specialized industrial applications, specifically semiconductors, AI data centers, and aerospace, as noted by **Air Products & Chemicals (APD)**, **Ecolab (ECL)**, and **PPG Industries (PPG)**. Conversely, traditional industrial and consumer-facing segments—including automotive, petrochemicals, and residential construction—are experiencing slight to moderate softening, a trend cited by **Albemarle (ALB)**, **DuPont (DD)**, and **Dow (DOW)**. Pricing power is largely stable to robust, with many firms utilizing contractual pass-through mechanisms or value-based pricing to offset input inflation, exemplified by **Ball Corp (BALL)**, **Nucor (NUE)**, and **Albemarle (ALB)**.
