Demand is robust and accelerating, particularly for AI-integrated products and infrastructure. **Broadcom (AVGO)** reports AI semiconductor revenue growth of 221% year-over-year, while **Cisco (CSCO)** is seeing a "networking supercycle" with campus networking orders growing over 25%. **Salesforce (CRM)** and **Adobe (ADBE)** report robust demand for "Agentic AI" and AI-first ARR, respectively.
