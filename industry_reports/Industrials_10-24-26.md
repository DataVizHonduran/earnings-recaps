## Sector Consensus

Demand across the Industrials sector is generally robust, though growth is increasingly bifurcated by end-market. A majority of firms, including **AMETEK**, **Caterpillar**, **GE Aerospace**, **Fastenal**, and **Eaton**, report strong order activity and expanding backlogs. This momentum is most pronounced in "secular growth" verticals, specifically data center infrastructure and aerospace. However, signals diverge sharply in residential and consumer-facing markets; while commercial activity is strong, firms like **A.O. Smith** and **Builders FirstSource** report softening demand linked to housing affordability and weak consumer spending. Geographically, North American activity remains stable to robust, whereas international demand—particularly in China and Europe—is described as moderate to softening by **Allegion** and **A.O. Smith**.

## Key Thematic Headings

**Consumer/Business Demand**
Business demand is accelerating in high-tech infrastructure, with **GE Vernova** booking $2.4 billion in data center equipment and **Eaton** seeing a 42% order acceleration in Electrical Americas. Conversely, consumer-linked demand is weak, as **A.O. Smith** notes residential water heater volumes are below expectations. **AXON** reports robust expansion into adjacent verticals, including healthcare and enterprise markets.

**Labor Markets & Wages**
Labor conditions are generally stable, though specific pockets of pressure persist. **Cummins** reports a challenging market for attracting and retaining key personnel, while **Delta Air Lines** notes a continuation of higher crew costs. **FedEx** reports that operating results were offset by higher wage rates and variable incentive compensation.

**Price & Cost Pressures**
Most firms maintain moderate to robust pricing power, with **Fastenal** contributing approximately 240-350 basis points to net sales via pricing. However, these gains are often offset by input costs, such as **Caterpillar** reporting unfavorable manufacturing costs due to higher tariffs. **Broadridge** is successfully passing through significant postage rate increases to customers.

**Inventory & Logistics**
Backlogs are reaching record levels for heavy industrials, with **Boeing** reporting a record $715 billion backlog and **GE Vernova** seeing its backlog grow to $163 billion. Supply chain throughput is improving for some, as **GE Aerospace** notes double-digit sequential increases in material input from priority suppliers. **Caterpillar** reports that dealer inventories increased more in early 2026 than in the prior year.
