A majority of firms, including **AbbVie**, **Boston Scientific**, **Dexcom**, **Edwards Lifesciences**, and **Quest Diagnostics**, report strong growth in new product launches and specialized therapies. ## Key Thematic Headings

**Consumer/Business Demand**
Demand is robust for innovation-led products, such as **AbbVie’s** Skyrizi and **Biogen’s** Leqembi. Conversely, legacy products are softening, evidenced by **Amgen’s** sales erosion in Prolia and **Bristol Myers Squibb’s** decline in its Legacy Portfolio. High-growth segments include **Quest Diagnostics'** double-digit growth in Alzheimer’s testing and **Dexcom's** expansion into non-insulin-using type 2 diabetics.

**Labor Markets & Wages**
Labor signals are sparse, but persistent upward pressure is noted in specialized care, with **DaVita** citing increased compensation expenses as a primary driver of patient care costs. Some firms are pursuing efficiency through restructuring, such as **Centene Corp's** enterprise optimization and **Charles River Laboratories'** site consolidation and severance initiatives. **Elevance Health** reports a modest increase in spending driven by targeted investments in its workforce.

**Price & Cost Pressures**
Pricing power is mixed; **Cigna** and **Centene Corp** are implementing premium rate increases to offset medical cost trends. However, regulatory headwinds are evident, with **Amgen** noting a 22% lower net selling price for Enbrel due to Medicare Part D price setting. Input cost pressures remain a risk, with **GE HealthCare** citing significant inflation in memory chips, oil, and freight.

**Inventory & Logistics**
Inventory levels are generally stable but vary by product, with **Biogen** noting that Vumerity revenue was impacted by inventory dynamics. Logistics risks are tied to geopolitics, as **Align Technology** identifies potential freight disruptions stemming from military action in the Middle East. Some firms are seeing inventory growth, such as **Quest Diagnostics**, which reported an increase in inventories from $189 million to $233 million.
