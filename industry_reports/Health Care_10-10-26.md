Demand is robust for innovation-led products, such as **AbbVie’s** Skyrizi and **Biogen’s** Leqembi. Conversely, legacy portfolios are softening, evidenced by **Amgen’s** sales erosion in Prolia and **Bristol Myers Squibb’s** decline in its legacy portfolio. Labor signals are largely muted across the sector, though some firms are pursuing efficiency. **Centene** is implementing "enterprise optimization" involving severance and contract exits, while **Elevance Health** reports increased spending on targeted workforce investments. **DaVita** reports that increased compensation expenses are a primary driver of rising patient care and administrative costs.

**Inventory & Logistics**
