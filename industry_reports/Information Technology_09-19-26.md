## Sector Consensus

Demand across the Information Technology sector is robust, primarily catalyzed by the global build-out of AI computing infrastructure and the transition toward "agentic" AI experiences. This momentum is broad-based, with firms such as **Broadcom**, **Applied Materials**, **Arista Networks**, **Nvidia**, and **Salesforce** reporting significant growth in AI-related revenue and bookings. Activity is particularly strong in data center networking, custom AI accelerators, and enterprise software platforms. However, signals diverge by end-market; while AI-aligned infrastructure is surging, legacy segments—such as **Akamai’s** delivery services and **AMD’s** gaming business—are softening. Geographically, growth is generally robust, though some firms note a transition in the Americas from AI exploration to production environments.

## Key Thematic Headings

**Consumer/Business Demand**
Business demand is robust and accelerating, specifically for AI-driven infrastructure and software. **Broadcom** reported AI semiconductor revenue growth of 221% year-over-year, while **Salesforce** noted that Agentforce and Data 360 ARR grew over 210%. In the hardware space, **Cisco** is experiencing a "networking supercycle," with networking product orders growing 40% year-over-year.

**Labor Markets & Wages**
Labor trends are mixed, with a strategic shift toward productivity and re-skilling over aggressive headcount expansion. **Cognizant** is implementing "Project Leap" to ensure its workforce is properly sized, anticipating up to $270 million in severance costs. Conversely, **CDW** reports moderate upward pressure on labor costs, citing an 8.6% increase in selling and administrative expenses due to higher compensation and performance-based incentives.

**Price & Cost Pressures**
Pricing power is generally strong, though some firms face persistent input cost volatility. **Autodesk** is actively improving price realization by reducing multi-year discounts and winding down certain subscription renewals. In contrast, **HP Inc.** continues to navigate a challenging cost environment, specifically citing the need to mitigate rising commodity costs for raw materials.

**Inventory & Logistics**
Inventory levels are increasing moderately as firms buffer against high-growth cycles. **Apple** saw inventories rise to $11.09 billion from $5.72 billion year-over-year, while **Arista Networks** increased stock to $2.54 billion to support AI fabric platforms. To mitigate logistics risks, **Fortinet** is diversifying its hardware pipeline through a strategic partnership with Intel to strengthen global supply chain resilience.
