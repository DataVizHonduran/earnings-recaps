## Sector Consensus

Demand across the Information Technology sector is robust, driven primarily by a global build-out of AI computing infrastructure and a corresponding "networking supercycle." This momentum is evident across multiple industry groups, including Broadcom [Semiconductors & Semiconductor Equipment], Arista Networks [Technology Hardware & Equipment], Salesforce [Software & Services], Applied Materials [Semiconductors & Semiconductor Equipment], and Cisco Systems [Technology Hardware & Equipment]. Most firms report strong activity in AI-driven accelerators, data center networking, and enterprise software transformation. However, signals diverge by end-market; while AI-centric product lines are expanding rapidly, legacy segments—such as gaming for AMD [Semiconductors & Semiconductor Equipment] and delivery services for Akamai [Software & Services]—are softening. Geographically, activity is broad-based, though some firms report particularly robust growth in the Americas and Asia Pacific.

## Divergences & Outliers

Certain firms contradict the broader sector trend of robust volume growth. HP Inc. [Technology Hardware & Equipment] reports a significant softening in unit volumes for personal systems, despite growth in total value. This divergence is driven by a product-mix shift, where customers are migrating toward higher-value, premium AI-enabled devices. In the professional services space, Cognizant [Software & Services] describes a "complex" macroeconomic environment with mixed short-term signals, including a slight decline in quarterly bookings, contrasting with the record-breaking momentum seen by pure-play AI software providers. This is likely due to a broader end-market mix and a higher sensitivity to general enterprise spending volatility. Additionally, Akamai [Software & Services] shows a sharp internal divergence, where robust expansion in cloud infrastructure is offset by softening demand for legacy cloud applications, reflecting a structural shift in customer architecture.

## Key Thematic Headings

**Consumer/Business Demand**
Business demand is robust and accelerating, centered on the adoption of agentic AI and infrastructure modernization. Broadcom [Semiconductors & Semiconductor Equipment] and Intel [Semiconductors & Semiconductor Equipment] report unprecedented demand for AI silicon and data center accelerators. Similarly, Salesforce [Software & Services] notes incredible demand for AI and data products, while Cisco Systems [Technology Hardware & Equipment] is seeing a major multi-year campus networking refresh cycle.

**Labor Markets & Wages**
Labor trends are mixed, with a strategic shift toward workforce optimization and specialized talent. CDW [Technology Hardware & Equipment] reports moderate upward pressure on compensation expenses, while Cognizant [Software & Services] is implementing reskilling initiatives and workforce sizing adjustments. IBM [Software & Services] is specifically increasing investment in specialized technical talent, such as Forward Deployed Engineers, to support enterprise AI deployment.

**Price & Cost Pressures**
Pricing power is generally stable to strong, though some firms face persistent input cost volatility. FICO [Software & Services] and Autodesk [Software & Services] demonstrate robust pricing power through unit price increases and the reduction of multi-year discounts. Conversely, HP Inc. [Technology Hardware & Equipment] is navigating rising commodity costs, while Apple [Technology Hardware & Equipment] and Amphenol [Technology Hardware & Equipment] have noted moderate relief from tariff refunds.

**Inventory & Logistics**
Inventory levels are increasing moderately as firms buffer against AI-driven demand surges. Applied Materials [Semiconductors & Semiconductor Equipment] has intentionally increased its build plan and logistics capacity to ensure operational readiness. However, supply remains a constraint for some, with AMD [Semiconductors & Semiconductor Equipment] noting that growth is tied to the ability to scale supply, and Arista Networks [Technology Hardware & Equipment] flagging risks related to sole-source component procurement.
