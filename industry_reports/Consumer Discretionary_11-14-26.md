Demand across the Consumer Discretionary sector is generally robust, though growth rates vary by end-market. Consumer Services firms, including **Carnival Corp**, **Hilton**, **Expedia**, and **Booking Holdings**, report strong activity, particularly in the U.S. and expansion markets like India. ## Key Thematic Headings

**Consumer/Business Demand**
Demand is robust in travel and digital marketplaces, with **Carnival Corp** reporting record bookings and **eBay** seeing triple-digit GMV growth in live shopping. In the automotive sector, demand is bifurcated; **General Motors** reports robust North American profitability, while **Ford** notes a significant drop in EV wholesales. Retail demand is more tempered, with **Home Depot** observing a shift toward smaller-scale projects and **Best Buy** seeing a decline in traditional gaming.

**Labor Markets & Wages**
Labor costs are experiencing moderate upward pressure in the service and food sectors, with **Chipotle** and **Darden Restaurants** citing wage inflation and higher benefits expenses. Some firms are mitigating these pressures through technology; **Airbnb** reports that over 40% of customer support issues are now resolved via AI without human agents. **Darden Restaurants** notes a positive outlier in labor stability, reporting historically high team member and manager retention.

**Price & Cost Pressures**
Moderate pricing power persists in travel, with **Expedia** and **Airbnb** reporting increases in Average Daily Rates (ADR). However, persistent input cost inflation remains a headwind for manufacturers and operators, specifically beef and freight costs for **Chipotle** and aluminum costs for **Ford**. Some firms have received modest relief through government actions, such as **Hasbro** and **Home Depot** benefiting from IEEPA tariff refunds.

**Inventory & Logistics**
Logistics efficiency is improving in high-scale retail, with **Amazon** expanding ultra-fast delivery to billions of items. Inventory levels are being managed leanly in some sectors, such as **Carnival Corp**, which reports the lowest inventory of rooms for sale on record. Conversely, **AutoZone** and **Home Depot** have increased merchandise inventories moderately to support growth initiatives and offset inflationary replacement costs.
