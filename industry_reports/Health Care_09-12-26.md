## Sector Consensus

Demand across the health care sector is generally robust, though activity is highly bifurcated by product lifecycle and geography. A common theme among firms—including AbbVie, Amgen, Boston Scientific, Dexcom, and GE HealthCare—is strong momentum for innovative, high-growth product lines and specialized therapies, which is frequently offset by softening demand for legacy portfolios and mature product lines. International markets are often described as robust, particularly in the Asia-Pacific and Latin American regions, while North American demand is more frequently characterized as stable or moderate. Signals diverge significantly by end-market: while advanced diagnostics and specialized medical devices show strong growth, demand is softening in nutrition, legacy multiple sclerosis treatments, and certain government-sponsored insurance memberships.

## Key Thematic Headings

**Consumer/Business Demand**
Demand is robust for next-generation technologies and specialized care, such as the growth portfolios at Bristol Myers Squibb and the advanced diagnostics at Quest Diagnostics. Conversely, legacy products are softening, evidenced by the decline in AbbVie’s Humira and Amgen’s Prolia due to biosimilar competition. In the services sector, demand is mixed, with robust specialty volume at Cigna offset by softening pharmacy customer membership.

**Labor Markets & Wages**
Labor signals are sparse, though some firms are pursuing organizational efficiency through restructuring, as seen with Centene and Becton Dickinson. DaVita reports that increased compensation expenses are a primary driver of higher patient care and administrative costs. Elevance Health notes that targeted investments in its workforce have contributed to a rise in its operating expense ratio.

**Price & Cost Pressures**
Pricing power is moderate but faces regulatory headwinds, with Amgen citing the Inflation Reduction Act as a driver for lower net selling prices for Enbrel. Input cost pressures are evident in the equipment sector, where GE HealthCare is navigating spikes in chip and freight costs and Cardinal Health is managing the adverse impact of tariffs. Some insurers, such as Cigna and Centene, are utilizing premium rate increases to offset elevated medical cost trends.

**Inventory & Logistics**
Inventory levels are increasing moderately for several diagnostics and device firms, including Quest Diagnostics, Dexcom, and GE HealthCare. Logistics remain a point of caution, with Align Technology citing potential shipping freight disruptions stemming from geopolitical instability in the Middle East. In the pharmaceutical space, Biogen notes that inventory dynamics are creating short-term volatility in reported sales for specific products.
