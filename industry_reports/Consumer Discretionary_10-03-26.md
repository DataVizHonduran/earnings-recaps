## Sector Consensus

Consumer Discretionary activity is generally robust, though growth is increasingly bifurcated by product line and geography. A majority of firms, including **AMZN**, **BKNG**, **HLT**, **CCL**, and **EBAY**, report strong top-line momentum and accelerating demand. This strength is most evident in high-end collectibles, travel services, and digital retail. However, signals diverge sharply by end-market: while travel and luxury-adjacent services remain robust, "big-ticket" consumer durables and traditional DIY retail are experiencing modest to softening activity. Geographically, North American demand is stable to strong for most, but several firms report softening in EMEA and Asia Pacific, often citing geopolitical volatility as a primary headwind.

## Key Thematic Headings

**Consumer/Business Demand**
Demand is robust in travel and digital marketplaces but softening in home-related sectors. **CCL** reports record bookings extending into 2028, and **EBAY** notes accelerated GMV growth in "Focus Categories." Conversely, **HD** and **LOW** report a shift toward "smaller projects" and "persistent DIY macro pressures," while **LEN** notes a 9% year-over-year decrease in new orders.

**Labor Markets & Wages**
Labor costs are experiencing moderate upward pressure, particularly in service-oriented sectors. **CMG** and **DRI** both cite "wage inflation" and higher compensation expenses as drivers of increased labor costs as a percentage of revenue. In contrast, **ABNB** is actively reducing reliance on human labor, noting that over 40% of customer support issues are now resolved via AI without a human agent.

**Price & Cost Pressures**
Pricing power is moderate but uneven, with persistent commodity inflation weighing on margins. **CCL** and **F** report significant unfavorable impacts from fuel and aluminum costs, respectively. Some firms are finding relief through regulatory offsets, such as **BBY**, **LOW**, and **LULU**, which all cited "tariff refunds" as a boost to gross margins.

**Inventory & Logistics**
Inventory levels are generally stable, with a strategic shift toward lean operations in durables. **LEN** and **DHI** have aggressively reduced unsold completed homes to maintain efficiency. In retail, **AMZN** is expanding ultra-fast delivery to tens of millions of customers, while **HAS** experienced temporary shipping and invoicing delays due to a cybersecurity event.
