## Sector Consensus

Demand across the Communication Services sector is bifurcated, with robust activity in next-generation digital services and a moderate to slight softening in legacy offerings. A strong consensus exists regarding the acceleration of AI-driven enterprise solutions and high-speed wireless connectivity, as seen with Alphabet, Meta, T-Mobile, AT&T, and Comcast. Signals diverge primarily by product line: while wireless and AI subscriptions are seeing robust growth, traditional video and copper-based connectivity services are experiencing a moderate decline.
