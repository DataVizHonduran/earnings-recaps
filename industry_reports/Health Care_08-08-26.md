## Sector Consensus

Demand across the health care sector is generally **robust**, though activity is highly bifurcated by product lifecycle and geography. A majority of firms, including **Eli Lilly**, **Johnson & Johnson**, **Intuitive Surgical**, and **Incyte**, report strong volume growth driven by innovation and new product launches. **Dexcom** and **Labcorp** similarly note robust demand, particularly in international markets. However, this consensus is tempered by a common trend of softening demand in legacy portfolios; for example, **AbbVie** and **Bristol Myers Squibb** report robust growth in new "growth portfolios" that is partially offset by moderate to sharp declines in mature, legacy product lines facing generic competition. Demand is robust for high-innovation segments, such as **Intuitive Surgical's** da Vinci 5 systems and **Eli Lilly's** Zepbound and Mounjaro. Conversely, demand is softening in respiratory testing for **Abbott Laboratories** due to a weaker virus season and in legacy multiple sclerosis products for **Biogen**. **Quest Diagnostics** reports robust growth in the consumer channel, specifically for Alzheimer’s and cardiometabolic testing.
