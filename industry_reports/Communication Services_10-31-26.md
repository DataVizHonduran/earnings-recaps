## Sector Consensus

Demand across the Communication Services sector is bifurcated, with robust activity in AI-driven services, wireless connectivity, and digital entertainment, contrasted by a moderate to slight softening in legacy broadband and linear media. Firms such as Alphabet, Meta, T-Mobile, and AT&T report robust growth in AI infrastructure and advanced connectivity. Conversely, Charter and Comcast report softening demand for residential broadband and video services. Within this consensus, signals diverge by product line: while wireless demand is robust across the board, residential "core connectivity" is weakening. Geographically, demand is mixed; Disney and Live Nation report robust international activity, though Disney notes specific softening in Asian parks.
