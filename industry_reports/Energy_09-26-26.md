## Sector Consensus

Activity across the energy sector is generally robust, characterized by strong production growth in the U.S. Permian Basin and expanding global energy infrastructure. Firms such as **ExxonMobil**, **Chevron**, **Diamondback Energy**, **Devon Energy**, and **EOG Resources** report production levels meeting or exceeding guidance, with a particular emphasis on "advantaged" long-lived assets. Conversely, midstream and infrastructure firms like **Kinder Morgan**, **Williams Companies**, and **Targa Resources** report robust growth in natural gas transport and gathering, driven by rising power demand from data centers and LNG export capacity.

This is attributed to a shift in consumer demand and temporary supply disruptions on the West Coast.
