## Key Thematic Headings

**Consumer/Business Demand**
Demand for premium consumer products and wealth services is **robust**, with **American Express** reporting the highest quarterly spending growth in three years and **Ameriprise Financial** seeing a 20% increase in retirement protection sales. Business activity is expanding in commercial lending, as **Bank of America** and **Fifth Third Bancorp** report growth across corporate and business banking loans. Conversely, investment banking activity is **softening**, with **Bank of America** reporting an 11% decline in fees and **Goldman Sachs** noting a slight decrease in its banking backlog.

**Labor Markets & Wages**

Some firms are expanding their workforce strategically, such as **Citizens Financial Group** hiring for its Private Bank buildout and **Ameriprise Financial** adding experienced advisors. However, others are prioritizing efficiency through headcount controls, evidenced by **Arthur J. Funding costs are a key pressure for banks, with **Fifth Third Bancorp** noting that "competition for deposits is heating up."
