## Sector Consensus

The utilities sector is experiencing robust demand growth, primarily driven by the rapid expansion of hyperscale data centers and industrial scaling. This trend is most pronounced in Texas and the Southeast, with firms such as American Electric Power (AEP), CenterPoint Energy (CNP), and NextEra Energy (NEE) reporting substantial new load commitments. Capital spending remains robust across the sector, with a consensus focus on grid modernization, resilience, and the integration of renewable energy, as evidenced by the aggressive investment plans of Exelon (EXC), FirstEnergy (FE), and Sempra (SRE). Pricing power is generally moderate and dependent on regulatory channels; most firms, including Ameren (AEE) and Eversource (ES), are successfully implementing rate increases to recover infrastructure costs. However, signals diverge by product line: while electric demand is accelerating, natural gas demand is stable to slightly softening, as noted by WEC Energy Group (WEC) and Xcel Energy (XEL).
