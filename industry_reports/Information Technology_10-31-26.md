## Sector Consensus

Demand across the Information Technology sector is robust, primarily catalyzed by the rapid global adoption of AI infrastructure and "agentic" AI services. This momentum is broad-based, with firms such as **Broadcom**, **AMD**, **Arista Networks**, **Applied Materials**, and **Cisco Systems** reporting record revenues or significant growth in AI-driven networking and semiconductor segments. Activity is particularly strong in the Data Center and Enterprise Networking end-markets. However, signals diverge within this consensus based on product line: while AI-centric hardware and software are surging, legacy segments are softening. For example, **Akamai** reports declining revenue in legacy delivery services, and **AMD** notes a significant softening in its Gaming business. Geographically, growth is generally robust, though **Accenture** reports a more modest pace in the Americas compared to Asia Pacific.

## Key Thematic Headings

**Consumer/Business Demand**
Business demand is robust for AI-enabled infrastructure, with **Broadcom** reporting a 221% year-over-year increase in AI semiconductor revenue. **Cisco Systems** is experiencing a "networking supercycle" with campus networking orders growing over 25%. Conversely, consumer demand is mixed, as **HP Inc.** reports a 19% decline in Consumer Personal Systems units. **Adobe** is seeing robust growth in its Business Professionals and Consumers segment, which grew 16%.

**Labor Markets & Wages**
Labor signals are generally stable, though some firms are prioritizing structural efficiency over expansion. **CDW** reports moderate upward pressure on labor costs, citing higher compensation and performance-based incentives. **Cognizant** is implementing "Project Leap" to properly size its workforce, anticipating up to $270 million in severance costs. **Cisco Systems** reports robust productivity gains, achieving its highest revenue-per-employee metrics in 30 years.

**Price & Cost Pressures**
Pricing power is robust for premium AI offerings, with **Salesforce** seeing bookings for premium AI SKUs more than double quarter-over-quarter. **Autodesk** is actively improving price realization by reducing multi-year discounts. However, some firms face input cost headwinds; **HP Inc.** explicitly cites the challenge of navigating rising commodity costs. **Cisco Systems** has integrated the estimated impact of tariffs into its future earnings guidance.

**Inventory & Logistics**
Inventory levels are increasing moderately as firms buffer for AI demand. **Apple** saw inventories rise to $11.09 billion from $5.72 billion year-over-year, and **Arista Networks** increased inventories to $2.54 billion. **Applied Materials** has intentionally increased its build plan and logistics capacity to ensure supply chain readiness. **AMD** notes that supply remains a primary constraint for its Data Center segment, tying growth to the ability to scale supply.
