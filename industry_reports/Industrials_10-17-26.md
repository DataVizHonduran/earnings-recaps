A dominant theme is the exceptional surge in demand for data center infrastructure, which is driving growth for companies such as **GE Vernova**, **Eaton**, **Caterpillar**, **Carrier**, and **Generac**. This "AI-driven" infrastructure build-out is providing a significant tailwind that offsets softness in other areas. However, signals diverge sharply by end-market: while commercial and industrial segments are expanding, residential demand is **softening** or flat, as seen in the residential lines of **Carrier**, **A.O. *   **FedEx (Transportation):** While domestic package volume is growing, the LTL (Less-than-Truckload) sector is **softening**, reflecting a divergence in freight demand compared to the robust activity in heavy machinery and power equipment.
