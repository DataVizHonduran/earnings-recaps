Conversely, consumer-facing residential demand is softening, as **Builders FirstSource** cites a "weak housing market" and **A.O. Smith** reports residential water heater volumes below expectations. High-end corporate travel is recovering robustly, with **Delta Air Lines** noting double-digit growth in banking and aerospace sectors.

**Inventory & Logistics**
