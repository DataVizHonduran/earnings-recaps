## Sector Consensus

The Utilities sector is experiencing robust long-term demand growth, primarily catalyzed by the rapid expansion of data centers, hyperscalers, and large-scale industrial loads. This trend is most pronounced in Texas and the Southeast, as reported by **American Electric Power (AEP)**, **Sempra (SRE)**, and **CenterPoint Energy (CNP)**. While long-term signals are strong, short-term retail activity remains sensitive to weather anomalies, with **Ameren Corp (AEE)** and **NRG Energy** noting softening in retail load due to warmer-than-normal winter temperatures. Capital expenditure across the sector is robust and expanding, with firms like **Exelon Corp (EXC)** and **FirstEnergy Corp (FE)** executing multi-billion dollar modernization plans to enhance grid resilience and capacity. Pricing power is generally moderate to stable, as firms such as **Eversource Energy (ES)** and **American Water Works (AWK)** leverage regulatory channels and rate cases to recover infrastructure costs. However, signals diverge regarding the impact of these increases; while some firms focus on cost recovery, others, such as **PG&E Corp (PCG)**, are actively reducing rates for vulnerable customer segments.
