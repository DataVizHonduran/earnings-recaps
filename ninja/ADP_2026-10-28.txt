 Document ADP Reports First Quarter Fiscal 2027 Results Revenues increased 6% compared to last year's first quarter to $5.5 billion 6% organic constant currency Net earnings increased 11% to $1.1 billion, and adjusted net earnings increased 11% to $1.1 billion Adjusted EBIT increased 12% to $1.5 billion, and adjusted EBIT margin increased 130 basis points to 26.8% Diluted earnings per share ( EPS ) increased 14% to $2.83 adjusted diluted EPS increased 13% to $2.82 Raising full year guidance for revenue and adjusted diluted EPS growth ROSELAND, N.J. October 28, 2026 ADP (Nasdaq ADP), a global leader in HR and payroll solutions, today announced its first quarter fiscal 2027 financial results and updated its fiscal 2027 outlook. First Quarter Fiscal 2027 Consolidated Results Compared to last year s first quarter, revenues increased 6% to $5.5 billion and 6% on an organic constant currency basis. Net earnings increased 11% to $1.1 billion, and adjusted net earnings increased 11% to $1.1 billion. Adjusted EBIT increased 12% to $1.5 billion, and adjusted EBIT margin increased 130 basis points to 26.8%. ADP s effective tax rate for the quarter was 22.7% on both a reported basis and an adjusted basis. Diluted EPS increased 14% to $2.83, and adjusted diluted EPS increased 13% to $2.82. ADP is off to a strong start in fiscal 2027 with first quarter results that reflect the resilience of our business model and the trust our clients continue to place in us, said Maria Black, President and Chief Executive Officer, ADP. The complexity of work is growing faster than employers can manage alone and our clients are turning to us and our deep domain expertise to fundamentally change how work gets done inside their organizations. We enter the rest of fiscal 2027 well positioned to build on our current momentum. Our first quarter results exceeded our expectations across revenue growth, adjusted EBIT margin expansion, and adjusted EPS growth, said Peter Hadley, Chief Financial Officer, ADP. Our ongoing commitment to a disciplined investment approach is enabling us to drive our AI transformation forward, and at the same time deliver strong financial results. 1 Adjusted EBIT, adjusted EBIT margin, adjusted net earnings, adjusted diluted earnings per share, adjusted effective tax rate and organic constant currency are all non-GAAP financial measures.
