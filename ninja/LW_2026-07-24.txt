Significant productivity gains generated cost savings to offset inflation and unexpected costs, as evidenced by our increased North America segment Q4 adjusted EBITDA margin. I am excited about the strategic work underway, led by Jan Craps, our executive chair, to focus our resources across our geographic footprint and create sustainable value for shareholders, concluded Smith.
