Q3 2026 Revenue Results We had record revenue of $1,202 million in the third quarter, 22.6% higher than the second quarter of 2026 and 63.1% higher than revenue in the third quarter of 2025. Compared with the second quarter of 2026, sales improved sequentially across Communications, Enterprise Data, Industrial, Automotive and Consumer end markets. In our Communications end market, third quarter revenue of $187.4 million was up 42.5% from the second quarter primarily as a result of higher sales of power solutions for optical modules and switches. Third quarter Communications revenue was up 134.7% year over year. Communications sales represented 15.6% of our total third quarter revenue compared with 13.4% in the second quarter of 2026. Third quarter Enterprise Data revenue of $506.7 million increased 33.2% from the second quarter. The sequential increase was driven by higher sales of our power management solutions for AI and server applications. Third quarter Enterprise Data revenue was up 164.6% year over year. Enterprise Data revenue represented 42.1% of our total third quarter revenue compared with 38.8% in the second quarter of 2026. Third quarter Industrial revenue of $71.4 million increased 30.2% from the second quarter primarily as a result of higher sales for power sources. Third quarter Industrial revenue was up 29.0% year over year. Industrial revenue represented 6.0% of our total third quarter revenue compared with 5.6% in the second quarter of 2026. Third quarter Automotive revenue of $180.7 million increased 15.0% from the second quarter primarily from higher sales of ADAS power solutions. Third quarter Automotive revenue was up 19.2% year over year. Automotive revenue represented 15.0% of our third quarter revenue compared with 16.0% in the second quarter of 2026. Third quarter Consumer revenue of $64.0 million increased 12.6% from the second quarter. Third quarter Consumer revenue was down 11.6% year over year. Consumer revenue represented 5.3% of our total third quarter revenue compared with 5.8% in the second quarter of 2026. Third quarter Storage and Computing revenue of $192.2 million decreased 3.8% from the second quarter. Third quarter Storage and Computing revenue was up 3.0% year over year. Storage and Computing revenue represented 16.0% of MPS s third quarter revenue compared with 20.4% in the second quarter of 2026. About Monolithic Power Systems MPS is a fabless global company that provides high-performance, semiconductor-based power electronics solutions. MPS s mission is to reduce energy and material consumption to improve all aspects of quality of life and create a sustainable future. Founded in 1997 by our CEO Michael Hsing, MPS has three core strengths deep system-level knowledge, strong semiconductor design expertise, and innovative proprietary technologies in the areas of semiconductor processes, system integration, and packaging. These combined advantages enable MPS to deliver reliable, compact, and monolithic solutions that are highly energy-efficient, cost-effective, and environmentally responsible, while providing a consistent return on investment to our stockholders.
