 Document Exhibit 99.1 FOR IMMEDIATE RELEASE October 28, 2026 FOR ADDITIONAL INFORMATION Media Investors media nisource.com investors nisource.com NiSource announces third quarter results Narrowing 2026 non-GAAP consolidated adjusted EPS guidance to upper half of $2.02-$2.07 range Introducing 2027 non-GAAP consolidated adjusted EPS guidance Extending base plan expected non-GAAP adjusted EPS annual growth guidance of 6%-8% to 2027-2033 Raising consolidated expected non-GAAP adjusted EPS compound annual growth rate to 10%-11% for 2027-2033 Expanding GenCo cost savings for existing customers to approximately $1.75 billion through new agreement with Microsoft MERRILLVILLE, Ind . - NiSource Inc. (NYSE NI) today announced, on a GAAP basis, net income available to common shareholders for the quarter ended September 30, 2026 of $129.5 million, or $0.27 of earnings per diluted share, compared to net income available to common shareholders of $94.7 million, or $0.20 of earnings per diluted share, for the same period of 2025. For the nine months ended September 30, 2026, on a GAAP basis, NiSource's net income available to common shareholders was $685.7 million, or $1.42 of diluted earnings per share, compared to net income available to common shareholders of $671.7 million, or $1.42 of diluted earnings per share, for the same period of 2025. NiSource also reported third quarter 2026 non-GAAP adjusted net income available to common shareholders of $130.9 million, or $0.27 of consolidated adjusted EPS, compared to non-GAAP adjusted net income available to common shareholders of $91.8 million, or $0.19 of consolidated adjusted EPS, for the same period of 2025. For the nine months ended September 30, 2026, NiSource's non-GAAP adjusted net income available to common shareholders was $718.1 million, or $1.49 of consolidated adjusted EPS, compared to non-GAAP adjusted net income available to common shareholders of $656.0 million, or $1.38 of consolidated adjusted EPS, for the same period of 2025. Schedule 1 of this press release contains a complete reconciliation of GAAP measures to non-GAAP measures. ** NiSource is narrowing its 2026 non-GAAP consolidated adjusted EPS guidance to the upper half of $2.02-$2.07. In 2027, non-GAAP consolidated adjusted EPS is expected to be in the range of $2.22-$2.30. NiSource is extending its capital plan through 2033. The new consolidated seven-year total capital expenditures of $40.0 billion are expected to support 9%-10% consolidated investment base growth from 2027-2033 and an expected consolidated non-GAAP adjusted EPS compound annual growth rate (CAGR) of 10%-11% for 2027-2033. The company s base plan non-GAAP adjusted EPS growth is expected to be 6%-8% annually with 8%-10% rate base growth expected from 2027-2033. 1 The consolidated capital expenditure plan is driven by $32.0 billion in base plan capital expenditures and $8.0 billion for data center investments for 2027-2033. The extension of our long-term plan reflects the significant opportunities ahead for NiSource and our confidence in our ability to deliver, said Lloyd Yates, President and Chief Executive Officer of NiSource. Our seven-year capital plan supports critical infrastructure investments to enhance the safety, reliability, and resiliency of our systems while enabling significant economic development across our footprint. This year's extreme weather events have reinforced the importance of those investments, while opportunities such as Microsoft and our GenCo pipeline further strengthen the outlook. Our signed GenCo agreements are expected to deliver approximately $1.75 billion in customer savings, positioning us to deliver industry-leading growth and long-term value for customers, communities, and shareholders. I also want to thank our employees for their dedication to serving customers and restoring service during severe weather events. Their commitment, along with the support of our regulators, policymakers, customers, investors, and community partners, positions us well to execute this plan and deliver sustainable value for all stakeholders. 2 **Non-GAAP Disclosure Statement This press release includes financial results and guidance for NiSource with respect to adjusted net income available to common shareholders, base plan adjusted EPS and consolidated adjusted EPS, which are non-GAAP financial measures as defined by the SEC. Commencing in 2026, the company began to present base plan adjusted EPS and consolidated adjusted EPS. As presented, guidance with respect to base plan adjusted EPS, including annual base plan adjusted EPS growth, excludes, in addition to the items historically excluded from adjusted EPS, the impact of data center operations and development activities relating to provision of electric service to current and future data center or other large load customers. The company provides guidance regarding base plan adjusted EPS because it expects that the earnings from its data center operations and development activities will experience a different growth profile compared to the base plan adjusted EPS growth. Providing guidance with respect to base plan adjusted EPS growth, together with guidance regarding consolidated adjusted EPS growth, provides investors with the same information that management considers to evaluate the company s ongoing business performance and provide greater transparency into the performance of different aspects of our business that are impacted by distinct trends and factors. Consolidated adjusted EPS represents base plan adjusted EPS together with adjusted EPS from our data center operations and development activities. The company includes these measures because management believes they permit investors to view the company s performance using the same tools that management uses and to better evaluate the company s ongoing business performance. With respect to guidance on base plan adjusted EPS and consolidated adjusted EPS, NiSource reminds investors that it does not provide a GAAP equivalent of its guidance on base plan adjusted EPS or consolidated adjusted EPS due to the impact of unpredictable factors such as fluctuations in weather, impact of asset sales and impairments and other unusual or infrequent items included in the comparable GAAP measures, which may be material. The company is not able to estimate the impact of such factors on the comparable GAAP measures and, as such, the company is not able to provide a reconciliation of its non-GAAP base plan adjusted EPS guidance or its non-GAAP consolidated adjusted EPS guidance to the comparable GAAP equivalents without unreasonable efforts.
