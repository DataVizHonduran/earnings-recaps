Eastman delivered solid results amid ongoing global macroeconomic uncertainty and escalation of the Middle East conflict, said Mark Costa, Board Chair and CEO. Input costs continued to elevate, and the Eastman team successfully implemented higher sequential price increases in our specialties. We remain on track to implement approximately $500 million of selling price increases in 2026 compared to our expectations from before the Middle East conflict began. In addition, sequential sales volume mix decreased due to seasonal declines across our key end markets and softening underlying demand in automotive and consumer durables. We also remain confident in the circular platform and are positioned for solid revenue growth this year. In this context, we remained focused on our disciplined approach toward cost savings, and we remain on track to reduce costs by $125 million to $150 million, net of inflation in 2026. We also continue to emphasize the importance of operating cash flow in this dynamic environment. Corporate Results 3Q 2026 versus 3Q 2025 Sales revenue increased 8 percent due to 7 percent higher selling prices and 1 percent higher sales volume mix. Higher selling prices across the company were primarily driven by higher raw material and distribution costs as well as tight market conditions in Chemical Intermediates due to the ongoing conflict in the Middle East. EBIT increased primarily due to improved asset utilization after actions to reduce inventory in third quarter 2025, including lower planned maintenance expense and favorable price-cost. These factors were partially offset by higher variable compensation expense. Segment Results 3Q 2026 versus 3Q 2025 Advanced Materials Sales revenue increased 5 percent due to 4 percent higher selling prices and 1 percent favorable foreign currency exchange rates. Higher selling prices in specialty plastics were due to higher raw material and distribution costs. Sales volume mix was unchanged as increases in specialty plastics and advanced interlayers were offset by lower sales volume mix in performance films. EBIT increased due to substantially improved asset utilization and lower planned maintenance costs. Additives Functional Products Sales revenue increased 9 percent due to 10 percent higher selling prices partially offset by 1 percent lower sales volume mix. Higher selling prices were due to cost-pass-through contracts adjusting to higher raw material and distribution costs. EBIT increased due to improvement in both price-cost and asset utilization. Fibers Sales revenue was unchanged as 4 percent higher sales volume mix was offset by 4 percent lower selling prices. Acetate tow volume was similar to last year. Higher sales volume mix was due to increased sales of textiles and acetyl chemical products. Lower selling prices were due to lower acetate tow contract pricing. EBIT decreased due to lower acetate tow contract pricing and increased raw material and distribution costs. Chemical Intermediates Sales revenue increased by 16 percent due to 12 percent higher selling prices and 4 percent higher sales volume mix. Higher selling prices and higher sales volume mix, particularly for olefin and derivative products, were driven by increased volume availability and tight market conditions due to the ongoing conflict in the Middle East. EBIT increased primarily due to higher spreads. Cash Flow In the third quarter of 2026, cash provided by operating activities was $290 million versus $402 million in the third quarter of 2025. The company returned $96 million to stockholders through dividends. See Table 5. Priorities for uses of available cash for 2026 include payment of the quarterly dividend, capital expenditures, and maintaining our solid investment-grade balance sheet. 2026 Outlook Commenting on the outlook for fourth-quarter and full-year 2026, Costa said We expect to deliver a solid full-year performance in both earnings and cash flow, despite a challenging macroeconomic environment and the escalation of the Middle East conflict. In particular, the Middle East conflict has led to higher raw material and distribution costs and increasing interest rates. We expect sequential demand declines with normal seasonality and some additional customer caution somewhat offset by innovation-driven growth. In this context, we are highly focused on delivering growth in our circular platform and driving innovation across our specialty businesses. We also expect an increase in acetate tow sales volume in Fibers, as customers meet annual contract commitments. Given the acceleration of raw material and distribution costs through this quarter, we are taking additional price actions across the company. We expect to have higher planned shutdown costs in the fourth quarter, which will be partially offset by a continuation of cost actions through the year. When putting these factors together, we project adjusted earnings per share for full-year 2026 to be between $6.00 and $6.20 and operating cash flow to be approximately $850 million. These results demonstrate the value of our innovation-driven growth model and our ability to deliver solid earnings growth in an uncertain macroeconomic environment. The fourth-quarter 2026 projected adjusted diluted EPS excludes any non-core, unusual, or non-recurring items. Our financial results forecasts do not include non-core items (such as mark-to-market pension and other post-retirement benefit gain or loss, and asset impairments and restructuring charges) or any unusual or non-recurring items because we are unable to predict with reasonable certainty the financial impact of such items. These items are uncertain and depend on various factors, and we are unable to reconcile projected adjusted diluted EPS excluding non-core and any unusual or non-recurring items to reported GAAP diluted EPS without unreasonable efforts. Financial Measures (Non-GAAP) In addition to the financial information presented in accordance with Generally Accepted Accounting Principles ( GAAP ), this press release includes the following non-GAAP financial measures adjusted EBIT, adjusted EBIT margin, and adjusted earnings per diluted share. We define adjusted EBIT as the GAAP measure EBIT adjusted for non-core, unusual, or non-recurring items. Adjusted earnings per diluted share is defined as the GAAP measure earnings per diluted share adjusted for non-core, unusual, or non-recurring items. Adjusted EBIT margin is defined as adjusted EBIT divided by the GAAP measure sales revenue in the Company's Unaudited Consolidated Statement of Earnings, Comprehensive Income and Retained Earnings for the same periods. See the reconciliation tables presented in this release for a detailed reconciliation of non-GAAP financial measures to the most directly comparable GAAP measure. We believe that in addition to our results determined in accordance with GAAP, these non-GAAP financial measures provide useful information to both management and investors in measuring our financial performance and highlight trends in our business that may not otherwise be apparent when relying solely on GAAP measures. These non-GAAP financial measures provide supplemental information regarding our operating performance that excludes certain gains, losses and non-cash charges that occur relatively infrequently and or that we consider to be unrelated to our core operations. Non-GAAP financial information is presented for supplemental informational purposes only and should not be considered in isolation or as a substitute for financial information presented in accordance with GAAP. Our presentation of non-GAAP measures should not be construed as an inference that our future results will be unaffected by unusual or non-recurring items. Other companies in our industry may calculate these measures differently, which may limit their usefulness as comparative measures. Founded in 1920, Eastman is a global specialty materials company that produces a broad range of products found in items people use every day. With the purpose of enhancing the quality of life in a material way, Eastman works with customers to deliver innovative products and solutions while maintaining a commitment to safety and sustainability. The company s innovation-driven growth model takes advantage of world-class technology platforms, deep customer engagement, and differentiated application development to grow its leading positions in attractive end markets such as transportation, building and construction, and consumables. As a globally inclusive company, Eastman employs approximately 13,000 people around the world and serves customers in more than 100 countries. The company had 2025 revenue of approximately $8.8 billion and is headquartered in Kingsport, Tennessee, USA.
