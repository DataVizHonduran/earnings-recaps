 Document GE AEROSPACE ANNOUNCES THIRD QUARTER 2026 RESULTS Year-to-date orders, revenue and total engine deliveries up over 20%, raising full-year guidance Third Quarter 2026 Total orders of $19.0B, +49% Total revenue (GAAP) of $14.1B, +16% adjusted revenue* $13.3B, +18% Profit (GAAP) of $2.8B, +12% operating profit* $3.0B, +30% Profit Margin (GAAP) of 19.9%, (80) bps operating profit margin* 22.5%, +220 bps Continuing EPS (GAAP) of $2.32, +14% adjusted EPS* $2.25, +36% Cash from operating activities (GAAP) of $2.2B, (14)% free cash flow* $2.0B, (14)% CINCINNATI — Oct 20, 2026 — GE Aerospace (NYSE GE) announced results today for the third quarter ending September 30, 2026. GE Aerospace Chairman and CEO H. Lawrence Culp, Jr., said, We delivered strong results this quarter as solid demand drove orders up 49%, with strength in commercial services up 23%, total revenue up 18% and EPS up 36%. We are raising our 2026 guidance across the board, and we now expect 2027 commercial services to grow low-double-digits supported by our over $170 billion commercial services backlog. Culp continued, Thanks to the collaboration of the GE Aerospace team with our supplier partners we continue to build momentum. By leveraging FLIGHT DECK fundamentals, year-to-date commercial services revenue and total engine deliveries were both up over 20%, including another quarter of record internal shop visit output. All the while, we're continuing to invest in our customers' needs with our agreement to acquire CPP to expand capacity, and over $3 billion in annual R D to advance current and future technologies. Recent highlights include Increased material input from priority suppliers double-digits year-over-year, contributing to strong year-to-date Commercial Engines Services (CES) services revenue and total engine deliveries, including LEAP growing 26%. Announced commitments for more than 1,800 engines at a historic Farnborough International Airshow , including the largest LEAP agreement ever for more than 1,000 LEAP-1A engines with IndiGo and in October, signed an agreement with Turkish Airlines for more than 300 LEAP-1B engines along with a long-term services agreement covering its entire LEAP-1B fleet of over 600 engines. In Defense, signed a contract worth up to $2.9B to provide ongoing support for the F414's powering the U.S. Navy's F A-18 and EA-18G fleets. Signed an agreement to acquire Consolidated Precision Products (CPP) to expand mission-critical castings capacity for the aerospace industry to meet demand across commercial engines, aftermarket and defense. This acquisition would also accelerate the introduction of new engine technologies to our current fleet and next-generation platforms. Completed the world's first high-altitude hybrid electric flight above 30,000 feet in partnership with the National Aeronautics and Space Administration (NASA), BETA Technologies, and Boeing, demonstrating the viability of more electric aircraft engine systems for greater efficiency, durability, and range. Advanced key defense programs, including the GEK800 (F143) engine with Kratos being designated as a second-source propulsion solution for the Joint Air-to-Surface Standoff Missile (JASSM) and continued progress on the XA102 adaptive-cycle engine as the U.S. Air Force s Next Generation Adaptive Propulsion (NGAP) program moves into hardware procurement and subcomponent testing. *Non-GAAP
