PAUL, Minn., October 27, 2026 CEO Comment Christophe Beck, Ecolab s chairman, president and chief executive officer, said, Third quarter results marked an inflection point in Ecolab's performance. Exceptional execution drove accelerating sales growth, margin expansion and double-digit adjusted operating income growth. Volume growth accelerated to 2% despite ongoing disruptions in the Middle East. As expected, pricing strengthened to 5% as we leveraged total value delivered to mitigate higher commodity costs while improving customer performance. This disciplined execution allowed us to stabilize our organic gross margin. The breadth of our performance makes these results even more encouraging. Growth across our core businesses strengthened, led by accelerating growth in Food Beverage and Light Water, alongside consistent growth in Institutional Specialty despite softer market trends. Performance in Paper and Heavy Water also improved, with both businesses returning to solid growth. Across these core businesses, our One Ecolab growth initiative is helping deliver greater customer value and share gains for Ecolab. Our growth engines continued to rapidly accelerate, with very strong performance across all businesses. Global High-Tech, our largest growth engine, significantly accelerated, growing 63% organically. Our recent acquisitions in this business, Ovivo Electronics and CoolIT, are also performing exceptionally well. This year, we expect Ovivo Electronics to grow mid-teens and CoolIT to more than double, compared with pre-acquisition sales. Together, these acquisitions are expected to add two percentage points to Ecolab's annual organic sales growth one year after close with increasing margins. Our differentiated water circularity solutions for microelectronics and next-gen liquid cooling technologies for AI data centers position Ecolab extremely well to capitalize on significant long-term growth opportunities. We are entering 2027 with momentum and confidence in our ability to deliver another very strong year. We expect organic sales to grow at the upper end of our 5% to 7% target and Ecolab's adjusted operating income margin to reach 20%. Adjusted EPS growth is expected to accelerate in the second half of the year as we lap M A transaction impacts and operating performance continues to accelerate. This provides a clear path to meet or exceed our long-term adjusted EPS growth target of 12% to 15% in 2027. About Ecolab A trusted partner for millions of customers, Ecolab (NYSE ECL) is a global leader in water, hygiene and infection prevention solutions and services that protect people and the resources vital to life. For more than a century, Ecolab has advanced innovation by integrating science-based solutions, data-driven insights, AI technology and world-class service. This unique combination enables Ecolab to partner with customers to define what best-in-class looks like and scale it across their operations, helping them achieve peak performance. Today, Ecolab delivers $16 billion in annual sales, employs 48,000 associates and serves customers in more than 170 countries and 40 industries. The company helps protect one-third of the world s food production and a quarter of the power generated while delivering innovative solutions across food, hospitality, healthcare, data centers, microelectronics and life sciences. As the world s water company, Ecolab plays an important role in AI growth by supporting the full water needs of advanced computing—from ultra pure water for chip manufacturing, to water solutions that support the power behind AI, to direct liquid cooling systems for high density computing that improves performance while reducing environmental impact through circular water use. In life sciences, Ecolab delivers end to end solutions that support the development and manufacturing of life-saving drugs, helping customers operate safely and consistently at scale while improving performance and reducing environmental impact. Through its comprehensive approach, Ecolab protects what s vital, with a goal by 2030 to 8 help protect 2 billion people from infections and conserve enough drinking water for 1 billion people, while continuing to enhance business performance.
