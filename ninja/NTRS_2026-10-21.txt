Net income was $642.9 million, compared to $792.2 million in the prior quarter and $457.6 million in the prior-year quarter. Third Quarter 2026 results included the following notable items $48.7 million (pre-tax) impact to Other Noninterest Income from notable items (after-tax $36.8 million) $34.0 million (pre-tax) gain on the sale of the guardianship services business, recognized in Other Operating Income (after-tax $25.7 million). $14.7 million (pre-tax) incremental gain related to Northern Trust s participation in the second Visa Exchange Offer, recognized in Other Operating Income (after-tax $11.1 million). MICHAEL O GRADY, CHAIRMAN AND CHIEF EXECUTIVE OFFICER Northern Trust delivered strong third-quarter results, demonstrating continued progress against our One Northern Trust strategy. Compared to the prior year and excluding notable items, revenues grew 11%, while disciplined expense management generated more than 600 basis points of positive operating leverage. As a result, pretax income increased 21% and earnings per share increased 40%. All three businesses delivered organic trust-fee growth, reflecting strong execution across the company. Market conditions continued to evolve but remained constructive overall. Our diversified business model positions us to perform across a range of environments. Our strong capital position enabled us to return more than $1.5 billion to common shareholders year to date. As we enter the final quarter of the year, we remain focused on supporting our clients, investing in growth and delivering sustainable long-term value to shareholders. About Northern Trust Northern Trust Corporation (Nasdaq NTRS) is a leading provider of wealth management, asset servicing, asset management and banking to corporations, institutions, affluent families and individuals. Founded in Chicago in 1889, Northern Trust has a global presence with offices in 24 U.S. states and Washington, D.C., and across 22 locations in Canada, Europe, the Middle East and the Asia-Pacific region. As of September 30, 2026, Northern Trust had assets under custody administration of US$19.9 trillion, and assets under management of US$2.0 trillion.
