 Nucor Reports Results for the Third Quarter of 2026 
 Third Quarter of 2026 Highlights 
 
 
 
 
 
 Net earnings attributable to Nucor stockholders of $1.32 billion, or $5.80 per diluted share
 
 
 
 
 
 
 Net sales of $11.25 billion 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 Net earnings before noncontrolling interests of $1.47 billion; EBITDA of $2.28 billion
 CHARLOTTE, N.C. October 26, 2026 - Nucor Corporation (NYSE: NUE) today announced consolidated net earnings attributable to
Nucor stockholders of $1.32 billion, or $5.80 per diluted share, for the third quarter of 2026. By comparison, Nucor reported consolidated net earnings attributable to Nucor stockholders of $1.16 billion, or $5.04 per diluted share, and
adjusted net earnings attributable to Nucor stockholders of $1.11 billion, or $4.84 per diluted share, for the second quarter of 2026. Nucor reported consolidated net earnings attributable to Nucor stockholders of $607 million, or $2.63
per diluted share, for the third quarter of 2025. Second quarter of 2026 results included a non-cash, pre-tax benefit of $61 million ($0.20 per diluted share) related to the increase in the valuation of our investment in Helion, a fusion energy company. 
 I want to thank our teammates for delivering another strong quarter and making 2026 the safest summer in Nucor history, said Leon Topalian,
Nucor s Chair and Chief Executive Officer. Supported by robust demand across key end markets, Nucor has generated approximately $2.7 billion of free cash flow and returned nearly $1.4 billion to shareholders through the first
nine months of the year. We also achieved important milestones in advancing our growth strategy, including the first heat at our West Virginia sheet mill in September and the recent start-up of our second
galvanizing line at Nucor Steel Berkeley. These projects, together with other recent investments, are expanding Nucor s capabilities and strengthening our position in attractive markets. With our diversified portfolio, strong balance sheet and
disciplined approach to capital allocation, Nucor is well positioned to deliver long-term shareholder value. Earnings Before Income Taxes

 Analysis of Third Quarter of 2026 Results Compared to the Second Quarter of 2026 
 Third quarter earnings increased in the steel mills and steel products segments, while decreasing in the raw materials segment. The increase in the steel mills
segment was due to higher average selling prices and increased volumes, partially offset by higher costs of products sold. Second quarter steel mills segment cost of products sold was reduced by $130 million in cash refunds associated with
prior periods raw material procurement costs, which did not recur in the third quarter of 2026. The increase in earnings in the steel products segment was due to higher volumes and higher average realized pricing. Raw materials segment
earnings decreased due to lower pricing and shipments.

valuation adjustment. Financial Strength At
the end of the third quarter of 2026, Nucor had $3.64 billion in cash and cash equivalents and short-term investments on hand. The Company s $2.25 billion revolving credit facility remains undrawn and does not expire until March
2030. The Company continues to have the strongest credit ratings in the North American steel sector (A-/A-/A3) with stable outlooks at Standard & Poor s,
Fitch Ratings and Moody s, respectively. Commitment to Returning Capital to Stockholders 
 During the third quarter of 2026, Nucor repurchased approximately 2.03 million shares of its common stock at an average price of $247.04 per share. Nucor
returned approximately $629 million to stockholders in the third quarter of 2026 in the form of share repurchases and dividend payments, and approximately $1.36 billion in the first nine months of 2026. 
 On September 18, 2026, Nucor s Board of Directors declared a cash dividend of $0.56 per share. This cash dividend is payable on November 10,
2026, to stockholders of record as of September 30, 2026 and is Nucor s 214 th consecutive quarterly cash dividend. 
 Fourth Quarter of 2026 Outlook Compared to the Third Quarter of 2026 
 We expect earnings in the fourth quarter of 2026 to be generally in-line with or modestly higher than the third
quarter. In the steel mills segment, we expect stable earnings due to higher realized pricing offset by seasonally lower volumes and higher costs. In the steel products segment, we expect stable earnings on higher realized pricing and
seasonally lower volumes. The raw materials segment is expected to have slightly lower earnings due to lower realized DRI pricing. Intersegment profit eliminations are expected to decrease in the fourth quarter.

are manufacturers of steel and steel products, with operating facilities in the United States, Canada and Mexico. Products produced include: carbon and alloy steel — in bars, beams, sheet and plate; hollow structural section tubing; electrical
conduit; steel racking; steel piling; steel joists and joist girders; steel deck; fabricated concrete reinforcing steel; cold finished steel; precision castings; steel fasteners; metal building systems; insulated metal panels; overhead doors; steel
grating; wire and wire mesh; and utility structures. Nucor, through The David J. Joseph Company and its affiliates, also brokers ferrous and nonferrous metals, pig iron and hot briquetted iron / direct reduced iron; supplies ferro-alloys; and
processes ferrous and nonferrous scrap. Nucor is North America s largest recycler.

 Non-GAAP Financial Measures 
 The Company uses certain non-GAAP (Generally Accepted Accounting Principles) financial measures in this news
release, including EBITDA, adjusted net earnings attributable to Nucor stockholders, adjusted net earnings per diluted share and, beginning in the third quarter of 2026, free cash flow. Generally,
 a non-GAAP financial measure is a numerical measure of a company s performance or financial position that either excludes or includes amounts that are not normally excluded or included in the
most directly comparable financial measure calculated and presented in accordance with GAAP. We define EBITDA as net earnings before noncontrolling
interests, adding back the following items: interest expense (income), net; provision for income taxes; losses and impairments of assets; depreciation; and amortization. For the second quarter of 2026, we defined adjusted net earnings attributable
to Nucor stockholders as net earnings attributable to Nucor stockholders subtracting certain non-cash benefits (in this case, the increase in the value of our investment in Helion), net of tax. We define
adjusted net earnings per diluted share as net earnings per diluted share subtracting certain non-cash benefits (in this case, the per diluted share impact of the increase in the value of our investment in
Helion), net of tax. We define free cash flow as cash provided by operating activities less capital expenditures. Please note that other companies might define their non-GAAP financial measures
differently than we do. Management presents the non-GAAP financial measures of EBITDA, adjusted net
earnings attributable to Nucor stockholders, adjusted net earnings per diluted share and free cash flow in this news release because it considers them to be important supplemental measures of performance. Management believes that these non-GAAP financial measures provide additional insight for analysts and investors evaluating the Company s financial and operational performance by providing a consistent basis of comparison
