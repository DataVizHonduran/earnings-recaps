Rahway, N.J., USA (NYSE: MRK), known as MSD outside the United States and Canada, today announced financial results for the third quarter
of 2026. 

 

 &ldquo;Our science-led strategy is transforming
our business,&rdquo; said Robert M. Davis, chairman and chief executive officer. &ldquo;The potentially practice-changing advances in
oncology presented at last weekend&rsquo;s ESMO meeting, including new data for our antibody-drug conjugate sac-TMT and the first Phase
3 validation of a therapeutic cancer vaccine, further clinically de-risk the substantial long-term opportunity of our late-phase pipeline.
With positive momentum across multiple therapeutic areas, I&rsquo;m increasingly confident in our ability to deliver long-term value
for patients and shareholders.&rdquo;

 GAAP Expense, EPS and Related Information 

 

 Gross margin was 72.9% for the third quarter of
2026 compared with 77.7% for the third quarter of 2025. The decrease was primarily due to higher amortization of intangible assets, restructuring
costs and production costs. 

 

 Selling, general and administrative (SG&A)
expenses were $2.9 billion in the third quarter of 2026, an increase of 10% compared with the third quarter of 2025. The increase was
primarily due to higher administrative costs, as well as higher promotional costs in support of product launches. 

 

 Research and development (R&D) expenses were
$4.5 billion in the third quarter of 2026, an increase of 7% compared with the third quarter of 2025. The increase was primarily due
to a $400 million charge related to a global license agreement with SciBrunch and higher clinical development spending, partially offset
by lower restructuring costs and a $200 million reduction in R&D expenses as part of a funding agreement with Blackstone Life Sciences
(Blackstone). R&D expenses in the third quarter of 2025 include a $300 million charge for a milestone payment to LaNova for the completion
of the technology transfer for MK-2010. 

 

 Other (income) expense, net, was $27 million of
expense in the third quarter of 2026 compared with $238 million of income in the third quarter of 2025. The unfavorability was primarily
due to higher net interest expense.

 Pipeline and Portfolio Highlights 

 

 In the third quarter, the Company achieved key
regulatory and clinical milestones to meaningfully advance its broad and diverse pipeline. 

 

 
 &middot; Oncology: 

 

 
 o U.S. Food and Drug Administration (U.S. FDA) approved WELIREG plus
 Lenvima, in collaboration with Eisai, for certain previously treated adult patients with
 advanced renal cell carcinoma with a clear cell component (ccRCC), based on Phase 3 LITESPARK-011
 trial. 

 

 
 &sect; Approval
 represents first approved combination of an oral hypoxia-inducible factor-2 alpha inhibitor
 and a multi-targeted vascular endothelial growth factor tyrosine kinase inhibitor (TKI) for
 these patients. 

 

 
 o In October, U.S. FDA accepted a Biologics License Application (BLA)
 seeking approval of sac-TMT for the treatment of adult patients with endometrial cancer who
 have previously received platinum containing chemotherapy and a programmed death receptor
 1 or programmed death ligand 1 inhibitor, separately or in combination, based on Phase 3
 TroFuse-005 trial. 

 

 
 &sect; U.S.
 FDA set Prescription Drug User Fee Act (PDUFA) date of June 15, 2027. The Company is
 in discussions with the U.S. FDA and is hopeful for an earlier approval. 

 

 
 o In October, European Commission (EC) approved KEYTRUDA plus Padcev
 as perioperative treatment for adults with resectable muscle-invasive bladder cancer (MIBC),
 based on Phase 3 KEYNOTE-B15 trial.
