 Document Exhibit 99.1 AMETEK Announces Record Third Quarter 2026 Results and Raises Full Year Guidance Berwyn, Pa., Nov 3, 2026 AMETEK, Inc. (NYSE AME) today announced its financial results for the third quarter ended September 30, 2026. AMETEK s third quarter 2026 sales were a record $2.22 billion, a 17% increase over the third quarter of 2025. On a GAAP basis, third quarter earnings were a record $1.85 per diluted share. Adjusted earnings for the third quarter were a record $2.21 per diluted share, up 17% from the prior year period. Adjusted earnings adds back non-cash, after-tax, acquisition-related intangible amortization and transaction and integration costs of $0.36 per diluted share. GAAP operating income for the quarter was a record $584.9 million. Adjusted operating income increased 20% to a record $594.7 million and operating margins were 26.8%, up 60 basis points from the third quarter of 2025. Operating cash flow in the quarter was up 27% to $560.6 million and free cash flow to net income conversion was 124%. A reconciliation of reported GAAP results to adjusted results is included in the financial tables accompanying this release and on the AMETEK website. AMETEK delivered outstanding results in the third quarter, stated David A. Zapico, AMETEK Chairman and Chief Executive Officer . Continued strong organic sales and orders growth, contributions from recent acquisitions, and tremendous operating performance led to 20% operating profit growth, an excellent 110 basis points of core margin expansion, and record free cash flow. In addition, during the third quarter we completed the acquisition of Indicor Instrumentation, an outstanding group of highly differentiated industrial technology businesses. Electronic Instruments Group (EIG) EIG sales in the third quarter were a record $1.44 billion, an increase of 16% over the third quarter of 2025. On a GAAP basis, EIG s third quarter operating income was a record $406.7 million. On an adjusted basis, EIG s operating income was up 15% to a record $414.6 million. EIG delivered excellent results in the third quarter with robust sales growth, exceptional orders growth, and outstanding operating performance, noted Mr. Zapico. Sales growth in the quarter was broad based across our businesses driven by strong organic sales growth and contributions from recent acquisitions. Additionally, EIG s tremendous operating performance drove core margin expansion of 60 basis points in the quarter. Page 1 Electromechanical Group (EMG) EMG sales in the third quarter were a record $778.0 million, up 20% from the prior year period. In the quarter, EMG s GAAP operating income was a record $210.0 million. On an adjusted basis, EMG s operating income increased 29% to a record $212.0 million and operating income margins were 27.2%. EMG delivered exceptional results in the third quarter. Overall sales were up 20%, driven by outstanding, broad-based organic sales growth and contributions from recent acquisitions. EMG also delivered tremendous operating results with a sizeable 29% growth in adjusted operating profit and 210 basis points of core margin expansion, noted Mr. Zapico. Fourth Quarter and Full Year 2026 Outlook Our performance in the third quarter showcased the quality of our businesses, attractiveness of our end market exposures and the proven effectiveness of the AMETEK Growth Model. Our businesses are well positioned as mission-critical providers of highly engineered solutions and their alignment with key secular growth trends supports our broad-based growth in sales and orders. Additionally, our operating results reflect the value of our disciplined capital deployment and acquisition strategy, added Mr. Zapico. For 2026, we now expect overall sales to be up mid-teens on a percentage basis versus 2025. Adjusted earnings per diluted share are now expected to be in the range of $8.53 to $8.57, up approximately 15% from our full year 2025 results. This is an increase from our prior guidance range of $8.20 to $8.30 per diluted share reflecting our excellent third quarter results and outlook for the fourth quarter, as well as the expected contribution from the acquisition of Indicor Instrumentation, he added. For the fourth quarter of 2026, overall sales are expected to be up in the low 20% range versus the fourth quarter of 2025. Adjusted earnings in the quarter are expected to be in the range of $2.26 to $2.30 per share, up 12% to 14% compared to the fourth quarter of 2025, concluded Mr. Zapico. Corporate Profile AMETEK (NYSE AME) is a leading global provider of industrial technology solutions serving a diverse set of attractive niche markets with annualized sales of approximately $9.0 billion. The AMETEK Growth Model integrates the Four Growth Strategies - Operational Excellence, Technology Innovation, Global and Market Expansion, and Strategic Acquisitions - with a disciplined focus on cash generation and capital deployment. AMETEK's objective is double-digit percentage growth in earnings per share over the business cycle and a superior return on total capital. Founded in 1930, AMETEK has been listed on the NYSE for over 95 years and is a component of the S P 500. For more information, visit www.ametek.com.
