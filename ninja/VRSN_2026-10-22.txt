 Document Verisign Reports Third Quarter 2026 Results RESTON, VA - Oct. 22, 2026 - VeriSign, Inc. (NASDAQ VRSN), a global provider of critical internet infrastructure and domain name registry services, today reported financial results for the third quarter of 2026. VeriSign, Inc. and its subsidiaries ( Verisign ) reported revenue of $443 million for the third quarter of 2026, up 5.7 percent from the same quarter in 2025. Operating income was $296 million for the third quarter of 2026, compared to $284 million for the same quarter of 2025. Verisign reported net income of $213 million and diluted earnings per share (diluted EPS ) of $2.35 for the third quarter of 2026, compared to net income of $213 million and diluted EPS of $2.27 for the same quarter of 2025. During the third quarter of 2026 the Company recognized a pre-tax operating loss of $7.5 million related to certain assets held for sale, which decreased diluted EPS by $0.06. We delivered a strong quarter operationally, having grown the domain name base by 6.1% year-over-year. The rollout of the .web top-level domain is on track, and we opened the limited beta phase of our high-assurance validation service, Verisign Informed , which can strengthen and enhance existing tools of trust that are deployed world-wide for authenticating data and users, said Jim Bidzos, Executive Chairman, President and Chief Executive Officer. Financial Highlights Verisign ended the third quarter of 2026 with cash, cash equivalents and marketable securities of $476 million, a decrease of $104 million from year-end 2025. Cash flow from operations was $268 million for the third quarter of 2026, compared to $308 million for the same quarter of 2025. Deferred revenues as of September 30, 2026, totaled $1.46 billion, an increase of $79 million from year-end 2025. During the third quarter of 2026, Verisign repurchased 0.7 million shares of its common stock for an aggregate cost of $189 million. As of September 30, 2026, there was $1.36 billion remaining for future share repurchases under the share repurchase program, which has no expiration. On October 19, 2026, Verisign s Board of Directors approved a cash dividend of $0.81 per share of Verisign s outstanding common stock to stockholders of record as of the close of business on November 17, 2026, payable on November 24, 2026. Business Highlights Verisign ended the third quarter of 2026 with 182.5 million .com and .net domain name registrations in the domain name base, a 6.1 percent increase from the end of the third quarter of 2025, and a net increase of 3.36 million domain names during the third quarter of 2026. During the third quarter of 2026, Verisign processed 13.1 million new domain name registrations for .com and .net, compared with 10.6 million for the third quarter of 2025. The final .com and .net renewal rate for the second quarter of 2026 was 75.2 percent compared to 75.5 percent for the same quarter of 2025. Renewal rates are not fully measurable until 45 days after the end of the quarter. About Verisign Verisign (NASDAQ VRSN), a global provider of critical internet infrastructure and domain name registry services, enables internet navigation for many of the world s most recognized domain names. Verisign helps enable the security, stability, and resiliency of the Domain Name System and the internet by providing root zone maintainer services, operating two of the 13 global internet root servers, and providing registration services and authoritative resolution for the .com and .net top-level domains, which support the majority of global e-commerce. To learn more please visit verisign.com .
