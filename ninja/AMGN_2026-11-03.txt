(Nov. 3, 2026) - Amgen (NASDAQ AMGN) today announced financial results for the third quarter of 2026. Amgen delivered another strong quarter as our strategy for long-term growth continued to produce results across the enterprise, said Robert A. Bradway, chairman and chief executive officer. The strength of our commercial portfolio, combined with important clinical advances and a promising late-stage pipeline, reinforces our confidence in Amgen s ability to deliver durable growth well into the next decade. Key results include For the third quarter, total revenues increased 8% to $10.4 billion in comparison to the third quarter of 2025. Produ ct sales grew 8% , driven by volume growth. Seventeen products delivered at least double-digit sales growth in the third quarter. Seventeen products are annualizing at more than $1 billion based on third quarter sales. GAAP earnings per share (EPS) increased 19% from $5.93 to $7.08 for the third quarter, driven by higher revenues. For the third quarter, GAAP operating income increased from $2.5 billion to $3.7 billion, and GAAP operating margin increased 9.9 percentage points to 37.5%. Non-GAAP EPS increased 14% from $5.64 to $6.45 for the third quarter, primarily driven by higher revenues, partially offset by higher operating expenses. For the third quarter, non-GAAP operating income increased from $4.3 billion to $4.7 billion, and non-GAAP operating margin increased 0.2 percentage points to 47.3%. The Company generated $3.7 billion of free cash flow in the third quarter of 2026 versus $4.2 billion in the third quarter of 2025, driven by changes in working capital, with the third quarter of 2025 benefiting from the timing of rebates and collections, partially offset by current period business performance. References in this release to non-GAAP measures, measures presented on a non-GAAP basis, and free cash flow (computed by subtracting capital expenditures from operating cash flow) refer to non-GAAP financial measures. Adjustments to the most directly comparable GAAP financial measures and other items are presented on the attached reconciliations. Refer to Non-GAAP Financial Measures below for further discussion.
