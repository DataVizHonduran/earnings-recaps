VERNON HILLS, Ill ., November 4, 2026 - CDW Corporation (Nasdaq CDW) announced today third quarter 2026 results. CDW also announced the approval by its Board of Directors of a quarterly cash dividend of $0.680 per common share to be paid on December 10, 2026, to all stockholders of record as of the close of business on November 25, 2026. CDW delivered robust third quarter results, powered by broad-based strength across our diverse customer end-markets and strong momentum in enterprise AI adoption, said Christine A. Leahy, chair and chief executive officer, CDW. By bringing together the technology, expertise and capabilities our customers need, we are helping advance their most important priorities and turn technology investment into meaningful business and mission outcomes. Strong customer demand and disciplined execution drove double-digit growth in gross profit, while operating leverage and capital management contributed to more than 20 percent growth in non-GAAP net income per diluted share, said Albert J. Miralles, chief financial officer, CDW. We continue to balance investments that support our customers and long-term growth with disciplined expense management and capital allocation, providing flexibility to deliver shareholder value. In an environment defined by rapid technology change and increasing complexity, CDW s role as a trusted advisor has never been more important, Leahy concluded. Our ability to address customers needs across the full technology stack and lifecycle, together with our continued strategic investments, reinforces our confidence in our ability to outpace US IT addressable market growth by 200 to 300 basis points on a constant currency basis and drive sustainable long-term value for our customers and shareholders. 1 Third Quarter of 2026 Highlights Net sales in the third quarter of 2026 were $6,755 million, compared to $5,737 million in the third quarter of 2025, an increase of 17.7 percent. There were 64 selling days for both the three months ended September 30, 2026 and 2025. Net sales on a constant currency basis increased 17.8 percent in the third quarter of 2026 compared to the third quarter of 2025. Customer demand drove Net sales growth primarily in data storage and servers, notebooks mobile devices, and software. The third quarter of 2026 Net sales performance included Commercial segment Net sales of $3,979 million, 20.8 percent higher than 2025. Commercial results were driven by an increase in Net sales to Financial Services, Corporate, and Healthcare customers of 73.2 percent, 16.8 percent, and 9.3 percent, respectively. Government segment Net sales of $913 million, 9.9 percent higher than 2025. Education segment Net sales of $982 million, 7.5 percent higher than 2025. Net sales for CDW s UK and Canadian operations, combined as Other for financial reporting purposes, were $880 million, 26.0 percent higher than 2025. Gross profit in the third quarter of 2026 was $1,385 million, compared to $1,256 million for the third quarter of 2025, representing an increase of 10.3 percent. Gross profit margin was 20.5 percent in the third quarter of 2026 compared to 21.9 percent in the third quarter of 2025. The decrease in Gross profit margin was primarily driven by mix into and lower margin in cert ain product categories. Selling and administrative expenses were $896 million in the third quarter of 2026, compared to $812 million in the third quarter of 2025, representing an increase of 10.3 percent. The increase was primarily due to higher performance-based incentives, workplace optimization costs, and acquisition costs. Operating income was $490 million in the third quarter of 2026, compared to $443 million in the third quarter of 2025, representing an increase of 10.4 percent. Non-GAAP operating income was $620 million in the third quarter of 2026, compared to $531 million in the third quarter of 2025, representing an increase of 16.8 percent. Operating income margin and Non-GAAP operating income margin were 7.2 percent and 9.2 percent, respectively, for the third quarter of 2026, compared to 7.7 percent and 9.2 percent, respectively, for the third quarter of 2025. Interest expense, net includes interest expense and interest income. Interest expense, net was $66 million in the third quarter of 2026, compared to $55 million for the third quarter of 2025, representing an increase of 19.8 percent. The increase is primarily due to higher average borrowings under our senior unsecured revolving loan facility and lower interest income earned on cash balances. The effective income tax rate was 26.9 percent in the third quarter of 2026, compared to 25.3 percent in the third quarter of 2025, which resulted in income tax expense of $114 million and $98 million, respectively. The increase in the effective income tax rate was primarily due to lower tax credits compared to prior year, and higher non-deductible expenses. Net income was $310 million in the third quarter of 2026, compared to $291 million in the third quarter of 2025, representing an increase of 6.5 percent. Non-GAAP net income was $410 million in the third quarter of 2026, compared to $357 million in the third quarter of 2025, representing an increase of 14.8 percent. Weighted-average diluted shares outstanding were 126 million for the third quarter of 2026, compared to 132 million for the third quarter of 2025. Net income per diluted share for the third quarter of 2026 was $2.46, compared to $2.21 for the third quarter of 2025, representing an increase of 11.6 percent. Non-GAAP net income per diluted share for the third quarter of 2026 was $3.26, compared to $2.71 for the third quarter of 2025, representing an increase of 20.4 percent. Non-GAAP Financial Measures Generally, a non-GAAP financial measure is a numerical measure of a company s performance or financial condition that either excludes or includes amounts that are not normally included or excluded in the most directly comparable measure calculated and presented in accordance with GAAP. Non-GAAP financial measures used by management may differ from similar measures used by other companies, even when similar terms are used to identify such measures. The non-GAAP performance measures discussed in this press release include Non-GAAP operating income, Non-GAAP operating income margin, Non-GAAP net income, Non-GAAP net income per diluted share, and Net sales on a constant currency basis, and the non-GAAP financial condition measures discussed in this press release include Free cash flow and Adjusted free cash flow. These non-GAAP performance measures and non-GAAP financial condition measures are collectively referred to as non-GAAP financial measures. The GAAP measures most directly comparable to Non-GAAP operating income, Non-GAAP operating income margin, Non-GAAP net income, Non-GAAP net income per diluted share, and Net sales on a constant currency basis are Operating income, Operating income margin, Net income, Net income per diluted share, and Net sales, respectively. The GAAP measure most directly comparable to Free cash flow and Adjusted free cash flow is Net cash provided by operating activities. Non-GAAP operating income excludes, among other things, charges related to the amortization of acquisition-related intangible assets, equity-based compensation and the associated payroll taxes, acquisition and integration expenses, transformation initiatives, and workplace optimization. Non-GAAP operating income margin is defined as Non-GAAP operating income as a percentage of Net sales. Non-GAAP net income and Non-GAAP net income per diluted share exclude, among other things, charges related to the amortization of acquisition-related intangible assets, equity-based compensation and the associated payroll taxes, acquisition and integration expenses, transformation initiatives, workplace optimization, and their associated income tax effects. Net sales on a constant currency basis is defined as Net sales excluding the impact of foreign currency translation on Net sales. Free cash flow is defined as Net cash provided by operating activities less capital expenditures. Adjusted free cash flow is defined as Free cash flow adjusted to include certain cash flows from financing activities incurred in the normal course of operations or as capital expenditures. We believe our non-GAAP performance measures provide analysts, investors, and management with useful information regarding the underlying operating performance of our business, because they remove the impact of items that management believes are not reflective of underlying operating performance. Management uses these measures to evaluate period-over-period performance as management believes they provide a more comparable measure of the underlying business. We also present non-GAAP financial condition measures because we believe they provide analysts, investors, and management with more information regarding our liquidity and capital resources. Certain non-GAAP financial measures are also used to determine certain components of performance-based compensation. When possible, we provide a reconciliation of non-GAAP financial measures to the most directly comparable GAAP financial measures. We are unable to provide a reconciliation of certain annual outlook non-GAAP financial targets to the corresponding GAAP financial measures on a forward-looking basis because we believe it would not be possible to have the required information necessary to quantitatively reconcile such measures with sufficient 4 precision without unreasonable efforts due to, among other things, the potential variability of future events that either cannot be controlled, such as currency impacts or interest rates, or reliably predicted because they are not part of our underlying performance, such as refinancing activities or acquisition and integration expenses. For the same reasons, we are unable to address the probable significance of such unavailable information. The financial statement tables that accompany this press release include a reconciliation of non-GAAP financial measures to the applicable most comparable GAAP financial measures.
