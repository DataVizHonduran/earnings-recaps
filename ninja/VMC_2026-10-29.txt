 VULCAN REPORTS THIRD QUARTER 2026 RESULTS 
 
 
 Solid Execution Demonstrates Resilience of Aggregates-Led Business 
 
 
 Expands Footprint with Strategic Acquisitions 
 
 
 Targets Earnings Growth Again in 2027 
 
 
 Birmingham, Alabama October 29, 2026 Vulcan Materials Company (NYSE: VMC), the nation s largest producer of construction aggregates, today announced results for the quarter ended September 30, 2026. 
 Ronnie Pruitt, Vulcan Materials Chief Executive Officer, said, Our results this quarter reflect disciplined operational execution
 across our entire organization. Our teams stayed focused on our core priorities, driving operational efficiencies, protecting margins, and delivering dependable value to our customers in a dynamic environment marked by energy inflation. 
 At the same time, we are actively expanding our reach through strategic, disciplined acquisitions that reinforce our position in attractive, long-term growth markets.
 These targeted aggregates-led investments, combined with our core operational momentum, position us well for continued earnings growth and value creation for shareholders.

 Segment Results 
 
 
 Aggregates 
 Commercial and operational execution drove continued expansion in aggregates cash gross profit per ton despite ongoing energy headwinds in the third quarter. Segment
 gross profit was $593 million ($9.46 per ton), and cash gross profit was $750 million ($11.95 per ton). 
 
 
 As compared to the prior year, aggregates shipments decreased 3 percent. Shipments in the prior year s third quarter benefited from favorable weather across the
 Company s footprint following a slow start to the construction season due to severe winter weather and rainfall throughout the first half of the year. 
 
 
 Price growth in the third quarter remained widespread across the Company s footprint. Freight-adjusted selling prices increased 5 percent on a mix-adjusted basis (4
 percent, or $0.82 per ton, on a reported basis). Continued cost management and operational execution helped offset energy cost headwinds and inflationary pressures on some parts and supplies. Freight-adjusted unit cash cost of sales increased 7
 percent, or $0.72 per ton, over the prior year. Excluding the impact of higher diesel fuel costs, cash cost of sales increased 3 percent. 
 
 
 Asphalt and Concrete 
 Non-aggregates segment gross profit in the third quarter was $72 million, and cash gross profit was $88 million. Asphalt gross profit margin remained strong at 17
 percent, despite lower shipments and higher liquid asphalt costs. The prior year results included the Company s Houston asphalt and construction business that was divested in the fourth quarter of 2025. Concrete gross profit margin expanded to 9
 percent. The prior year s third quarter included results from the concrete assets in California, that were divested in the second quarter of 2026. 
 
 
 Selling, Administrative and General (SAG) 
 SAG expense in the quarter was $137 million, 6 percent lower than the prior year and 6.3 percent of total revenues. On a trailing-twelve months basis, SAG expense as a
 percent of total revenues was 6.9 percent and 30 basis points lower than the prior year. 
 
 
 Financial Position, Liquidity and Capital Allocation 
 Capital expenditures for maintenance and growth projects were $174 million in the third quarter, and the Company returned $417 million to shareholders through $350
 million of common stock repurchases and $67 million of dividends. 
 
 
 During the third quarter, the Company completed two bolt-on acquisitions that align with our aggregates-led strategy and enhance our position in Tennessee and expand our
 reach into southern Ohio. The Company remains well positioned for continued growth with a strong liquidity position and balance sheet profile.

 As of September 30, 2026, the ratio of total debt to trailing-twelve months Adjusted EBITDA was 2.0 times and within the Company s target range of 2.0 to 2.5 times. On a
 trailing-twelve months basis, return on average invested capital was 15.9 percent. 
 
 
 Outlook 
 Regarding the Company s outlook, Mr. Pruitt said, Our results this year highlight the durability of our business. Despite significant energy headwinds, we are
 preserving our Adjusted EBITDA margin and expanding unit profitability in aggregates. We have taken proactive pricing and cost actions to mitigate the elevated energy inflation that has continued into the fourth quarter. Backed by our operational
 and commercial discipline, we expect to deliver approximately $2.4 billion in Adjusted EBITDA for the full year. 
 
 
 Mr. Pruitt continued, As we look to 2027, our execution and ongoing portfolio expansion set the stage for another year of earnings growth. We anticipate a continuation
 of the current demand environment next year and expect to continue expanding aggregates cash gross profit per ton with mid- to high- single digit pricing gains through execution of the Vulcan Way of Selling and cost benefits from the Vulcan Way of
 Operating. 
 
 
 We have the right aggregates-led strategy and the operational rigor to navigate dynamic market conditions. By maintaining disciplined
 capital deployment, integrating high-quality assets into our network, and executing on the fundamentals we control, we will continue to generate strong cash flow and compound superior value for our shareholders.

 About Vulcan Materials Company 
 
 
 Vulcan Materials Company, a member of the S&P 500 Index with headquarters in Birmingham, Alabama, is the nation's largest supplier of construction aggregates 
 primarily crushed stone, sand and gravel and a major producer of aggregates-based construction materials, including asphalt and ready-mixed concrete. For additional information about Vulcan, go to www.vulcanmaterials.com .
