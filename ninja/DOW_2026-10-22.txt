 Document Exhibit 99.1 October 22, 2026 Dow reports third quarter 2026 results FINANCIAL HIGHLIGHTS Net sales were $11.3 billion, up 13% year-over-year, reflecting increases in all operating segments. Local price increased 14% versus the year-ago period, led by gains in Packaging Specialty Plastics, with higher polyethylene prices in all regions. Currency was flat year-over-year. Volume decreased 1% year-over-year. This reflects higher volumes in all regions except Asia Pacific, where impacts from the Middle East conflict are most pronounced. GAAP net income was $525 million. Operating EBIT 1 was $887 million, up $707 million year-over-year, primarily driven by higher prices as well as benefits from the Company s collective self-help actions, which more than offset higher raw materials and logistics costs. GAAP earnings per share (EPS) was $0.62 operating EPS 1 was $0.71, compared to a loss of $0.19 in the year-ago period. Op. EPS excludes significant items totaling $0.09 per share, primarily driven by costs associated with the Company s collective self-help actions. Cash provided by operating activities continuing operations was $1.8 billion, up $709 million year-over-year, reflecting improved earnings and working capital, and up $515 million sequentially, primarily reflecting working capital improvements. Free cash flow 1 was $1.1 billion, with trailing twelve-month cash flow conversion 1 of 84%. Returns to shareholders totaled $253 million of dividends in the quarter. CEO QUOTE Dow delivered a solid third quarter, driven by targeted downstream growth and disciplined execution against an evolving macro backdrop, said Karen S. Carter, Dow chief executive officer. Our teams are delivering on our unique-to-Dow self-help commitments including our 2025 $1 billion cost program, our European asset actions, and Transform to Outperform. Specific to the transformation, we are on track to achieve the upsized $700 million in growth and productivity benefits in 2026, as well as the approximately $2 billion in total uplift by the end of next year. In addition, we captured sequential gains from the first of our three European asset shutdowns, where the total benefits are expected to build through 2027. We also remain focused on cash, including releasing more than $400 million of working capital during the quarter and reducing debt by approximately $600 million year-to-date. Our near-term actions are building a Dow that delivers stronger, more consistent results and long-term value for our shareholders. OUTLOOK We are building a more resilient and competitive Dow that is positioned to create long-term value through our continued focus on three priorities, said Carter. First, driving growth and innovation in higher-value areas of our businesses where demand is growing well above GDP including applications for AI infrastructure, home and personal care, electronics, flexible packaging and mobility. Second, we are taking action to strengthen the competitiveness of Dow s portfolio by investing in differentiated low-cost positions while shutting down higher-cost upstream capacity. And third, we re maintaining a disciplined and balanced approach to capital allocation that supports long-term shareholder value creation. These priorities are underpinned by our self-help actions, which are on track to deliver more than $1 billion of EBITDA uplift in both 2026 and 2027. Operating Earnings Per Share is defined as Earnings (loss) per common share - diluted excluding the after-tax impact of significant items. Operating EBIT is defined as earnings (i.e., Income (loss) before income taxes ) before interest, excluding the impact of significant items. Operating EBIT Margin is defined as Operating EBIT as a percentage of net sales. Operating EBITDA is defined as earnings (i.e., Income (loss) before income taxes ) before interest, depreciation and amortization, excluding the impact of significant items. Free Cash Flow is defined as Cash provided by operating activities - continuing operations, less capital expenditures. Under this definition, Free Cash Flow represents the cash generated by the Company from operations after investing in its asset base. Free Cash Flow, combined with cash balances and other sources of liquidity, represent the cash available to fund obligations and provide returns to shareholders. Free Cash Flow is an integral financial measure used in the Company's financial planning process. Cash Flow Conversion is defined as Cash provided by operating activities - continuing operations, divided by Operating EBITDA. Management believes Cash Flow Conversion is an important financial metric as it helps the Company determine how efficiently it is converting its earnings into cash flow.
