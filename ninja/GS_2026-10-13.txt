Diluted earnings per common share (EPS) 1 was $ 17.01 for the third quarter of 2026 compared with $ 12.25 for the third quarter of 2025 and $ 20.98 for the second quarter of 2026, and was $ 55.56 for the first nine months of 2026 compared with $ 37.33 for the first nine months of 2025. Annualized return on average common shareholders' equity (ROE) 2 was 18.7% for the third quarter of 2026 and 20.7% for the first nine months of 2026. The increase compared with the third quarter of 2025 primarily reflected significantly higher net revenues in Global Banking Markets. Net Revenues $ in millions 3Q26 $ 18,679 Global Banking Markets Net revenues in Global Banking Markets were $ 13.46 billion for the third quarter of 2026, 32% higher than the third quarter of 2025 and 13% lower than the second quarter of 2026. Investment banking fees were $ 3.22 billion, 21% higher than the third quarter of 2025, primarily due to higher net revenues in Advisory, reflecting an increase in completed mergers and acquisitions volumes, and significantly higher net revenues in Equity underwriting, primarily reflecting higher net revenues from initial public offerings and secondary offerings. Net revenues in Debt underwriting were also higher, primarily reflecting significantly higher net revenues from investment-grade activity. The firm s Investment banking fees backlog 3 decreased compared with the end of the second quarter of 2026 and decreased slightly compared with the end of 2025. Net revenues in Fixed Income, Currency and Commodities (FICC) were $ 3.47 billion, essentially unchanged compared with the third quarter of 2025. Net revenues in FICC intermediation were lower, due to significantly lower net revenues in mortgages and credit products and lower net revenues in interest rate products, partially offset by significantly higher net revenues in currencies and commodities. Net revenues in FICC financing were higher, primarily due to higher net revenues in mortgages and structured lending. Net revenues in Equities were $ 6.44 billion, 72% higher than the third quarter of 2025, due to significantly higher net revenues in Equities financing (driven by significantly higher net revenues in prime financing and portfolio financing) and in Equities intermediation (primarily driven by significantly higher net revenues in derivatives). Net revenues in Other were $ 322 million, compared with $ 282 million for the third quarter of 2025, primarily reflecting higher net revenues in transaction banking.
