 Document For Immediate Release Hasbro Reports Third Quarter 2026 Financial Results Magic The Gathering Surpasses $600 million in Quarterly Revenue for First Time Raises 2026 Financial Outlook Doubles Share Repurchase Target Pawtucket, R.I., October 20, 2026 Hasbro, Inc. (NASDAQ HAS), a leading games, IP, and toy company, today reported financial results for the third quarter 2026. Inspiring a lifetime of play is our superpower, and this quarter showed what it can do, said Chris Cocks, Hasbro Chief Executive Officer. We delivered a fourth consecutive quarter of double-digit revenue growth, and Magic The Gathering topped $600 million in quarterly revenue for the first time. Our asset-light licensing model lets great partners extend our brands while we focus on what we do best. With a Magic lineup that keeps building, we expect a strong finish to 2026 and continued growth in 2027. This quarter again showed the power of Hasbro s brand portfolio paired with strong execution, said Gina Goetter, Hasbro Chief Financial Officer and Chief Operating Officer. Given this momentum, we are raising our 2026 outlook and doubling our share repurchase target for the year, balancing investment in the business with returning cash to shareholders. Third Quarter 2026 Results Hasbro, Inc.'s revenue increased 15% vs. LY, driven by growth in Wizards and Digital Gaming (+31%), Consumer Products (+4%), and Entertainment (+9%). Operating profit was $517 million and Adjusted operating profit was $541 million (+52% vs. LY), reflecting topline momentum, favorable mix and cost productivity. Results include approximately $55 million of tariff refunds. Reported net earnings were $2.77 per diluted share and Adjusted net earnings per diluted share were $2.90. Returned $206 million to shareholders through the quarterly dividend and share repurchases. During the quarter, the Company deployed $79 million toward debt reduction through open-market bond repurchases and U.S. Treasury purchases to prefund the 2026 and 2027 bond maturities. Third Quarter 2026 Segment Details Wizards and Digital Gaming Segment Revenue increased 31%, led by Magic The Gathering (+34%). Digital and licensed gaming grew 3%. Magic The Gathering growth fueled by The Hobbit and Reality Fracture . Monopoly Go! contributed $41 million of revenue in the third quarter. Operating profit of $ 367 million (+46% vs. LY), with a 49% operating margin, includes a $7 million benefit from tariff refunds. Consumer Products Segment Revenues were up 4% in the quarter, led by Marvel , KPop Demon Hunters and Transformers . Q3 sales benefited from entertainment releases, including Spider-Man Brand New Day , and continued momentum in GEM 2 categories. Operating profit of $174 million and Adjusted operating profit of $178 million (+100% vs. LY) driven by productivity initiatives. Operating profit and adjusted operating profit include a $48 million benefit from tariff refunds. Entertainment Segment Revenue increased 9%, driven by growth in Family Brands. Operating profit of $17 million and Adjusted operating profit of $18 million up 63% due to favorable licensing mix. Year-to-Date 2026 Results Year-to-date Hasbro, Inc. revenue increased 15% vs. LY, driven by growth in Wizards and Digital Gaming (+28%) and Consumer Products (+3%), partially offset by a decline in Entertainment (-13%). Operating profit was $1,040 million and Adjusted operating profit was $1,110 million (+35% vs. LY), reflecting a strong topline. Both figures include a $56 million impairment related to digital games, nearly offset by a $55 million benefit from tariff refunds. Reported net earnings were $5.27 per diluted share and Adjusted net earnings per diluted share were $5.65. Returned $445 million to shareholders through the quarterly dividend and share repurchases. During the first nine months, the Company deployed $226 million toward debt reduction through open-market bond repurchases and U.S. Treasury purchases to prefund the 2026 and 2027 maturities. Year-to-Date 2026 Segment Details Wizards and Digital Gaming Segment Revenue increased 28%, led by Magic The Gathering (+34%). Digital and licensed gaming grew 7%. Magic The Gathering benefited from growth in tabletop and digital revenues, across first-party and Universes Beyond sets. Year-to-date Monopoly Go! contributed $127 million of revenue. Operating profit of $935 m illion (+29% vs. LY), with a 47% operating margin, which includes a $56 million impairment related to the Company s refocused digital games portfolio. Consumer Products Segment Revenues increased 3% as Marvel, Star Wars, Beyblade, and KPop Demon Hunters more than offset disruptions from the previously disclosed unauthorized network access. Operating profit o f $112 million and Adjusted operating profit of $130 million (+119% vs. LY) driven by productivity improvements. Entertainment Segment Revenue decline of 13% related to the nature and timing of deals. Operating profit of $40 million and Adjusted operating profit o f $47 million (+22% vs. LY) primarily due to lower program amortization. See the financial tables accompanying the press release for a reconciliation of GAAP to non-GAAP financial measures. 2026 Company Outlook and Capital Allocation For the full year, the Company now expects Total Hasbro revenue up 7-9% in constant currency (previously up 5-7% in constant currency) Adjusted operating margin of 27-28% (previously 25-26%) Adjusted EBITDA of $1.575 billion to $1.625 billion (previously $1.45 billion to $1.50 billion) 2026 Capital Allocation priorities Invest in core business. Return cash to shareholders through dividends and share repurchases. Continue to pay down debt. Dividend Announcement During the third quarter, the Company paid $99 million in cash dividends to shareholders. The Board of Directors has declared a quarterly cash dividend of $0.70 per common share payable on December 2, 2026, to shareholders of record at the close of business on November 18, 2026. About Hasbro Hasbro is a leading games, IP and toy company whose mission is to create joy and community through the magic of play. With 165 years of expertise, Hasbro delivers groundbreaking play experiences and reaches more than 1 billion fans annually around the world, through physical and digital games, video games, toys, licensed consumer products, location-based entertainment, film, TV and more. Through its franchise-first approach, Hasbro unlocks value from both new and legacy IP, including Magic The Gathering, Dungeons Dragons, Monopoly, Hasbro Games, Nerf, Transformers, Play-Doh and Peppa Pig, as well as premier partner brands. Powered by its portfolio of thousands of iconic marks and a diversified network of partners and subsidiary studios, Hasbro brings fans together wherever they are, from tabletop to screen. For more than a decade, Hasbro has been consistently recognized for its corporate citizenship, including being named one of the 100 Best Corporate Citizens by 3BL Media, a 2026 JUST Capital Industry Leader, a Brand that Matters by Fast Company, and one of the 50 Most Community-Minded Companies in the U.S. by the Civic 50 for fourteen consecutive years. Non-GAAP Financial Measures The financial tables accompanying this press release include non-GAAP financial measures as defined under SEC rules, specifically Adjusted operating profit, Adjusted operating margin, Adjusted net earnings and Adjusted net earnings per diluted share, which exclude, where applicable, acquired intangible amortization, strategic transformation initiatives, restructuring and severance costs, loss on disposal of business, eOne Film and TV business divestiture related costs, non-cash goodwill impairment charges, the nonrecurring gain on sale of assets, and the direct costs associated with the unauthorized network access. Also included in this press release are the non-GAAP financial measures of EBITDA and Adjusted EBITDA. EBITDA represents net earnings attributable to Hasbro, Inc. excluding interest expense, income tax expense, net earnings attributable to noncontrolling interests, depreciation and amortization of intangibles. Adjusted EBITDA also excludes strategic transformation initiatives, restructuring and severance costs, loss on disposal of business, eOne Film and TV business divestiture related costs, non-cash goodwill impairment charges, direct costs associated with the unauthorized network access, the nonrecurring gain on sale of assets, and the impact of stock compensation. As required by SEC rules, we have provided reconciliations on the attached schedules of these measures to the most directly comparable GAAP measure. Management believes that Adjusted net earnings, Adjusted net earnings per diluted share, Adjusted operating profit and Adjusted operating margin provide investors with an understanding of the underlying performance of our business absent unusual events. Management believes that EBITDA and Adjusted EBITDA are appropriate measures for evaluating the operating performance of our business because they reflect the resources available for strategic opportunities including, among others, to invest in the business, strengthen the balance sheet and make strategic acquisitions. The Company is not able to reconcile its forward-looking non-GAAP adjusted operating margin and adjusted EBITDA measures because the Company cannot predict with certainty the timing and amounts of discrete items such as charges associated with its cost-savings program, which could impact GAAP results. Constant currency is also a non-GAAP financial measure. The impact of changes in foreign currency exchange rates used to translate the consolidated statements of operations is quantified by translating the current or future period revenues at the prior period exchange rates and comparing this amount to the prior period reported revenues. The Company believes that the presentation of the impact of changes in exchange rates, which are beyond the Company s control, is helpful to an investor s understanding of the performance of the underlying business. These non-GAAP measures should be considered in addition to, not as a substitute for, or superior to, net earnings or other measures of financial performance prepared in accordance with GAAP as more fully discussed in our consolidated financial statements and filings with the SEC. As used herein, GAAP refers to accounting principles generally accepted in the United States of America.
