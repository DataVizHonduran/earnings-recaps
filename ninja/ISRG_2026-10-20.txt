 Document Exhibit 99.1 Contact Investor Relations (408) 523-2161 INTUITIVE ANNOUNCES THIRD QUARTER EARNINGS SUNNYVALE, CALIF., October 20, 2026 Intuitive (the Company ) (Nasdaq ISRG), a global technology leader in minimally invasive care and the pioneer of robotic-assisted surgery, today announced financial results for the quarter ended September 30, 2026. Q3 Highlights Worldwide procedures (da Vinci and Ion combined) grew approximately 15% compared with the third quarter of 2025. Da Vinci procedures grew approximately 14%, and Ion procedures grew approximately 31%. The Company placed 546 da Vinci surgical systems, compared with 427 in the third quarter of 2025. The third quarter 2026 da Vinci surgical system placements included 315 da Vinci 5 systems, compared with 240 in the third quarter of 2025. The Company placed 56 Ion endoluminal systems, compared with 50 in the third quarter of 2025. The Company grew its da Vinci surgical system installed base to 12,094 systems as of September 30, 2026, an increase of 12% compared with 10,763 as of September 30, 2025. The Company grew its Ion endoluminal system installed base to 1,152 systems as of September 30, 2026, an increase of 21% compared with 954 as of September 30, 2025. Third quarter 2026 revenue of $3.05 billion increased 22%, compared with $2.51 billion in the third quarter of 2025. Third quarter 2026 GAAP net income attributable to Intuitive Surgical, Inc. was $523 million, or $1.49 per diluted share, compared with $704 million, or $1.95 per diluted share, in the third quarter of 2025. Third quarter 2026 GAAP net income attributable to Intuitive Surgical, Inc. included a pre-tax charge of $450 million in connection with the settlement of previously disclosed antitrust class action litigation. The third quarter 2026 expense was $347 million, net of tax, or $0.99 per diluted share. Third quarter 2026 non-GAAP* net income attributable to Intuitive Surgical, Inc. was $1.05 billion, or $3.00 per diluted share, compared with $867 million, or $2.40 per diluted share, in the third quarter of 2025. Third quarter 2026 GAAP and non-GAAP* net income attributable to Intuitive Surgical, Inc. included a benefit of $22 million, net of tax, or $0.06 per diluted share, related to refunds for tariffs paid in prior periods under the International Emergency Economic Powers Act ( IEEPA ). The Company repurchased 7.8 million shares of its common stock for $2.94 billion in the third quarter of 2026. Q3 Financial Summary Gross profit, income from operations, net income attributable to Intuitive Surgical, Inc., and net income per diluted share attributable to Intuitive Surgical, Inc. are reported on a GAAP and non-GAAP* basis. The non-GAAP* measures are described below and are reconciled to the corresponding GAAP measures at the end of this release. Third quarter 2026 revenue was $3.05 billion, an increase of 22% compared with $2.51 billion in the third quarter of 2025. The higher third quarter revenue was driven by growth in procedure volume, higher operating lease revenue, and an increase in the installed base of systems. Third quarter 2026 instruments and accessories revenue increased by 18% to $1.80 billion, compared with $1.52 billion in the third quarter of 2025. The increase in instruments and accessories revenue was primarily driven by approximately 14% growth in da Vinci procedure volume and approximately 31% growth in Ion procedure volume. Third quarter 2026 systems revenue was $769 million, compared with $590 million in the third quarter of 2025. The higher systems revenue reflected increased da Vinci system placements and a higher lease installed base compared with the third quarter of 2025. The Company placed 546 da Vinci surgical systems, of which 315 were da Vinci 5 systems, in the third quarter of 2026, compared with 427 systems, of which 240 were da Vinci 5 systems, in the third quarter of 2025. The third quarter 2026 da Vinci surgical system placements included 291 systems placed under operating lease arrangements, of which 160 systems were placed under usage-based operating lease arrangements, Page 1 of 11 compared with 231 systems placed under operating lease arrangements, of which 115 systems were placed under usage-based operating lease arrangements in the third quarter of 2025. Third quarter 2026 GAAP income from operations decreased to $593 million, compared with $760 million in the third quarter of 2025. Third quarter 2026 GAAP income from operations included share-based compensation expense of $216 million, compared with $206 million in the third quarter of 2025. Third quarter 2026 non-GAAP* income from operations increased to $1.27 billion, compared with $976 million in the third quarter of 2025. In October 2026, following a mediation in the In re da Vinci Surgical Robot Antitrust Litigation class action, the Company reached written agreement, without any admission of liability or wrongdoing, to settle the previously disclosed antitrust litigation for a one-time payment of $450 million and mutually agreed injunctive relief. The Company recorded a $450 million charge to selling, general, and administrative expenses in the third quarter of 2026. The settlement remains subject to preliminary and final Court approval, and there can be no assurance the Court will approve it or that individual class members will not object. The third quarter 2026 expense was $347 million, net of tax, or $0.99 per diluted share. Third quarter 2026 GAAP net income attributable to Intuitive Surgical, Inc. was $523 million, or $1.49 per diluted share, compared with $704 million, or $1.95 per diluted share, in the third quarter of 2025. Third quarter 2026 GAAP net income attributable to Intuitive Surgical, Inc. included excess tax benefits of $13 million, or $0.04 per diluted share, compared with $24 million, or $0.07 per diluted share, in the third quarter of 2025. Additionally, third quarter 2026 GAAP net income included a benefit of $22 million, net of tax, or $0.06 per diluted share, related to refunds for tariffs paid in prior periods under IEEPA. Third quarter 2026 non-GAAP* net income attributable to Intuitive Surgical, Inc. was $1.05 billion, or $3.00 per diluted share, compared with $867 million, or $2.40 per diluted share, in the third quarter of 2025. Third quarter 2026 non-GAAP* net income included a benefit of $22 million, net of tax, or $0.06 per diluted share, related to refunds for tariffs paid in prior periods under IEEPA. The Company ended the third quarter of 2026 with $6.80 billion in cash, cash equivalents, and investments, a decrease of $1.83 billion during the quarter, primarily driven by cash used for repurchases of common stock and purchases of property, plant, and equipment, partially offset by cash generated from operations. Our strong performance this quarter was marked by broad-based adoption across our multi-port, single-port, and Ion platforms, with customers using our systems to perform, on average, over 10,000 procedures per day throughout the period, said Dave Rosa, Intuitive CEO. Page 4 of 11 *About Non-GAAP Financial Measures To supplement its consolidated financial statements, which are prepared and presented in accordance with U.S. generally accepted accounting principles ( GAAP ), the Company uses the following non-GAAP financial measures non-GAAP gross profit, non-GAAP income from operations, non-GAAP net income attributable to Intuitive, and non-GAAP net income per diluted share attributable to Intuitive ( EPS ). The presentation of this financial information is not intended to be considered in isolation or as a substitute for, or superior to, the financial information prepared and presented in accordance with GAAP. The Company uses these non-GAAP financial measures for financial and operational decision-making and as a means to evaluate period-to-period comparisons. The Company believes that these non-GAAP financial measures provide meaningful supplemental information regarding its performance by excluding items such as amortization of intangible assets, share-based compensation ( SBC ) and long-term incentive plan ( LTIP ) expenses, acquisition-related items, a facilities asset abandonment charge, litigation charges, gains on the sale of businesses and assets, gains or losses on strategic investments, tax adjustments, and other special items. LTIP expense relates to phantom share awards granted in China by the Company s Intuitive-Fosun joint venture to its employees that vest over four years and can remain outstanding for seven to ten years. These awards are valued based on certain key performance metrics. Accordingly, they are subject to significant volatility based on the performance of these metrics and are not tied to performance of the Company s business within the period. The Company believes that both management and investors benefit from referring to these non-GAAP financial measures in assessing its performance and when planning, forecasting, and analyzing future periods. These non-GAAP financial measures also facilitate management s internal comparisons to its historical performance. The Company believes these non-GAAP financial measures are useful to investors, because (1) they allow for greater transparency with respect to key metrics used by management in its financial and operational decision-making, and (2) they are used by institutional investors and the analyst community to help them analyze the performance of the Company s business. Non-GAAP gross profit . The Company defines non-GAAP gross profit as gross profit, excluding SBC and LTIP expenses, amortization of intangible assets, and acquisition-related items. Non-GAAP income from operations . The Company defines non-GAAP income from operations as income from operations, excluding SBC and LTIP expenses, amortization of intangible assets, a facilities asset abandonment charge, litigation charges, acquisition-related items, and gains on the sale of businesses and assets. Non-GAAP net income attributable to Intuitive and EPS . The Company defines non-GAAP net income as net income attributable to Intuitive, excluding SBC and LTIP expenses, amortization of intangible assets, a facilities asset abandonment charge, litigation charges, acquisition-related items, gains on the sale of businesses and assets, gains or losses on strategic investments, tax adjustments, including the excess tax benefits associated with SBC arrangements and the net tax effects related to intra-entity transfers of non-inventory assets, and adjustments attributable to noncontrolling interest in joint venture, net of the related tax effects. The Company excludes the excess tax benefits associated with SBC arrangements as well as the tax effects associated with non-cash amortization of deferred tax assets related to intra-entity non-inventory transfers, because the Company does not believe these items correlate with the ongoing results of its core operations. The tax effects of the non-GAAP items are determined by applying a calculated non-GAAP effective tax rate, which is commonly referred to as the with-and-without method. Without excluding these tax effects, investors would only see the gross effect that these non-GAAP adjustments had on the Company s operating results. The Company s calculated non-GAAP effective tax rate is generally higher than its GAAP effective tax rate. The Company defines non-GAAP EPS as non-GAAP net income attributable to Intuitive divided by diluted shares outstanding, which are calculated as GAAP weighted-average outstanding shares plus dilutive potential shares outstanding during the period. There are a number of limitations related to the use of non-GAAP measures versus measures calculated in accordance with GAAP. Non-GAAP gross profit, non-GAAP income from operations, non-GAAP net income attributable to Intuitive, and non-GAAP EPS exclude items such as SBC and LTIP expenses, amortization of intangible assets, excess tax benefits associated with SBC arrangements, and non-cash amortization of deferred tax assets related to intra-entity transfers of non-inventory assets, which are primarily recurring items. SBC expense has been, and will continue to be for the foreseeable future, a significant recurring expense in the Company s business. In addition, the components of the costs that the Company excludes in its calculation of non-GAAP net income attributable to Intuitive and non-GAAP EPS may differ from the components that its peer companies exclude when they report their results of operations. Management addresses these limitations by providing specific information regarding the GAAP amounts excluded from non-GAAP net income attributable to Intuitive and non-GAAP EPS Page 5 of 11 and evaluating non-GAAP net income attributable to Intuitive and non-GAAP EPS together with net income attributable to Intuitive and net income per share attributable to Intuitive calculated in accordance with GAAP.
