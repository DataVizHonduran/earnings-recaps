General and administrative During the third quarter, APA incurred general and administrative expenses totaling approximately $115 million. Third-quarter 2026 earnings call APA will host a conference call to discuss its third-quarter 2026 results at 10 a.m. Central time, Thursday, Nov. 5. About APA APA Corporation owns consolidated subsidiaries that explore for and produce oil and natural gas in the United States, Egypt and the United Kingdom and that explore for oil and natural gas offshore Suriname and elsewhere. APA posts announcements, operational updates, investor information and press releases on its website, www.apacorp.com .
