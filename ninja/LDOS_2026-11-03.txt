Revenues for the quarter were $4.63 billion, up 4% compared to the third quarter of 2025. Revenue grew year-over-year due to increased customer demand for defense tech products, air traffic management solutions, and intelligence mission support as well as the acquisition of ENTRUST Solutions, which offset declines in some managed health and federal civilian IT programs. For the third quarter, net income was $413 million, or $3.28 per diluted share. Net income and diluted EPS were up 12% and 16%, respectively, year-over-year net income margin was 8.9% compared to 8.3% in the third quarter of 2025. Net income and diluted EPS for the quarter reflect $12 million in costs associated with recent strategic corporate transactions. Adjusting for these and certain other items, non-GAAP net income increased 14% year-over-year, to $455 million for the third quarter, and non-GAAP diluted EPS increased 19% to $3.62. GAAP and non-GAAP earnings measures reflect a lower effective tax rate, primarily due to an increase in the federal research tax credit and a decrease in unrecognized tax benefits. In addition, adjusted EBITDA was $644 million for the third quarter, up 5% year-over-year. Adjusted EBITDA margin of 13.9% increased from 13.8% in the third quarter of 2025. NEW BUSINESS AWARDS Net bookings totaled $5.5 billion in the quarter, representing a book-to-bill ratio of 1.2. As a result, backlog at the end of the quarter was $49.6 billion, of which $11.1 billion was funded. Trailing-twelve-month book-to-bill of 1.1 resulted in year-over-year growth in total and funded backlog of 4% and 22%, respectively. Quarterly bookings included several key awards u U.S. Air Force Air Combat Command (ACC) Intelligence Directorate (A2) Intelligence, Surveillance, Reconnaissance (ISR). Leidos secured a $717 million contract to continue providing critical threat mitigation and ISR operations support for the Air Force's ACC A2. Leidos is executing at 35 global locations, delivering embedded advanced data fusion across ACC headquarters, numbered Air Forces, and wings to ensure that the joint force maintains a decisive information advantage. u Transportation Security Administration (TSA) Security Equipment Deployment Services (SEDS). TSA awarded Leidos a $672 million contract to continue to deploy, relocate, and manage critical security screening equipment across all federalized U.S. airports and major domestic special events. This award builds upon more than a decade of Leidos supporting TSA infrastructure, leveraging their deep logistics and integration expertise to protect the nation's transportation systems and adapt swiftly to emerging security threats. u Defense Health Agency (DHA) Military Health System (MHS) GENESIS Bridge. Leidos secured a 12-month, $318 million sole-source bridge contract to sustain and enhance MHS GENESIS, including system maintenance, cybersecurity, performance monitoring, technology updates, and end-user support. The award ensures continuity of critical operations while reinforcing Leidos' expertise in modernizing complex federal health systems and delivering technology that supports healthcare for service members and their families. u U.S. Army Defensive Cyber Solutions Branch (DCSB) Defensive Cyber Operations (DCO) 2.0. Leidos was awarded a $301 million follow-on contract to provide 24 7 365 defensive cyber operations for the Department of War Information Network (DoDIN). To support the agency's migration toward continuous, automated outcome delivery, Leidos is deploying its UpHold Effect Agentic SOC Automation platform. The platform utilizes autonomous AI agents that dramatically compresses threat response windows and eliminates analyst alert fatigue to ensure a hardened, reliable global network capable of defending against growing and evolving threats. u Space Development Agency (SDA) Accelerated Missile Defense Tranche 3 (AMDT3). Leidos secured a $272 million award from Sierra Space to provide infrared sensing payloads, onboard signal-processing capabilities, and mission support for the AMDT3 Tracking Layer. The payloads will fly on 18 missile-tracking satellites, which are designed to detect hypersonic and other advanced threats from Low Earth Orbit. This award expands Leidos' position in space sensing and onboard digital processing, and builds on its execution record on Tranches 0, 1, and 2. u United Kingdom (UK) National Air Traffic Services (NATS) Flight Data Processing (FDP) Evolution. Leidos received a $170 million contract to upgrade the core flight data processing software under the UK NATS FDP Evolutions program, replacing legacy systems with a modern architecture that automatically delivers real-time flight data to air traffic controllers. This award strengthens Leidos' global position in next-generation air traffic management and reinforces its pedigree for upcoming modernization projects. u U.S. Cyber Command (CYBERCOM) Cyberspace Operations Support Services (COSS). UNAUDITED NON-GAAP FINANCIAL MEASURES Leidos uses and refers to non-GAAP operating income, non-GAAP operating margin, adjusted EBITDA, adjusted EBITDA margin, non-GAAP diluted EPS, non-GAAP free cash flow and non-GAAP free cash flow conversion, which are not measures of financial performance under generally accepted accounting principles in the U.S. and, accordingly, these measures should not be considered in isolation or as a substitute for the comparable GAAP measures and should be read in conjunction with Leidos's consolidated financial statements prepared in accordance with GAAP. Management believes that these non-GAAP measures provide another representation of the results of operations and financial condition, including its ability to comply with financial covenants. These non-GAAP measures are frequently used by financial analysts covering Leidos and its peers. The computation of non-GAAP measures may not be comparable to similarly titled measures reported by other companies, thus limiting their use for comparability. Organic revenues capture the revenue that is inherent in the underlying business excluding the impact of acquisitions and divestitures made within the prior year it is computed as current revenues excluding revenues from acquisitions within the last 12 months and divestitures within the current and year-ago periods. Non-GAAP operating income is computed by excluding the following discrete items from operating income u Acquisition, integration and restructuring costs Represents acquisition, integration, lease termination, severance and retention costs and asset markdowns related to acquisitions and restructuring activities. u Amortization of acquired intangible assets Represents the amortization of the fair value of the acquired intangible assets. We do not exclude the revenue associated with these acquired intangible assets from non-GAAP operating income. u Asset impairment charges Represents impairments of long-lived intangible assets and other assets. Non-GAAP non operating income is computed by excluding the discrete items from operating income and the following discrete items from non operating income. u Settlement loss on pension plan buy-out Represents the settlement loss in connection with the buy-out of our UK defined benefit pension plan. u Acquisition related financing costs Represents the cost associated with the termination of the bridge loan facility in connection with the acquisition of Entrust. Non-GAAP operating margin is computed by dividing non-GAAP operating income by revenues. Adjusted EBITDA is computed by excluding the following items from income before income taxes (i) discrete items as identified above (ii) interest expense (iii) interest income (iv) depreciation expense and (v) amortization of internally developed intangible assets. Adjusted EBITDA margin is computed by dividing adjusted EBITDA by revenues. Non-GAAP net income is computed by excluding the discrete items listed under non-GAAP operating income and non-GAAP non operating income and their related tax impacts. Non-GAAP diluted EPS is computed by dividing net income attributable to Leidos common stockholders, adjusted for the discrete items as identified above and the related tax impacts, by the diluted weighted average number of common shares outstanding. Non-GAAP free cash flow is computed by deducting expenditures for property, equipment and software from net cash provided by (used in) operating activities.
