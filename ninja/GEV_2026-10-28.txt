(NYSE GEV), a unique industry leader enabling the world to electrify to thrive and decarbonize, today reported financial results for the third quarter ending September 30, 2026. We delivered strong financial results in the third quarter as demand for electricity infrastructure increases around the world. We now expect our backlog to exceed $200 billion in the fourth quarter, with improved margins, and to exit 2026 with at least 130 GW of gas equipment under contract. Given our performance and confidence in our trajectory, we are raising our 2026 financial guidance , said GE Vernova CEO Scott Strazik. We continue to innovate and develop new solutions for utility and data center customers to meet growing demand , including electrification technologies that support integration and resiliency . Our opportunity to create value is accelerating and I m proud of how our team is executing with discipline and focus. In the quarter, orders of $29.3 billion increased + 96% organically, with robust equipment growth in Power and continued services strength. Revenue of $12.6 billion was up + 27% , + 18% organically*, led by equipment growth at Power and Electrification, along with higher services, partially offset by equipment at Wind . Margins expanded significantly from higher price, volume, and productivity . Free cash flow* of $ 5.4 billion increased $ 4.7 billion, primarily due to higher positive benefits from working capital and stronger adjusted EBITDA*, partially offset by higher taxes . Power Orders of $21.8 billion increased + 178% organically and revenues of $6.1 billion increased + 25% on a U.S. GAAP basis and organically*, led by Gas Power equipment. Segment EBITDA margin grew + 800 basis points, + 830 basis points organically*. Signed 11 gigawatts (GW) of new gas equipment contracts, including 8 GW of slot reservation agreements and 3 GW of orders. Converted 13 GW of existing slot reservation agreements to orders and shipped 5 GW of equipment resulting in backlog growth from 53 to 64 GW and a decrease in slot reservation agreements from 63 to 58 GW . Electrification Orders of $5.4 billion decreased (9)% organically, given large synchronous condenser orders in the third quarter of last year, with continued strong demand for grid equipment and a book-to-bill ratio of approximately 1.4 . Revenues of $3.9 billion increased + 51% , + 16% organically*, driven by Grid Systems Integration and Power Transmission . Segment EBITDA margin grew + 400 basis points, + 470 basis points organically*. Increased equipment backlog to $ 42.2 billion, up $ 15.7 billion, or 60% year-over-year, including $5 billion from Prolec GE . Wind Orders of $2.1 billion increased 16% organically due to Onshore Wind, primarily in North America . Revenues of $2.7 billion increased 4% , 3% organically*, primarily driven by Onshore Wind services and Offshore Wind equipment, partially offset by Onshore Wind equipment. Segment EBITDA margin grew + 490 basis points , +420 basis points organically*. Resolved all outstanding litigation regarding the Vineyard Wind offshore wind project, providing reliable, affordable energy to more than 400,000 homes and businesses across Massachusetts.
