CHANDLER, Arizona - November 5, 2026 - (NASDAQ MCHP) - Microchip Technology Incorporated, a broadline supplier of semiconductors committed to making innovative design easier through total system solutions, today reported results for the three months ended September 30, 2026. Our September quarter net sales were above the high end of our guidance, increasing 42.2% year over year and 9.2% sequentially to $1.622 billion, said Steve Sanghi, Microchip s President and Chief Executive Officer. The momentum from the start of fiscal 2027 continued in the September quarter, with broad-based growth across our end markets, driven by industrial and data center applications. Our growth is increasingly supported by new design wins and product innovations, which we believe positions us well for the opportunities ahead. - - more - - Microchip Technology Incorporated 2355 West Chandler Blvd. Chandler, AZ 85224-6199 Main Office 480 792 7200 Microchip Technology Reports Second Quarter Fiscal 2027 Financial Results Page 2 Mr. Sanghi added, Bookings remained robust, with book-to-bill well above one, consistent with recent quarters. We are winning new designs across our end markets, including data center, where our PCIe Gen6 switch and retimer design wins have grown to 24 to date, up from 12 at the end of the June quarter. We also broadened our portfolio, with a steady cadence of product and technology introductions across data center, aerospace and defense, automotive and industrial applications, which expands the foundation for future design wins. Eric Bjornholt, Microchip's Corporate Senior Vice President and Chief Financial Officer, said, Our September quarter results reflect ongoing operational improvements, with non-GAAP operating expenses growing well below net sales and supporting higher profitability. We believe our existing manufacturing footprint enables us to grow with modest incremental capital investment, and we remain focused on strengthening our balance sheet while making targeted investments that accelerate our product roadmap. Mr. Sanghi concluded, As we enter the December quarter, we are experiencing some normal seasonal factors in our business. Normal seasonality is for our revenue to be down sequentially 3% to 5% in the December quarter. We expect net sales for the December quarter to be between down 3% and up 1% sequentially, with a midpoint of down 1%. The midpoint of our December quarter guidance represents year-over-year growth of approximately 35.4%. Our guidance of down 1% at the midpoint needs to be considered with the fact that we had about $53 million of infrequent revenue items in the September quarter which do not repeat in the December quarter. Taking this into consideration, the midpoint of our guidance for the December quarter represents guidance that is 5% to 7% better than normal seasonality. Lead times are lengthening across our supply chain, and we are working to expand capacity and secure additional wafer allocation. We expect non-GAAP gross margin to be between 64% and 65% and GAAP gross margin to be between 62.9% and 63.9%.
