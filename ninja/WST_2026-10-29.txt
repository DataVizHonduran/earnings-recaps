 Document Exhibit 99.1 West Reports Third-Quarter 2026 Results Raising Full-Year Net Sales and Adjusted-Diluted EPS guidance Exton, PA, October 29, 2026 West Pharmaceutical Services, Inc. (NYSE WST), a leading provider of innovative, high-quality injectable solutions and services, today announced its financial results for the third quarter of 2026. Third-Quarter 2026 Summary (comparisons to prior-year period) Net sales of $874.5 million increased 8.7% organic growth was 11.9%. Diluted earnings per share ( EPS ) of $3.02 increased 57.3%. Adjusted-diluted EPS of $2.66 increased 35.7%. The Company repurchased $231 million of stock in the quarter, bringing the year-to-date total to $685 million. Year-to-date operating cash flow was $403 million. Capital expenditures were $125 million. The Company also announced on October 19, 2026, that its Board of Directors declared a fourth-quarter dividend of $0.26 per share, an increase of 18% over the $0.22 per share paid in the prior quarter. Outlook for Full-Year 2026 Full-year 2026 net sales guidance increased to a range of $3.41 billion to $3.42 billion, up from the previous range of $3.345 billion to $3.380 billion. Full-year 2026 adjusted-diluted EPS guidance increased to a range of $9.38 to $9.48, up from the previous range of $8.85 to $9.05. Michel Lagarde, President and Chief Executive Officer, commented I am pleased to report strong third-quarter 2026 results, with net sales and adjusted EPS exceeding our expectations. Our results were again led by our High-Value Product (HVP) Components business. The robust sales performance drove strong operating income margin expansion and EPS growth. As a result of our team's strong execution in the third quarter, we are increasing our full-year 2026 guidance. Mr. Lagarde concluded I want to thank our global teams for their relentless focus on execution to deliver products and services for our customers and the patients they serve. West is a critical primary containment and drug delivery partner for pharmaceutical companies, biotechs and CDMOs around the world. We are grateful for the trust our customers put in us, and we remain committed to driving continuous improvement to even better serve their current and future needs. Proprietary Products Segment Net sales of $728.8 million grew by 12.6% and increased 16.6% on an organic basis. High-Value Product ( HVP ) Components net sales of $466.7 million increased 19.7% and rose 20.3% on an organic basis. HVP Components accounted for 53% of total company net sales in the quarter. HVP Delivery Devices net sales of $99.1 million were flat, and were up 26.4% on an organic basis excluding foreign currency translation effects and the impact of the divestiture of SmartDose 3.5mL. HVP Delivery Devices accounted for 11% of total company net sales in the quarter. SmartDose 3.5mL net sales represented $11.8 million in the quarter and $29.8 million in the prior-year quarter and are excluded from both periods to calculate organic growth. Standard Products net sales of $163.0 million increased by 2.9% and rose 3.1% on an organic basis. Standard Products accounted for 19% of total company net sales this quarter. West Vantage Segment Net sales of $145.7 million decreased by 7.3% and declined 6.6% on an organic basis. West Vantage accounted for 17% of total company net sales in the quarter. Full-Year 2026 Financial Guidance The Company is increasing its full-year 2026 net sales guidance range to $3.41 billion to $3.42 billion, up from the previous range of $3.345 billion to $3.380 billion. Organic net sales growth guidance increased to a range of 11.3% to 11.6%, up from the prior range of 10% to 11%. SmartDose 3.5mL generated $55.3 million in net sales in the second half of 2025 and generated $11.8 million in net sales in the third quarter of 2026. No additional SmartDose 3.5mL product sales are expected for the remainder of the year. These net sales are excluded in both periods to calculate full-year 2026 organic growth. The Company is increasing its full-year 2026 adjusted-diluted EPS guidance range to $9.38 to $9.48, up from the previous range of $8.85 to $9.05. Full-year 2026 capital spending guidance is updated to a range of $215 million to $220 million, down from the prior range of $250 million to $275 million.
