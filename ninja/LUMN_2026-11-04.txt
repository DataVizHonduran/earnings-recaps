 Document NEWS RELEASE Lumen Technologies Reports Solid Third Quarter 2026 Results Strategic Growth and Operating Leverage Gain Momentum Company delivers 10% year-over-year Strategic revenue growth, remains on track for adjusted EBITDA inflection, and advances its transformation DENVER, Nov 4, 2026 — Lumen Technologies, Inc. (Nasdaq LUMN) today reported third quarter 2026 results, delivering strong Strategic revenue growth and remaining on track for the adjusted EBITDA inflection in 2026. The company continues progress toward its financial goals and strategic transformation with expanded adoption of its Network-as-a-Service (NaaS) platform and advanced portfolio simplification. Among the company s highlights from the quarter Financial Performance Third-quarter revenue was $2.72 billion. Strategic revenue grew 10% year over year to $1.28 billion, representing 55% of business revenue, up from 53% in the second quarter. Digital Platform Adoption More than 4,000 customers on Lumen s NaaS platform, active customers up 24% sequentially, and a 38% increase in active ports. 20% of NaaS Internet on Demand ports are carrying multiple services at nearly 100% direct margin, creating operating leverage and a scaled opportunity. Sales Momentum and Execution Strategic sales rose 30% year over year and Strategic waves sales increased 22% sequentially, building a solid foundation for future revenue growth. Lumen signed $700 million in additional PCF deals year to date, bringing cumulative signed agreements to over $13.5 billion. Portfolio Transformation and Innovation Lumen introduced Intelligent Internet across more than 10 million U.S. locations, unifying its internet offerings in one digitally managed, programmable service. Plans to accelerate copper decommissioning in Florida, Iowa, Nevada and Utah target lower legacy costs and investment in Strategic growth. Announced plans to redefine networking for the AI and quantum eras. We re delivering what we promised and building a stronger, more valuable Lumen, said Kate Johnson, CEO of Lumen. AI-driven demand for connectivity is fueling momentum in our Strategic revenue base. Customers are choosing our platform to simplify their networks and get more from every connection. As we modernize our portfolio, we re strengthening the economics of our business and investing in what comes next. Our transformation is translating into financial results a higher-quality revenue base, with stronger margins and more operating leverage, said Chris Stansbury, president and CFO of Lumen. The third-quarter results reinforce that Lumen is being built on Strategic revenue growth, not on preserving the past. Reconciliations of non-GAAP financial measures to the most comparable GAAP measures are included in the attached financial schedules and our Investor Relations website. Special Items. We use the term Special Items to describe items that impacted a period s statement of operations or cash flows which the Company believes do not relate to the ordinary course of the Company's business and do not reflect the Company's underlying business performance. As described herein, the Company presents certain GAAP and non-GAAP financial measures both including and excluding the effects of Special Items. Significant components of our Special Items reflected in this release are net gain on sale of business related to the sale of our Mass Markets Fiber-to-the-Home business to AT T and net losses associated with the early retirement of debt. The other main components of our Special Items include Modernization and Simplification costs, Transaction and Separation costs, and Income from Transition and Separation Services. Modernization and Simplification costs are associated with a multi-year transformation initiative to streamline our network infrastructure, product portfolio, and IT systems, and to modernize our workforce, designed to deliver $1 billion in annualized cost savings on a run-rate basis exiting 2027. Transaction and Separation costs reflect transaction and separation costs associated with the sale of our Mass Markets Fiber-to-the-Home business to AT T and additional transaction and separation costs associated with supporting transition and separation services of our previous divestitures. Income from Transition and Separation Services includes charges we billed for transitional services and IT professional services provided to the purchasers in connection with our recent divestitures. Other special items impacting Adjusted EBITDA and Net Loss include remittance of awards and associated fees related to the voluntary relinquishment of our program awards under the FCC's Rural Digital Opportunity Fund ( RDOF ), certain charges primarily related to the recognition of losses on disposal of certain operating assets related to our divestitures and certain charges or payments related to litigation related expenses arising from specific matters that are not indicative of normal, recurring business activities, and revenue related to support payments received under the Federal Communications Commission's Connect America Fund ( CAF ) Phase II program, which is also not indicative of normal, recurring business activities.
