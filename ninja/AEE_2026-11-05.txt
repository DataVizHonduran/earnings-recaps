LOUIS (November 5, 2026) — Ameren Corporation (NYSE AEE) today announced third quarter 2026 net income attributable to common shareholders in accordance with generally accepted accounting principles (GAAP) of $676 million, or $2.42 per diluted share, compared to third quarter 2025 GAAP net income of $640 million, or $2.35 per diluted share. Excluding a third quarter 2025 tax benefit described in the table below, Ameren recorded third quarter 2025 adjusted (non-GAAP) net income attributable to common shareholders of $592 million, or $2.17 per diluted share. Third quarter 2026 results, compared to adjusted third quarter 2025 results, reflected increased earnings on infrastructure investments to improve system reliability, resiliency and service quality, increased earnings on investments in innovative energy technologies, and higher Ameren Missouri electric retail sales, primarily driven by warmer temperatures. These positive contributions were partially offset by higher Ameren Missouri operations and maintenance expenses, primarily driven by increased reliability-focused tree trimming and energy center maintenance. Finally, the earnings per diluted share comparison reflected higher weighted-average basic common shares outstanding in the third quarter of 2026. Our third-quarter results were driven by continued execution of our strategy and disciplined investment in critical energy infrastructure to deliver reliable energy for our customers at the lowest cost possible, said Martin J. Lyons, Jr., chairman, president and chief executive officer of Ameren Corporation. We are well positioned to meet growing energy needs, support significant economic development across our region and deliver lasting benefits for the customers and communities we serve. Ameren Missouri Segment Results Ameren Missouri third quarter 2026 earnings were $582 million, compared to third quarter 2025 earnings of $518 million. The year-over-year increase reflected earnings on increased infrastructure investments, higher electric retail sales, primarily driven by warmer weather, and lower income tax expense due, in part, to timing differences. These positive factors were partially offset by higher operations and maintenance expenses, primarily driven by increased reliability-focused tree trimming and energy center maintenance, and higher interest expense. Ameren Transmission Segment Results Ameren Transmission third quarter 2026 earnings were $110 million, compared to third quarter 2025 GAAP and adjusted earnings of $151 million and $103 million, respectively. Adjusted earnings in 2025 excluded the tax benefit described in the table above. The year-over-year comparison reflected earnings on increased infrastructure investments. Ameren Illinois Electric Distribution Segment Results Ameren Illinois Electric Distribution third quarter 2026 earnings were $72 million, compared to third quarter 2025 earnings of $57 million. The year-over-year increase reflected earnings on increased infrastructure investments. Ameren Illinois Natural Gas Segment Results Ameren Illinois Natural Gas third quarter 2026 loss was $7 million, compared to third quarter 2025 loss of $13 million. The year-over-year comparison reflected increased infrastructure investments included in natural gas service rates that became effective December 2, 2025. Ameren Parent Results (includes items not reported in a business segment) Ameren Parent third quarter 2026 loss was $81 million, compared to a third quarter 2025 loss of $73 million. The year-over-year comparison primarily reflected higher income tax expense due, in part, to timing differences, and higher interest expense, partially offset by higher earnings from investments in innovative energy technologies. About Ameren St. Louis-based Ameren Corporation powers the quality of life for 2.5 million electric customers and more than 900,000 natural gas customers in a 64,000-square-mile area through its Ameren Missouri and Ameren Illinois rate-regulated utility subsidiaries. Ameren Illinois provides electric transmission and distribution service and natural gas distribution service. Ameren Missouri provides electric generation, transmission and distribution service, as well as natural gas distribution service. Ameren Transmission Company of Illinois develops, owns and operates rate-regulated regional electric transmission projects in the Midcontinent Independent System Operator (MISO), Inc. For more information, visit Ameren.com, or follow us at AmerenCorp, Facebook.com AmerenCorp, or LinkedIn.com company Ameren. Use of Non-GAAP Financial Measures In this release, Ameren has presented adjusted earnings and adjusted earnings per share, which are non-GAAP measures and may not be comparable to those of other companies. A reconciliation of GAAP to non-GAAP information is included in this release. Generally, adjusted earnings or losses include earnings or losses attributable to Ameren common shareholders and exclude income or loss from significant discrete items that management does not consider representative of ongoing earnings. Ameren uses adjusted earnings internally for financial planning and for analysis of performance. Ameren also uses adjusted earnings as the primary performance measurement when communicating with analysts and investors regarding our earnings results and outlook, as the company believes that adjusted earnings allow the company to more accurately compare its ongoing performance across periods. In providing adjusted earnings guidance, there could be differences between adjusted earnings and earnings prepared in accordance with GAAP as a result of our treatment of certain items.
