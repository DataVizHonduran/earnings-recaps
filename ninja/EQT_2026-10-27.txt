 Document EQT Reports Third Quarter 2026 Results CANONSBURG, October 27, 2026 -- EQT Corporation (NYSE EQT) today announced financial and operational results for the third quarter of 2026. Bcf of storage capacity at Leaf River Energy Center's salt dome expansion project along the Gulf Coast increases EQT's Gulf Coast storage capacity up to 11 Bcf and provides optimization flexibility to support the LNG business Dividend Increase Increased dividend by 3% to $0.68 per share annualized compounded annual dividend growth rate of 7% since 2022 with durability underpinned by material cost structure improvements and synergy capture over this period President and CEO Toby Z. Rice stated, Third quarter results built on what has been an exceptional year of execution at EQT. Despite downward pressure on natural gas prices during the quarter, our marketing efforts drove stronger realized pricing, operating expenses came in at the low end of guidance and capital spending was well below expectations as efficiencies continue to accrue. This resulted in yet another quarter of greater-than-expected free cash flow generation. Rice added, We also announced our strategic combination with Range Resources, pairing two of the highest-quality, longest-duration asset bases in North America and creating a company uniquely positioned for the next era of natural gas. Our expanded resource base solidifies EQT as the operator of choice for premium, long-term supply arrangements and provides unique growth optionality underpinned by contractual demand. We believe this combination creates a must-own energy company capable of generating durable, through-cycle free cash flow while compounding value for shareholders for decades to come. Adjusted EPS is defined as adjusted net income attributable to EQT divided by diluted weighted average common shares outstanding. The Company's management believes that adjusted net income attributable to EQT and adjusted EPS provide useful information to investors regarding the Company s financial condition and results of operations because it helps facilitate comparisons of operating performance and earnings trends across periods by excluding the impact of items that, in their opinion, do not reflect the Company s core operating performance. For example, adjusted net income attributable to EQT and adjusted EPS reflect only the impact of settled derivative contracts thus, the measures exclude the often-volatile revenue impact of changes in the fair value of derivative instruments prior to settlement.
