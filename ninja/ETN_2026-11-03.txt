 Document Exhibit 99 Eaton Reports Record Third Quarter 2026 Results, with Strong Organic Growth, Accelerating Record Orders and Backlog Raises Organic Growth Guidance Third quarter sales increased 28%, with organic sales growth of 19%, above the high end of guidance Twelve-month rolling average organic orders increased 39% in Electrical Americas, 51% in Electrical Global and 20% in Aerospace, with order dollars reaching record levels in all three segments, driven by broad end-market strength Strong year-over-year total backlog growth of 46% in Electrical and 33% in Aerospace Record segment margins of 25.3%, above the high end of guidance, with Electrical Americas margins improving 230 basis points sequentially Announced agreement to acquire COL Group, expanding manufacturing capacity and power distribution capabilities for data center and utility markets in Europe Full year 2026 earnings per share expected to be between $10.39 and $10.49 adjusted earnings per share expected to be between $13.70 and $13.80 DUBLIN — November 3, 2026 — Intelligent power management company Eaton Corporation plc (NYSE ETN) today reported strong third quarter 2026 results, including earnings per share of $2.77. Adjusted earnings per share were a record of $3.68, excluding charges of $0.62 per share related to intangible amortization, $0.30 per share related to acquisitions and divestitures, and income of $0.01 per share related to a multi-year restructuring program. Sales in the quarter were a record $8.9 billion, up 28% from the third quarter of 2025. The sales increase consisted of 19% growth in organic sales, 8% growth from acquisitions, and 1% growth from foreign exchange. Segment margins were a record 25.3%, 30 basis points above both the high end of the guidance range and the third quarter of 2025, driven by higher sales and improved performance amid continued capacity investments to support growth. The Company continued to generate solid cash flow, supporting investment in growth initiatives while maintaining financial flexibility. Operating cash flow was $1.31 billion, and free cash flow was $1.03 billion. Paulo Ruiz, Eaton chief executive officer, said, Our strong third quarter performance reflects the focus and discipline our teams have demonstrated all year as we expand capacity, strengthen execution and position the business to meet accelerating customer demand. We delivered organic growth and segment margins above the high end of our guidance, driven by strong demand across our markets and continued momentum across our Electrical businesses. Record orders grew sequentially, backlog continued to expand, and we remain well-positioned to help customers deliver on their critical power infrastructure investments. These results reinforce our confidence in the remainder of the year. In the quarter, the company also announced an agreement to acquire COL Group, a leader in medium-voltage electrical distribution solutions, expanding the company s European power distribution capabilities and manufacturing footprint and enhancing its ability to support growing customer demand across data center and utility markets. BUSINESS SEGMENT INFORMATION During the first quarter of 2026, Eaton re-segmented certain business segments due to a reorganization of the Company's businesses. The new segment is Mobility, which includes the legacy Vehicle and eMobility segments. Historical segment information has been recast to reflect this change. Mobility The Mobility segment designs, manufactures, markets, and supplies a broad portfolio of mechanical, electrical, and electronic systems that improve emissions, fuel economy, power management, performance, and safety across on road and off road vehicles. The Mobility segment serves global OEMs and aftermarket customers with solutions spanning internal combustion, hybrid, and electrified powertrains, including transmissions and transmission components, clutches, differentials, hybrid systems, engine valves, fuel and vapor components, as well as high voltage inverters and converters, power electronics, circuit protection, vehicle controls, and power distribution systems. The principal markets for the Mobility segment are OEM and aftermarket customers of heavy-, medium-, and light duty trucks, SUVs, CUVs, passenger cars, construction, agricultural, material handling, and mining equipment. Planned Separation of Mobility Business On January 26, 2026, Eaton announced its intention to separate its Mobility business segment from the rest of Eaton via a spin-off. On June 10, 2026, Eaton entered into definitive agreements with Dana Incorporated (Dana), and on October 16, 2026, entered into a supplemental letter agreement, whereby Eaton will separate the Mobility business and combine it with Dana in a Reverse Morris Trust (RMT) transaction (collectively referred to as the Transaction). As part of the Transaction, Eaton will distribute the Mobility business (other than certain assets and liabilities that will be sold directly to Dana in a concurrent asset sale) to Eaton shareholders through an exchange offer (split-off), in which Eaton shareholders will have the opportunity to tender their Eaton shares in exchange for shares of Mobility (USA) Corporation, a wholly owned subsidiary of Eaton (SpinCo), followed, if necessary, by a clean-up pro rata distribution. Immediately thereafter, a two-step merger will take place. In the first merger step, a direct, wholly owned corporation subsidiary of SpinCo will merge with and into Dana, with Dana surviving such first merger as a direct, wholly owned subsidiary of SpinCo. Then immediately following the first merger, Dana will merge with and into a direct, wholly owned limited liability company subsidiary of SpinCo (the Second Merger Sub), with the Second Merger Sub surviving as a direct, wholly owned subsidiary of SpinCo. Following completion of the Transaction, Eaton shareholders are expected to own 50.1% of the combined company's outstanding shares. Certain of Eaton's subsidiaries will also receive a cash distribution of approximately $1.1 billion prior to completion of the Transaction, subject to a customary cash and indebtedness adjustment and tax payments to various global jurisdictions and transaction related adjustments. Eaton expects to use the cash distribution consistent with its capital allocation framework, including repayment of outstanding indebtedness. The RMT transaction is intended to be tax-free for U.S. federal income tax purposes to Eaton and Eaton s shareholders and is expected to close in the first four months of 2027, subject to Dana stockholder approval, regulatory approvals, and customary closing conditions. Until the Transaction closes, the Mobility business segment will continue to operate as a business segment of Eaton and its financial results reported in Eaton s continuing operations. In the event the Transaction is not consummated, Eaton intends to separate its Mobility business segment in a spin-off. Note 4.
