 Document EXHIBIT 99.1 Republic Services, Inc. Reports Third Quarter 2026 Results Reported Earnings Per Share of $1.93 and Adjusted Earnings Per Share of $1.94 Generated Year-to-Date Cash Flow from Operations of $3.52 Billion and Adjusted Free Cash Flow of $2.28 Billion Invested Approximately $1.1 Billion in Value-Creating Acquisitions Year-to-Date Updated Full-Year Financial Guidance Board Authorized $5 Billion for Share Repurchases Great Place to Work Certification for the 10 th Consecutive Year PHOENIX (October 29, 2026) Republic Services, Inc. (NYSE RSG) today reported net income of $592 million, or $1.93 per diluted share, for the three months ended September 30, 2026, versus $550 million, or $1.76 per diluted share, for the comparable 2025 period. Excluding certain expenses and other items, on an adjusted basis, net income for the three months ended September 30, 2026, was $595 million, or $1.94 per diluted share, versus $594 million, or $1.90 per diluted share, for the comparable 2025 period. We delivered strong third quarter results that underscored the resilience of our business model and the effectiveness of our strategy, said Jon Vander Ark, president and chief executive officer. Positive growth trends across the business and improved performance in Environmental Solutions reinforce our confidence in the opportunities ahead. We remain focused on disciplined pricing, effective cost management and strategic investments that are designed to strengthen our competitive position and create long-term value for our customers and shareholders. 1 Third-Quarter and Year-to-Date 2026 Highlights Total revenue growth of 8.2 percent includes 4.8 percent organic growth from our recycling and waste business, 2.1 percent growth from our environmental solutions business, and 1.3 percent growth from acquisitions. Core price on total revenue increased revenue by 5.3 percent. Core price on related business revenue increased revenue by 6.3 percent, which consisted of 7.6 percent in the open market and 4.1 percent in the restricted portion of the business. Revenue growth from average yield on total revenue was 2.9 percent, and volume decreased revenue by 0.6 percent. Revenue growth from average yield on related business revenue was 3.5 percent, and volume decreased related business revenue by 0.7 percent. Net income was $592 million, or a margin of 13.0 percent. EPS was $1.93 per share, an increase of 9.7 percent over the prior year. Adjusted EPS, a non-GAAP measure, was $1.94 per share, an increase of 2.1 percent over the prior year. Adjusted EBITDA, a non-GAAP measure, was $1.45 billion. Adjusted EBITDA margin, a non-GAAP measure, was 31.8 percent of revenue. Year-to-date cash invested in acquisitions was $1.12 billion. Year-to-date cash returned to shareholders was $1.43 billion, which included $848 million of share repurchases and $577 million of dividends paid. The Company's average recycled commodity price per ton sold at its recycling centers during the third quarter was $142. This represents an increase of $16 per ton over the prior year.
