THIRD-QUARTER 2026 FINANCIAL SUMMARY Total revenue in the third quarter increased 3% to $4.1 billion compared to the prior-year period, reflecting 6% organic revenue growth, partially offset by a 3% unfavorable impact primarily from divestitures. Risk Capital revenue increased $157 million, or 6%, to $2.7 billion and Human Capital revenue decreased $54 million, or 4%, to $1.4 billion. Total operating expenses in the third quarter increased 3% to $3.3 billion compared to the prior-year period due primarily to an increase in expense associated with 6% organic revenue growth, investments in long-term growth, and an increase in Accelerating Aon United Program expenses, partially offset by lower expenses associated with the sale of the NFP Wealth business and $20 million of net restructuring savings. Risk Capital operating expenses increased $134 million, or 7%, and Human Capital operating expenses decreased $68 million, or 6%. Foreign currency translation had a $0.02 favorable impact on both diluted EPS and adjusted EPS in the third quarter. If currency were to remain stable at today s rates, the Company would expect a de minimis impact on adjusted EPS in the fourth quarter of 2026 and a favorable impact on adjusted EPS of approximately $0.42 per share for the full year 2026. Effective tax rate was 20.6% in the third quarter compared to 21.3% in the prior-year period. After adjusting to exclude the applicable tax impact associated with certain non-GAAP adjustments, the adjusted effective tax rate for the third quarter of 2026 was 20.2% compared to 19.2% in the prior-year period. The primary drivers of the change in the effective tax rate were changes to the geographical distribution of income and a greater net unfavorable impact from discrete items. The primary drivers of the change in the adjusted effective tax rate were changes to the geographical distribution of income and a net unfavorable impact from discrete items. Weighted average diluted shares outstanding decreased to 213.0 million in the third quarter compared to 216.7 million in the prior-year period. The Company did not repurchase any shares in the third quarter. As of September 30, 2026, the Company had approximately $7.7 billion of remaining authorization under its share repurchase program. YEAR-TO-DATE 2026 CASH FLOW SUMMARY Cash flows provided by operations for the first nine months of 2026 increased $93 million, or 4%, to $2.2 billion compared to the prior-year period, as strong adjusted operating income growth offset the impact of working capital and USI-related transaction costs. 2 Free cash flow , defined as cash flow from operations less capital expenditures, increased 3%, to $1.9 billion for the first nine months of 2026 compared to the prior-year period, reflecting an increase in cash flows provided by operations, partially offset by a $45 million increase in capital expenditures. Free cash flow is cash flows from operating activity less capital expenditures. The adjusted effective tax rate excludes the applicable tax impact associated with adjustments previously described, generally at the estimated annual effective tax rate or jurisdictional rate, where appropriate. Leverage ratio is calculated by dividing total debt by trailing 12-month EBITDA. EBITDA is net income minus the impact of interest, taxes, depreciation and amortization, and the non-recurring gain from the sale of the substantial majority of NFP's Wealth business.
