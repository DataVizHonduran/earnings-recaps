Reports Highest Q3 Revenue on Record for Q3 2026 Double-digit revenue and net income growth CHICAGO, October 21, 2026 - CME Group Inc. (NASDAQ CME) today reported financial results for the third quarter of 2026. The company reported revenue of $1.7 billion and operating income of $1.1 billion for the third quarter of 2026. Net income was $1.0 billion and diluted earnings per common share were $2.89. On an adjusted basis, operating income was $1.2 billion, net income was $1.1 billion and diluted earnings per common share were $3.02. Financial results presented on an adjusted basis for the third quarter of 2026 and 2025 exclude certain items, which are detailed in the reconciliation of non-GAAP results. 1 As uncertainty intensified in Q3, clients turned to CME Group in record numbers to manage risk and capitalize on opportunities, said CME Group Chairman and Chief Executive Officer Terry Duffy. Our average daily volume reached 29.4 million contracts, the highest Q3 ADV on record. With double-digit volume growth across all six asset classes, Q3 was our second highest quarter ever for revenue and adjusted net income. We launched several innovative new products and generated a new high of $100 billion in average daily capital efficiencies. We also made strong progress in our cloud migration, making CME Group the only exchange to offer clients the choice of both co-location and cloud-managed services to expand access to our markets for years to come. Q3 2026 average daily volume (ADV) climbed to a third-quarter record of 29.4 million contracts, and non-U.S. ADV reached a third-quarter high of 9 million contracts. Clearing and tr ansaction fees revenue for third-quarter 2026 totaled $1.4 billion. The total average rate per contract was $0.679. Market data revenue totaled a record $243 million for third-quarter 2026, an increase of 20%. 1. A reconciliation of the non-GAAP financial results mentioned to the respective GAAP figures can be found within the Reconciliation of Adjusted Operating Income and Adjusted Net Income and Adjusted Earnings per Common Share charts at the end of the financial statements. - more - Page 2 As of September 30, 2026, th e company had $2.4 billion in cash (including $225 million deposited with Fixed Income Clearing Corporation, which is included in other current assets) and $3.4 billion of debt. The company returned nearly $1 billion to shareholders during the quarter, with $465 million in regular quarterly dividends and $524 million in share repurchases . As the world's leading derivatives marketplace, CME Group ( www.cmegroup.com ) enables clients to trade futures, options, cash and OTC markets, optimize portfolios, and analyze data empowering market participants worldwide to efficiently manage risk and capture opportunities. CME Group exchanges offer the widest range of global benchmark products across all major asset classes based on interest rates , equity indexes , foreign exchange , cryptocurrencies , energy , agricultural products and metals . The company offers futures and options on futures trading through the CME Globex platform, fixed income trading via BrokerTec and foreign exchange trading on the EBS platform. In addition, it operates one of the world's leading central counterparty clearing providers, CME Clearing.
