( October 20, 2026 ) Capital One Financial Corporation (NYSE COF) today announced net income for the third quarter of 2026 of $3.5 billion, or $5.61 per diluted common share, compared with net income of $3.0 billion, or $4.73 per diluted common share in the second quarter of 2026, and with net income of $3.2 billion, or $4.83 per diluted common share in the third quarter of 2025. Adjusted net income (1) for the third quarter of 2026 was $6.69 per diluted common share. The Discover integration continues to go well and we re making good progress on delivering the expected synergies said Richard D. Fairbank, Founder, Chairman, and Chief Executive Officer. We continue to invest to deliver sustained growth and value creation over the long-term. About Capital One Capital One Financial Corporation (NYSE COF) is a leading technology-based financial services company with $480.7 billion in deposits and $672.9 billion in total assets as of September 30, 2026. Headquartered in McLean, Virginia, the company operates as a premier global payments provider and diversified financial institution, delivering a broad suite of products and consumer lifestyle and shopping experiences through its Credit Card, Consumer Banking including its Global Payment Network, and Commercial Banking lines of business.
