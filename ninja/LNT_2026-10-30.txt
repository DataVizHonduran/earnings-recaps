For the first nine months of 2026, Alliant Energy reported EPS of $3.04, a 17% increase from $2.59 for the same period in 2025. Ongoing EPS for the first nine months of 2026 was $2.99, a 14% increase from $2.62 for the same period in 2025. Based on results for the first nine months of 2026, ongoing earnings per share guidance was narrowed to $3.41 - $3.46. Alliant Energy s utilities Interstate Power and Light Company (IPL) and Wisconsin Power and Light Company (WPL) have 3.4 gigawatts of contracted demand from data centers. Alliant Energy expects peak demand to increase by approximately 60% by 2031, an industry-leading growth rate. The energy resources needed to serve this expected load while maintaining safe and reliable service for all customers have increased the Company s forecasted capital expenditures for 2027 through 2030 to $15.9 billion. The Company announced 2027 earnings guidance of $3.63 to $3.73 per share. Also, Alliant Energy has increased its 2027 expected annual common stock dividend target to $2.25 per share. We delivered another solid quarter of operating performance and our full-year earnings expectation has been narrowed to within the top half of the original 2026 ongoing EPS guidance range, said Lisa Barton, Alliant Energy President and CEO. We are driving meaningful economic development for the communities we are privileged to serve and converting it into durable investment growth, while earning our authorized returns, strengthening reliability, and maintaining affordability. We are extending IPL s retail electric base rate stability plan by one additional year, providing Iowa retail electric customers with predictability through the end of 2030. About Alliant Energy Corporation Alliant Energy is the parent company of two public utility companies - Interstate Power and Light Company and Wisconsin Power and Light Company - and of Alliant Energy Finance, LLC, the parent company of Alliant Energy s non-utility operations. Alliant Energy, whose core purpose is to serve customers and build stronger communities, is an energy-services provider with utility subsidiaries serving approxim ately 1,015,000 electric and 435,000 na tural gas customers. Providing its customers in the Midwest with regulated electricity and natural gas service is the Company s primary focus. Alliant Energy, headquartered in Madison, Wisconsin, is a component of the S P 500 and is traded on the Nasdaq Global Select Market under the symbol LNT.
