SUMMARY Consolidated Net sales increased 7.9% to $6.86 billion in the quarter Net sales from stores in the Paint Stores Group open more than twelve calendar months increased 3.9% in the quarter Diluted net income per share increased 0.9% to $3.38 per share in the quarter compared to $3.35 per share in the third quarter of 2025 Adjusted diluted net income per share increased 1.1% to $3.63 per share in the quarter compared to $3.59 per share in the third quarter of 2025 Net income decreased 1.5% in the quarter to $821.0 million, or 12.0% of Net sales Earnings Before Interest, Taxes, Depreciation and Amortization (EBITDA) in the quarter increased 3.4% to $1.39 billion, or 20.3% of Net sales Adjusted EBITDA in the quarter increased 3.3% to $1.40 billion, or 20.5% of Net sales Revising full year 2026 diluted net income per share guidance in the range of $10.92 to $11.22 per share, including Valspar acquisition-related amortization expense of $0.82 per share and severance and other restructuring expenses of $0.11 per share Narrowing full year 2026 adjusted diluted net income per share guidance in the range of $11.85 to $12.15 per share CEO REMARKS Sherwin-Williams third quarter performance demonstrated the resilience of our Company and the strength of our strategy in a challenging demand environment. Despite muted underlying market volumes, we continued to win new customers, increase share-of-wallet and deliver sales growth across all three reportable segments, at or above our guidance, supported by the differentiated value and productivity our solutions deliver to customers. At the same time, we took disciplined pricing and cost actions to address inflationary pressures, thoughtfully timed with our customers in mind, said Chair, President and Chief Executive Officer, Heidi G. Petz. Gross margin was primarily impacted in the quarter by higher raw material and supply chain related costs and by mix, including dilution from Suvinil and higher growth in lower margin reportable segments. Excluding the dilutive impact of the Suvinil acquisition, our year-to-date gross margin is flat compared to the prior year. SG A as a percent of sales decreased in the quarter, as we continue to identify and implement efficiencies throughout our Company. Adjusted EBITDA and adjusted diluted earnings per share both grew over last year s third quarter levels. We returned $749.0 million to shareholders in the quarter through dividends and share repurchases. Our team remains focused on serving our customers and controlling what we can control to continue top and bottom-line growth. 1 THIRD QUARTER CONSOLIDATED RESULTS (in millions, except per share data) Three Months Ended September 30, 2026 2025 $ Change % Change Net sales $ 6,860.0 $ 6,358.2 $ 501.8 7.9 % Income before income taxes $ 1,063.5 $ 1,060.5 $ 3.0 0.3 % Percent to Net sales 15.5% 16.7% Net income per share - diluted $ 3.38 $ 3.35 $ 0.03 0.9 % Adjusted net income per share - diluted $ 3.63 $ 3.59 $ 0.04 1.1 % Consolidated Net sales increased primarily due to higher Net sales in all reportable segments, inclusive of the October 2025 acquisition of Suvinil, and selling price increases, which impacted Net sales by a mid-single digit percentage. The Company manufactures products under well-known brands such as Sherwin-Williams , Valspar , HGTV HOME by Sherwin-Williams, Dutch Boy , Krylon , Minwax , Thompson s WaterSeal , Cabot , Suvinil and many more. With global headquarters in Cleveland, Ohio, Sherwin-Williams branded products are sold exclusively through a chain of more than 5,400 Company-operated stores and branches, while the Company s other brands are sold through leading mass merchandisers, home centers, independent paint dealers, hardware stores, automotive retailers and industrial distributors. The Sherwin-Williams Performance Coatings Group supplies a broad range of highly-engineered solutions for the construction, industrial, packaging and transportation markets in more than 120 countries around the world. Sherwin-Williams shares are traded on the New York Stock Exchange (symbol SHW).
