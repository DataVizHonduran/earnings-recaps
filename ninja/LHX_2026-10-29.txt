 Document Exhibit 99.1 L3Harris Delivers Record Backlog and Strong Third-Quarter Results Broad-based growth with robust earnings and cash flow increases full-year guidance Orders of $19.3 billion drove record backlog of $53.2 billion book-to-bill of 3.2x Revenue of $6.1 billion, up 7%, and up 9% on an organic 1 basis Operating income margin of 13.0%, up 200 bps vs. prior year segment operating margin 2 of 15.9% Diluted EPS of $3.28, up 33% year-over-year Operating cash flow of $1.4 billion free cash flow 3 of $1.3 billion, up 195% compared to prior year Guidance Update Increases bottom end of the revenue guidance range to $23.5 billion to $23.7 billion (prev. $23.2 billion to $23.7 billion), increases GAAP EPS range to $12.25 to $12.35 (prev. $11.80 to $12.00) MELBOURNE, Fla., October 29, 2026 — L3Harris Technologies (NYSE LHX) reported its third quarter 2026 results. Our team s exceptional execution delivered solid financial performance in the third quarter. We grew backlog to record levels, underscoring the consistently strong demand for our mission-critical capabilities. Our priority now is to convert that demand into results. We will continue to invest in production capacity and differentiated capabilities across missiles and missile solutions, space, resilient communications and multi-domain sensing, which will enable us to deliver for the warfighter, our customers and our shareholders, said Sam Mehta, President and CEO, L3Harris. Third-Quarter Summary Revenue of $6.1 billion increased $401 million compared to prior year, up 7%, and up 9% on an organic 1 basis, driven by double-digit growth in the Space Mission Systems and Missile Solutions segments. Operating income margin was 13.0%, up 200 bps compared to prior year, driven by higher revenues and continued management of corporate expenses. Segment operating margin 2 was 15.9%, flat to prior year. Third quarter GAAP EPS increased 33% to $3.28 driven by higher volumes, a lower level of corporate expense, lower interest expense and a lower effective tax rate. Third-Quarter Cash Generation Net cash provided by operating activities in the quarter totaled $1.4 billion. Capital expenditures were $179 million. Free cash flow 3 was $1.3 billion, up 195% over prior year. The Company ended the quarter with $11.0 billion in total debt, $2.5 billion in cash and equivalents and $550 million in short-term investments. PAGE 1 Backlog and Orders Orders received in the quarter totaled $19.3 billion, resulting in backlog of $53.2 billion. Book-to-bill ratio was 3.2x for the quarter. By segment, Space Mission Systems book-to-bill was 1.0x, Communications Spectrum Dominance was 1.9x and Missile Solutions was 11.8x. FY26 Guidance Management increases the bottom end of the revenue guidance range to $23.5 billion to $23.7 billion (prev. $23.2 billion to $23.7 billion), increases GAAP EPS range to $12.25 to $12.35 (prev. $11.80 to $12.00) and maintains FY26 Free Cash Flow guidance of $3 billion 3 .
