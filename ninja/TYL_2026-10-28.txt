 Document Tyler Technologies Reports Earnings for Third Quarter 2026 SaaS revenues grew 21.2% and free cash flow reached a new quarterly high PLANO, Texas October 28, 2026 Tyler Technologies, Inc. (NYSE TYL), a large-cap growth and value S P 500 company, today announced financial results for the third quarter ended September 30, 2026. We achieved solid third quarter results, with double-digit recurring revenue growth, robust margin expansion, strong bookings, and record quarterly free cash flow, said Lynn Moore, Tyler's executive chair, president and chief executive officer. Strong SaaS bookings reinforce healthy public sector demand, reflected in a growing sales pipeline and driven by continued public sector investment in digital modernization. Our government-grade AI strategy is delivering tangible progress, with approximately 300 deals containing AI sold across our client base to date. Combined with disciplined capital allocation, including our repurchase of approximately 8.5% of our outstanding shares year-to-date, these achievements position us well for a strong finish to 2026 and reinforce our confidence in our long-term growth initiatives that underpin our Tyler 2030 objectives, concluded Moore. Third Quarter 2026 Financial Highlights (all comparisons are to the third quarter of 2025) Revenues Total revenues were $647.6 million, up 8.7%. Recurring revenues were $568.8 million, up 11.0%, and comprised 87.8% of total revenues. Subscription revenues were $462.6 million, up 15.3%. SaaS revenues grew 21.2% to $242.2 million. Transaction revenues grew 9.5% to $220.4 million. Annualized recurring revenue (ARR) was $2.28 billion, up 11.0%. Earnings EBITDA GAAP operating income was $117.0 million, up 19.5%. Non-GAAP operating income was $182.1 million, up 14.8%. GAAP net income was $95.1 million, or $2.33 per diluted share, up 12.7%. Non-GAAP net income was $143.4 million, or $3.52 per diluted share, up 10.0%. Adjusted EBITDA was $192.7 million, up 13.4%. Cash Flow Cash flows from operations were $282.5 million, up 10.7%. Free cash flow was $277.0 million, up 11.9%. Tyler Technologies Reports Earnings for Third Quarter 2026 October 28, 2026 Page 2 Third quarter results reflect the strength and resilience of Tyler's recurring revenue model. Recurring revenues grew 11.0%, driven by SaaS revenue growth of 21.2%. Transaction-based revenues growth accelerated to 9.5% (14.2% excluding the impact of the Texas payments contract wind-down). Free cash flow reached a quarterly record of $277 million, up 11.9%, supported by earnings growth and improved working capital performance, said Brian Miller, Tyler's executive vice president and chief financial officer. During the quarter, we deployed capital across both strategic growth initiatives and shareholder returns, including the acquisition of CODY Systems and the repurchase of 1.1 million shares of our common stock. Supported by our strong balance sheet, robust cash generation, and significant remaining share repurchase authorization, we maintain financial flexibility to invest in growth initiatives, pursue disciplined acquisition opportunities, and continue building shareholder value, concluded Miller. Recent Business Highlights On July 30, we completed the acquisition of CODY Systems, an established public safety company that provides SaaS software and data management, integration, and exchange solutions for public safety, law enforcement, and other public sector entities, for approximately $27.7 million in cash. We repurchased 1,148,389 shares of our common stock during the quarter for approximately $379 million, with approximately $1.37 billion remaining under our previously announced repurchase authorizations. Non-GAAP Financial Measures Tyler Technologies has provided in this press release financial measures that have not been prepared in accordance with generally accepted accounting principles (GAAP) and are therefore considered non-GAAP financial measures. This information includes non-GAAP gross profit, non-GAAP gross margin, non-GAAP operating income, non-GAAP operating margin, non-GAAP net income, non-GAAP earnings per diluted share, EBITDA, adjusted EBITDA, free cash flow, and free cash flow margin. We use these non-GAAP financial measures internally in analyzing our financial results and believe they are useful to investors, as a supplement to GAAP measures, in evaluating Tyler s ongoing operational performance because they provide additional insight in comparing results from period to period while isolating the effects of some items that vary from period to period without correlation to core operating performance. Tyler believes the use of these non-GAAP financial measures provides an additional tool for investors to use in evaluating ongoing operating results and trends and in comparing our financial results with other companies in our industry, many of which present similar non-GAAP financial measures. EBITDA is net income before interest expense, other income, income taxes, depreciation, and amortization. Non-GAAP and adjusted financial measures discussed above exclude share-based compensation expense, employer portion of payroll taxes on employee stock transactions, expenses associated with amortization of intangibles arising from business combinations, acquisition-related expenses, restructuring costs and other, gain on remeasurement of equity investment, and non-recurring items in other income, net. Annualized recurring revenue (ARR) is calculated by annualizing the current quarter's recurring revenues from subscriptions and maintenance. Tyler currently uses a non-GAAP tax rate of 23.0%. This rate is based on Tyler's estimated annual GAAP income tax rate forecast, adjusted to account for items excluded from GAAP income in calculating Tyler's non-GAAP income, as well as significant non-recurring tax adjustments. The non-GAAP tax rate used in future periods will be reviewed periodically to determine whether it remains appropriate in consideration of factors including Tyler's periodic annual effective tax rate calculated in accordance with GAAP, changes resulting from tax legislation, changes in the geographic mix of revenues and expenses, and other factors Tyler Technologies Reports Earnings for Third Quarter 2026 October 28, 2026 Page 4 deemed significant. Due to differences in tax treatment of items excluded from non-GAAP earnings, as well as the methodology applied to Tyler's estimated annual tax rate as described above, the estimated tax rate on non-GAAP income may differ from the GAAP tax rate and from Tyler's actual tax liabilities. Non-GAAP financial measures should be considered in addition to, and not as a substitute for, or superior to, financial information prepared in accordance with GAAP. The non-GAAP measures used by Tyler Technologies may be different from non-GAAP measures used by other companies.
