 Eversource Energy Reports Third Quarter 2026
Results 

 

 HARTFORD, Conn. and BOSTON, Mass. (November 4, 2026) &ndash;
 Eversource Energy (&ldquo;Eversource&rdquo; or the &ldquo;Company&rdquo;) (NYSE: ES) today reported GAAP and non-GAAP earnings of
$346.0 million, or $0.92 per share, for the third quarter of 2026, compared with GAAP earnings of $367.5 million, or $0.99 per share and
non-GAAP recurring earnings of $442.5 million 1 , or $1.19 per share 1 , in the third quarter of 2025. There were no
non-GAAP adjustments in the third quarter of 2026. 

 

 For the first nine months of 2026, Eversource reported GAAP earnings
of $1.01 billion, or $2.67 per share, compared with $1.27 billion, or $3.44 per share, for the first nine months of 2025. Non-GAAP recurring
earnings totaled $1.33 billion 1 , or $3.52 per share 1 , in the first nine months of 2026, compared with $1.35 billion 1 ,
or $3.64 per share 1 , in the first nine months of 2025. 

 

 GAAP results for the first nine months of 2026 include a non-cash,
after-tax charge of $111.4 million, or $0.30 per share, related to the sale of Aquarion Water Company on June 30, 2026, an after-tax
charge of $164.0 million, or $0.43 per share, related to an increase in Eversource's expected future payments related to its offshore
wind divestiture as part of the September 30, 2024, sale of South Fork Wind and Revolution Wind, and an after-tax charge of $43.9
million, or $0.12 per share, related to estimated refunds associated with the Federal Energy Regulatory Commission (FERC) decision of
March 19, 2026, that reduced the return on equity (ROE) rate for New England transmission owners from 10.57% to 9.57%. GAAP results
for the first nine months of 2025 include an aggregate net after-tax loss of $75.0 million, or $0.20 per share, related to an increase
in Eversource&rsquo;s offshore wind contingent liability, net of tax benefits associated with the tax losses on the sale of the offshore
wind projects. These charges were excluded from non-GAAP results in their respective quarters. 

 

 &ldquo;We continued to execute well across our key strategic
initiatives and our employees continued to deliver safe and reliable energy service and advance the investments necessary to support
our customers and communities,&rdquo; said Joe Nolan, Eversource Chairman, President and Chief Executive Officer. &ldquo;We were
pleased to receive a constructive decision from PURA on previously deferred storm costs, which provides a clear path to
securitization that ultimately will benefit our Connecticut customers. This outcome demonstrates the importance of constructive,
transparent engagement with our regulators as we work collaboratively to balance customer affordability, system reliability, and the
investments needed to maintain and strengthen the energy infrastructure for our customers. Looking ahead, our focus remains on
disciplined execution and successfully delivering on the operational, regulatory, and financial objectives we established for
2026.&rdquo; 

 

 The Company is updating its 2026 non-GAAP recurring earnings guidance
to be in the range of $4.62 per share 1 and $4.70 per share 1 reflecting an increased midpoint of $4.66 per share 1 .
It also reaffirms its cumulative long-term earnings per share growth rate within the range of 5 to 7 percent through 2030, using the aforementioned
revised 2026 non-GAAP earnings guidance midpoint of $4.66 per share 1 as the base year. Eversource expects annual earnings growth
towards the upper half of its long-term guidance by 2028. 

 

 

 
 
 
 

 

 Electric Transmission 

 

 Eversource Energy&rsquo;s transmission segment, excluding the non-recurring
FERC ROE refund charge noted previously, earned $182.0 million in the third quarter of 2026 and $590.0 million 1 in the first
nine months of 2026, compared with earnings of $185.5 million in the third quarter of 2025 and $593.0 million in the first nine months
of 2025. Transmission segment results in both 2026 periods reflect the impact from the reduction to the allowed ROE mentioned previously,
as well as higher interest expense, partially offset by continued investment in Eversource&rsquo;s electric transmission system. 

 

 Electric Distribution 

 

 Eversource Energy&rsquo;s electric distribution segment earned $200.9
million in the third quarter of 2026 and $574.1 million in the first nine months of 2026, compared with earnings of $221.6 million in
the third quarter of 2025 and $571.6 million in the first nine months of 2025. Third quarter and year-to-date results were unfavorably
impacted by disallowed storm costs from PURA's July 29, 2026, final decision on CL&P's storm prudency proceeding, as well as higher
depreciation, interest expense and property taxes. These unfavorable earnings impacts were partially offset in both periods by higher
revenues from base distribution rate increases at Eversource&rsquo;s Massachusetts and New Hampshire electric businesses, and continued
investments in the Company&rsquo;s distribution system. 

 

 Natural Gas Distribution 

 

 Eversource Energy&rsquo;s natural gas distribution segment had a loss
of $43.4 million in the third quarter of 2026 and earned $281.6 million in the first nine months of 2026, compared with a loss of $16.8
million in the third quarter of 2025 and earnings of $236.9 million in the first nine months of 2025. Lower results in the third quarter
are due primarily to lower revenues at Eversource's Massachusetts natural gas businesses driven by a change in the seasonal rate design
that shifted the recovery of quarterly revenues, as well as higher depreciation expense. Improved results in the first nine months were
due primarily to base distribution rate increases at Eversource&rsquo;s Massachusetts and Connecticut natural gas businesses, effective
November 1, 2025, to recover continued investment in the Company&rsquo;s natural gas infrastructure, partially offset by higher depreciation
expense, operations and maintenance expense, property taxes, interest expense, a higher effective income tax rate, and the impact from
timing of certain cost recovery items. 

 

 Water Distribution 

 

 Lower year-to-date results for Eversource Energy&rsquo;s water distribution
segment are due to the absence of earnings in the third quarter of 2026 due to the sale of the Aquarion water business on June 30,
2026.The water segment, excluding the charge related to the sale of Aquarion noted previously, earned $17.9 million 1 in the
first nine months of 2026, compared with earnings of $36.8 million in the first nine months of 2025. 

 

 Eversource Energy Parent and Other Companies 

 

 Eversource Energy parent and other companies, excluding the increases
in the offshore wind contingent liability noted previously, earned $6.5 million in the third quarter of 2026 and had a loss of $137.8
million 1 in the first nine months of 2026, compared with earnings of $33.3 million 1 in the third quarter of 2025
and a loss of $92.2 million 1 in the first nine months of 2025. Lower results in 2026 for both periods are driven by a higher
effective income tax rate and higher interest expense.

 1 All per-share amounts in this news release are reported
on a diluted basis. The only common equity securities that are publicly traded are common shares of Eversource Energy. The earnings discussion
includes financial measures that are not recognized under generally accepted accounting principles (non-GAAP) referencing earnings and
EPS excluding a 2026 charge on the sale of the Aquarion water distribution business, 2026 and 2025 charges associated with increasing
the offshore wind contingent liability, and a 2026 charge related to the March 2026 FERC decision in the FERC base ROE complaints.
EPS by business is also a non-GAAP financial measure and is calculated by dividing the Net Income Attributable to Common Shareholders
of each business by the weighted average diluted Eversource Energy common shares outstanding for the period. The earnings and EPS of each
business do not represent a direct legal interest in the assets and liabilities of such business, but rather represent a direct interest
in Eversource Energy&rsquo;s assets and liabilities as a whole. 

 

 Eversource Energy uses these non-GAAP financial measures to evaluate
and provide details of earnings results by business and to more fully compare and explain results without including these items. This
information is among the primary indicators management uses as a basis for evaluating performance and planning and forecasting of future
periods. Management believes the charge on the sale of the Aquarion water distribution business, the charges associated with increasing
our offshore wind contingent liability, and the charge related to the March 2026 FERC decision in the FERC base ROE complaints are
not indicative of Eversource Energy's ongoing costs and performance. Management views these charges as not directly related to the ongoing
operations of the business and therefore not indicators of baseline operating performance. Due to the nature and significance of the effect
of these items on Net Income Attributable to Common Shareholders and EPS, management believes that the non-GAAP presentation is a more
meaningful representation of Eversource Energy's financial performance and provides additional and useful information to readers of this
news release in analyzing historical and future performance of the business. These non-GAAP financial measures should not be considered
as alternatives to reported Net Income Attributable to Common Shareholders or EPS determined in accordance with GAAP as indicators of
Eversource Energy's operating performance. 

 

 Eversource Energy does not provide a reconciliation of guidance
