Phillips 66 delivered strong operating performance in the third quarter and is executing on its financial and operating commitments, said Mark Lashier, chairman and CEO of Phillips 66. Our integrated portfolio, commercial capabilities and focus on continuous improvement enable the company to create value for stakeholders. The company remains steadfast in its disciplined approach to capital allocation. We target returning more than 50% of net operating cash flow to shareholders and delivering attractive, sustainable dividend growth across commodity cycles. Our recently announced second dividend increase of 2026 reinforces the company s commitment to prudent long-term returns of capital. Third-Quarter 2026 Financial Results Reported earnings were $5.6 billion for the third quarter of 2026 versus $3.8 billion in the second quarter of 2026. Third-quarter earnings included a pre-tax special item adjustment of ($29) million in the Refining segment. Adjusted earnings for the third quarter were $5.6 billion versus adjusted earnings of $3.8 billion in the second quarter of 2026. Midstream pre-tax income decreased compared with the second quarter mainly due to lower margins associated with gas transportation. Chemicals pre-tax income decreased compared with the second quarter mainly due to lower polyethylene margins, partially offset by higher polyethylene volumes and lower costs. Refining adjusted pre-tax income increased compared with the second quarter mainly due to higher realized margins and volumes. Margins were primarily driven by an increase in market crack spreads. Marketing and Specialties adjusted pre-tax income decreased compared with the second quarter mainly due to lower global marketing fuel margins, primarily driven by a rising price environment. Renewable Fuels pre-tax income decreased compared with the second quarter mainly due to lower margins, including inventory impacts, as well as higher costs. Corporate and Other pre-tax loss decreased compared with the second quarter mainly due to lower net interest expense, partially offset by higher employee-related costs. As of September 30, 2026, the company had $5.8 billion of cash and cash equivalents and $6.4 billion of committed capacity available under credit facilities. About Phillips 66 Phillips 66 (NYSE PSX) is a leading integrated downstream energy provider that manufactures, transports and markets products that drive the global economy. The company s portfolio includes Midstream, Chemicals, Refining, Marketing and Specialties, and Renewable Fuels businesses. Headquartered in Houston, Texas, Phillips 66 has employees around the globe who are committed to safely and reliably providing energy and improving lives while pursuing a lower-carbon future.
