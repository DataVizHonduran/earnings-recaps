(NYSE TJX), the leading off-price apparel and home fashions retailer in the U.S. and worldwide, today announced sales and operating results for the third quarter ended October 31, 2026. Net sales for the third quarter of Fiscal 2027 were $16.0 billion, an increase of 6% versus the third quarter of Fiscal 2026. Third quarter Fiscal 2027 consolidated comparable sales increased 4%. Net income for the third quarter of Fiscal 2027 was $1.5 billion. Third quarter Fiscal 2027 diluted earnings per share were $1.39, up 9% versus $1.28 in the third quarter of Fiscal 2026. Excluding a $.05 net benefit from tariff refunds, third quarter Fiscal 2027 adjusted diluted earnings per share were $1.34, up 5% versus the prior year. For the first nine months of Fiscal 2027, net sales were $45.5 billion, an increase of 7% versus the first nine months of Fiscal 2026. Consolidated comparable sales for the first nine months of Fiscal 2027 increased 4%. Net income for the first nine months of Fiscal 2027 was $4.4 billion. For the first nine months of Fiscal 2027, diluted earnings per share were $3.94, up 19% versus $3.30 in the first nine months of Fiscal 2026. Excluding a $.19 net benefit from tariff refunds, adjusted diluted earnings per share for the first nine months of Fiscal 2027 were $3.75, up 14% versus the prior year. CEO and President Comments Ernie Herrman, Chief Executive Officer and President of The TJX Companies, Inc., stated, I am very pleased with our consolidated third quarter results. Overall sales, profitability, and earnings per share all exceeded our expectations. Consolidated comparable sales grew 4%, driven by continued strength at HomeGoods, TJX Canada, and TJX International, and we saw improved sales at Marmaxx. Importantly, we expect further improvement at Marmaxx in the fourth quarter. We believe our above-plan results in the third quarter highlight the benefit of our global, diversified business. Looking ahead to the fourth quarter, while we believe the unseasonably warm weather in the U.S. and Canada has been holding back consumer demand in some categories, we are expecting sales momentum to accelerate throughout the holiday season. We are confident in our plans for the holiday selling season and availability of quality merchandise continues to be outstanding. All of our retail banners are very strongly positioned as gifting destinations and we are confident that consumers will be very pleased with our holiday gift giving assortment. Further, we will be flowing fresh merchandise and exciting gifts to our stores and online multiple times a week throughout the season. Longer term, we remain energized by the opportunities we see to further drive sales, grow our retail footprint, and keep capturing market share around the globe. Impact of Foreign Currency Changes in foreign currency exchange rates affect the translation of sales and earnings of the Company s international businesses into U.S. dollars for financial reporting purposes. In addition, ordinary course, inventory-related hedging instruments are marked to market at the end of each quarter. Changes in currency exchange rates can have a material effect on the magnitude of these translations and adjustments when there is significant volatility in currency exchange rates. Given the global operations of the Company, to facilitate comparability, the Company has provided sales growth and inventory on a constant currency basis, which assumes a constant exchange rate between periods for translation based on the rate in effect for the prior period. The movement in foreign currency exchange rates had a neutral impact on the Company s net sales growth in the third quarter of Fiscal 2027 versus the prior year. The overall net impact of foreign currency exchange rates had a $.01 negative impact on third quarter Fiscal 2027 diluted earnings per share. The movement in foreign currency exchange rates had a one percentage point positive impact on the Company s net sales growth in the first nine months of Fiscal 2027 versus the prior year. The overall net impact of foreign currency exchange rates had a $.01 positive impact on the first nine months of Fiscal 2027 diluted earnings per share. The foreign currency exchange rate impact to diluted earnings per share does not include the impact currency exchange rates have on various transactions, which the Company refers to as transactional foreign exchange.
