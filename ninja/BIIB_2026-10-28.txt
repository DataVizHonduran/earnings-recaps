The change in our GAAP effective tax rates was primarily driven by the non-deductible IPR D charge related to the RayThera stock acquisition. Financial Position and Cash Flows Third quarter 2026 net cash flow from operations was approximately $641 million. Capital expenditures were approximately $40 million, and free cash flow, a Non-GAAP financial measure defined as net cash flow from operations less capital expenditures, was approximately $600 million. As of September 30, 2026, Biogen had cash and cash equivalents totaling approximately $1.3 billion and approximately $7.7 billion in total debt, resulting in net debt of approximately $6.4 billion. For the third quarter of 2026, the Company s weighted average diluted shares were approximately 148 million and 149 million, used to calculate the Company's GAAP and Non-GAAP diluted EPS, respectively. All unvested equity-based awards are antidilutive for GAAP due to reporting a GAAP net loss for the third quarter 2026.
