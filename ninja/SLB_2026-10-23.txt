 Broad Growth Driven by Middle East Recovery, North America and Deep Water 
 SLB delivered solid third-quarter results with all regions growing sequentially — led by a gradual recovery in the Middle East, growth in North America land
and broadening international activity in deepwater offshore basins, said SLB Chief Executive Officer Olivier Le Peuch. In the Middle East, our
business grew 13% sequentially despite late-quarter disruptions. Outside the region, growth was supported by higher activity in U.S. land, highlighted by ChampionX achieving its highest quarterly revenue since the acquisition. Strong SLB OneSubsea activity further supported growth with a steady flow of new contract awards in the international markets which increased backlog and enhanced revenue visibility into 2027, Le Peuch said.
 Production Systems, Well Construction and Reservoir Performance Revenue Grew Sequentially 
 Production Systems, Well Construction and Reservoir Performance revenue grew sequentially, reflecting increased activity across the
 exploration-to-production life cycle. Production Systems was supported by
higher SLB OneSubsea activity and increased sales of production chemicals and artificial lift. This demonstrates the value of our production offering as customers increasingly prioritize production optimization, improved recovery and extended asset
life. Well Construction and Reservoir Performance benefited from the rebound in the Middle East and increased international offshore activity, mainly in
Europe & Africa, Latin America and Asia. Growth was driven by increased drilling fluids sales, higher drilling measurements revenue, and heightened evaluation and intervention activity as operators ramp up development efforts to restore
production and bring new capacity online. These results reflect broad-based sequential revenue growth across each of our geographical areas and Core
divisions as the upcycle gains momentum, Le Peuch said. Data Center Solutions Accelerates as an Independent Growth Driver 
 Our Data Center Solutions business continued to accelerate, with revenue increasing 23% sequentially, driven by growing demand, a broadening scope of services and
geographic expansion into Asia. AI is driving unprecedented investment in data center infrastructure, and customers are looking for partners who can deploy
quickly, reliably and at scale. Our modular approach is meeting this need today, and the previously announced acquisition of Kelvion will further strengthen our technology portfolio and advance our ability to deliver more integrated solutions for
customers. Overall, we continue to strengthen our position as an industrial technology partner to the data center industry and to expand our participation
in this rapidly growing market, Le Peuch said. 
 

 
 

 
 Broad International Expansion Strengthens the 2027 Growth Outlook 
 Our view of supportive market fundamentals remains firmly intact. Energy security and supply shocks continue to drive a quest for international supply
diversification, reserve replacement and sustained production — driving a distinct upcycle, despite near-term uncertainty in the Middle East. Our
international outlook continues to strengthen, driven by deepwater momentum across Latin America, Europe, Sub-Saharan Africa and Asia, underscored by recent SLB OneSubsea contract awards. Furthermore,
international diversification is accelerating as major U.S. shale operators expand abroad and partner with national oil companies across global basins. 
 Combined with significant activity increases in Venezuela and expanding momentum across our Core, Digital and Data Center Solutions businesses, we see a very
compelling outlook for 2027, with further upside as Middle East activity recovers, Le Peuch concluded. Other Events 
 During the quarter, SLB repurchased 12.9 million shares of its common stock for a total purchase price of $656 million. During the first nine months of 2026,
SLB repurchased a total of 34.0 million shares of its common stock for a total purchase price of $1.76 billion. On August 31, 2026, SLB signed an
agreement to acquire Kelvion, a global provider of thermal management and heat exchange technologies. The acquisition strengthens SLB s Data Center Solutions business with critical thermal management technologies and expands the
company s role in data center infrastructure, which is one of the world s fastest-growing industrial and technology markets. Under the terms of the agreement, SLB will acquire Kelvion from Apollo-managed funds — the majority owner
— and funds advised by Triton, which holds a minority interest, for approximately $3.4 billion in cash and will assume approximately $0.7 billion of debt. The transaction is subject to customary closing conditions and regulatory
approvals and is expected to close in the first half of 2027. On September 1, 2026, SLB completed its acquisition of S&P Global Energy s geoscience
and petroleum engineering software business portfolio. The acquisition enhances SLB s digital subsurface and planning portfolio, adding workflow-centric applications widely used by North American onshore and unconventional operators, and
strengthening its ability to help customers improve efficiency, accelerate decision making and unlock greater value from subsurface data. On October 22, 2026,
SLB s Board of Directors approved a quarterly cash dividend of $0.295 per share of outstanding common stock, payable on January 7, 2027, to stockholders of record on December 2, 2026.

 Latin America Revenue in Latin America of $1.75 billion increased 2%
sequentially, primarily driven by higher SLB OneSubsea sales and increased drilling activity in Guyana and Brazil. Growth was further supported by higher revenue from Asset Performance Solutions (APS) projects in Ecuador and stronger drilling
activity in Argentina. Year on year, revenue increased 16%, driven primarily by stronger offshore activity in Brazil, Guyana and Mexico. Higher revenue from APS
projects in Ecuador also contributed to the increase, as the prior-year quarter was affected by pipeline-related disruptions. Europe & Africa 
 Revenue in Europe & Africa of $2.44 billion increased 2% sequentially, driven by higher SLB OneSubsea revenue in Scandinavia and Angola and production and
recovery activity in Nigeria. These increases were partially offset by lower revenue in Libya and reduced SLB Capturi revenue as project milestones were completed. 
 Year on year, revenue decreased 1%, primarily reflecting lower activity across North Africa. This decline was partially offset by strong growth in Nigeria from higher
production systems sales and increased evaluation, intervention and stimulation activity as well as higher revenue in Kazakhstan, Azerbaijan and Turkmenistan. 
 

 
 

 
 Middle East & Asia 
 Revenue in the Middle East & Asia of $2.81 billion increased 9% sequentially, driven by 13% growth in the Middle East and a 2% increase in Asia. The Middle
East accounted for 67% of the area s revenue in the third quarter of 2026. Growth in the Middle East reflected the rebound in activity following operational
disruptions related to the regional conflict in the prior quarter. Activity improved in Iraq, Kuwait, Oman, Saudi Arabia, Qatar and the United Arab Emirates, although certain countries experienced renewed disruptions during the final weeks of the
quarter. In Asia, sequential revenue growth was supported by continued strength in offshore activity, particularly in India, East Asia and Indonesia. 
 Year on year, revenue decreased 7%, primarily reflecting a 12% decline in the Middle East due to conflict-related disruptions, partially offset by strong growth across
Asia, led by offshore markets. North America Revenue in North America
of $2.40 billion increased 7% sequentially, driven by higher sales of production chemicals, artificial lift and valves in U.S. land, along with a 23% increase in Data Center Solutions revenue. Growth was further supported by increased drilling
activity in Canada following the spring breakup in the prior quarter. These gains were partially offset by lower digital exploration sales in the Gulf of America. 
 Year on year, revenue increased 13%, primarily reflecting stronger unconventional activity in U.S. land, as well as continued momentum in Data Center Solutions, where
revenue grew 76%.

systems for complex deepwater developments worldwide. 
 
 
 
 
 SLB and Equinor have signed a multi-year agreement for advanced reservoir stimulation services across the Norwegian
Continental Shelf, including a major upgrade of the well stimulation vessel MV Island Captain. The agreement secures dedicated capacity to support development of tight offshore reservoirs and helps maximize recovery from future wells.
 
 
 
 
 
 SLB has been awarded a contract by Brunei Shell Petroleum (BSP) to support production restoration from shut-in wells across multiple offshore fields. The contract scope spans subsurface evaluation, well candidate selection, engineering and offshore execution. Project management, intervention services, monitoring,
metering and marine logistics are integrated within a single coordinated execution model designed to support efficient production restoration. 
 
 
 
 
 In Oman, Petroleum Development Oman awarded SLB a 5-year contract for the provision
of integrated fracturing services onshore Block-6 in both oil and gas fields. The scope includes the deployment of advanced stimulation, well intervention, fracturing automation and digital solutions
capabilities that will enhance productivity, improve reservoir contact and maximize asset performance. 
 
 
 
 
 In Kuwait, Kuwait Oil Company (KOC) awarded SLB a five-year reservoir characterization technology contract for specialized
services for unconventional reservoirs. The contract deploys SLB s Ora deep transient testing, OnWave autonomous logging
platform and Reveal surveillance solutions, together with different subsurface, conveyance and digital technologies, to support advanced formation evaluation across different reservoirs in
Kuwait. 
 
 
 
 
 In Pakistan, Oil & Gas Development Company (OGDC) awarded SLB a three-year contract for electric submersible pump
(ESP) and electric submersible progressing cavity pump (ESPCP) technologies, marking the third consecutive ESP award from OGDC. Through its existing ESP program with SLB, OGDC has delivered approximately 11,000 barrels per day of incremental oil
production and 5.3 million barrels cumulatively. The new contract expands deployments across OGDC concessions, supported by SLB s Avalon real-time surveillance to optimize
performance and reliability. 
 
 
 
 
 In Australia, GB Energy partnered with SLB to deliver an integrated package of critical midstream gas processing equipment
and valve systems for the country s first offshore gas storage reservoir initiative. SLB will supply key gas processing and flow control technologies, including flowline monoethylene glycol regeneration, triethylene glycol dehydration, mercury
and H2S removal, sand removal, and onshore manual and actuated valves for the gas processing facility and pipeline. The project supports south-east Australia s long-term gas supply strategy, with initial capacity expected to meet approximately
 one-third of Victoria s peak-day gas demand. The project will increase gas storage capacity in south-east Australia by more than 70% and strengthen energy security
and grid stability in the region. 
 

 
 

 
 Technology Highlights 
 Notable technology introductions and deployments in the quarter include the following: 
 
 
 
 
 In the United States, SLB deployed a sensor-based production chemistry optimization program across nearly 1,400 producing
wells for a major operator in the Permian Basin, replacing manual inventory management with real-time monitoring, machine learning anomaly detection, and AI-driven injection control. Pump monitor controller
units and tank-level monitor units enabled remote recalibration and centralized weekly operational reviews. The program increased on-target injection performance from 63% to 92%, reduced overtreatment from 17%
to 3%, and avoided $4.4 million in net costs in the first nine months. 
 
 
 
 
 Also in the United States, SLB and CNX deployed Invincible Trim 
extreme-wear-resistant choke trim in one of North America s most abrasive unconventional gas basins, improving production uptime during a critical phase of well life. Purpose-engineered for high-erosion environments, the technology reduced
shutdowns and interventions associated with conventional choke trim and supported continuous production through the most erosive period. The deployment helped optimize production, reduce maintenance requirements and improve well economics for CNX,
reinforcing SLB s ability to deliver advanced flow control solutions that enhance asset performance in challenging unconventional environments. 
 
 
 
 
 In Ecuador, SLB partnered with an onshore operator to reverse prolonged production decline at a mature onshore field
through an integrated recovery program combining waterflooding, infill drilling and the country s first chemical-enhanced oil recovery pilot. The program, supported by Agora edge AI
and IoT solutions, reduced intervention time by 98%, restored production to a peak of 86,000 barrels of oil per day, delivered 1.9 times the reserves projected at baseline and is projected to enable a 7% incremental recovery factor by 2035.
 
 
 
 
 
 Offshore Brazil, SLB delivered a 37-well subsea plug and abandonment campaign for a
deepwater operator, completing an average of 18 days per well against a 22-day target and a 33-day industry benchmark. Using an integrated portfolio of digital slickline
services — including the eFire-SL slickline-deployed electronic firing head, SIMplus retrievable bridge plugs, and SIM permanent plugs — alongside coiled tubing intervention, SLB reduced personnel on board by 19% compared to the benchmark and delivered $900,000 in avoided costs per well. The campaign,
completed over 20 months with more than 150 digital slickline runs and 130 coiled tubing runs, demonstrates how fit-for-purpose wellbore decommissioning technology can
compress both cost and cycle time at scale in deepwater environments. 
 
 
 
 
 In Kuwait, SLB used precise multilateral access to control water and unlock oil production for KOC. A mature three-lateral
producer experienced a water cut increase that rose to approximately 98%, threatening the well s economic viability. Using the access service of ExaCT electrical downhole CT control
system, SLB established fast, repeatable access to each lateral, identified the dominant water-producing branch, and executed a targeted water shutoff treatment. The intervention reduced water cut by approximately 20%, increased oil production by
about 505 barrels per day, and saved an estimated 96 hours compared with a conventional approach. 
 
 
 
 
 In Brunei, SLB and BSP successfully completed the first deployment of the Retina at-bit imaging system in Southeast Asia, delivering high-resolution subsurface data with no impact on drilling performance. Enabled by a fit-for-purpose bit design and integrated with Automated Lithology, the Retina system captured 100% of the interval in a single run across two sections while providing
detailed imaging of geological features. The deployment demonstrates how at-bit measurements can be converted into high-quality borehole images to strengthen formation evaluation, well planning and drilling
decisions. 
 
 
 
 
 Offshore Malaysia, an operator sought to maximize recovery from a thin, variably dipping sand reservoir while minimizing
drilling risk amid subsurface uncertainty. SLB deployed a fully autonomous geosteering system combining Subsurface Advisor intelligent subsurface drilling solution, Neuro autonomous geosteering and GeoSphere HD high-definition reservoir mapping-while-drilling service. The system executed four autonomous
trajectory changes with sub-20-second decision cycles, maintaining reservoir contact within 3 meters of the reservoir base throughout the lateral section, marking
Asia s first fully autonomous geosteering operation.

 10) 
 What are the revenue categories in the Digital Division that offer solutions to SLB s customers?
 Within Digital, revenue is generated from four key solutions — Platforms & Applications, Digital Operations,
Digital Exploration and Professional Services. Platforms & Applications includes SLB s cloud technologies such
as the Delfi and Lumi platforms, along with a suite of specialized, domain-focused applications such as Petrel and Techlog offered as SaaS subscription or perpetual licenses. These platforms and applications automate complex models to simulate the impact of reservoir development plans and aid in the planning of key
operations such as drilling, completion and production designs. Additionally, they unlock data and utilize AI and machine learning to reduce cycle time and improve efficiency of workflows to allow customers to make better, faster decisions to
improve their project economics and reservoir performance. Revenue is recurring (with exception of one-off 
license sales) underpinned by a substantial base of ARR from a globally installed base, complemented by customer adoption of new cloud-based capabilities and IoT-enabled solutions. 
 This category has best-in-class retention rates and limited churn. 
 Digital Operations combines the unique strengths of SLB s oilfield services with advanced digital technologies to deliver more reliable,
efficient and autonomous field operations. By integrating connected solutions with Performance Live digital service delivery centers, customers gain real-time monitoring, remote decision
making and automated execution across their workflows from autonomous drilling to automated well intervention, all while reducing costs and improving project economics. 
 Revenue is generated from the same customer base as the Core divisions and is, therefore, repeatable. Additionally, a portion of the revenue is recurring
in nature. To incentivize the Core divisions — Well Construction, Reservoir Performance and Production Systems — and Digital to develop
and promote this category, the resulting revenue is recognized in both the respective Core division as well as in the Digital Division. This effect is eliminated in consolidation. 
 Digital Exploration represents SLB s exploration data business. The exploration data library is a differentiated asset library of seismic
surveys and other subsurface data that customers rely on for better exploration and development decisions. These licensed datasets also support carbon storage design and monitoring. The library covers key exploration and producing basins worldwide
and datasets are refreshed and reprocessed to benefit from the latest imaging algorithms and AI technologies, enabled by high-performance cloud computing. Revenue is generated from one-time, non-transferable license sales and is therefore non-recurring in nature. 
 Professional Services includes consulting and other services required to support customers digital transformations. These services include
transition support from on-prem to cloud-based digital solutions, data clean-up and migration, workflow automation — including deployment of workflow solutions
built within SLB s global network of Innovation Factori workspaces — and training to further enable customers digital transformations. 
 Revenue in this category is largely project-based, and repetitive engagements with the same customers are common. These services generate pull-through
opportunities across other digital revenue streams.

 The Data Center Solutions business designs and manufactures critical infrastructure components — such as modular data center enclosures, cooling
systems and other hardware — for hyperscalers and enterprises. By leveraging scalable, standardized production with rapid lead times, and rigorous quality assurance, SLB provides configurable solutions that balance cost efficiency, reliability
and customization to meet accelerating data center demand. AI-driven data demand is fueling rapid growth, and this business is going to be a
material and meaningful contributor to SLB s portfolio in the future. This business is reported in the All Other category.
