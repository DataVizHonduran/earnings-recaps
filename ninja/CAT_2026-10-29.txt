Third-quarter 2026 sales and revenues increased 17% to $20.7 billion Third-quarter 2026 profit per share of $7.13 adjusted profit per share of $7.20 Deployed $2.0 billion of cash for share repurchases and dividends in the third quarter IRVING, Texas, Oct. 29, 2026 Caterpillar Inc. (NYSE CAT) announced third-quarter 2026 results. Our third-quarter results reflect the durability of our end markets, with strong order rates and a record backlog, said Caterpillar Chairman and CEO Joe Creed. Our team continues to execute our strategic priorities in a dynamic environment, positioning us for a strong year while delivering long-term value for our customers and shareholders. Sales and revenues for the third quarter of 2026 were $20.7 billion, a 17% increase compared with $17.6 billion in the third quarter of 2025. The increase was primarily due to higher sales volume of $2.2 billion and favorable price realization of $663 million. Operating profit margin was 20.4% for the third quarter of 2026, compared with 17.3% for the third quarter of 2025. Adjusted operating profit margin was 20.6% for the third quarter of 2026, compared with 17.5% for the third quarter of 2025. Third-quarter 2026 profit per share was $7.13, compared with third-quarter 2025 profit per share of $4.88. Adjusted profit per share in the third quarter of 2026 was $7.20, compared with third-quarter 2025 adjusted profit per share of $4.95. For the third quarter of 2026 and 2025, adjusted operating profit margin and adjusted profit per share excluded restructuring costs. For the third quarter of 2026, enterprise operating cash flow was $4.4 billion, and the company ended the third quarter with $7.9 billion of enterprise cash. In the quarter, the company deployed $1.2 billion of cash for repurchases of Caterpillar common stock and $0.7 billion of cash for dividends.
