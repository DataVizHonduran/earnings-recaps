 We faced severe weather throughout the third quarter which highlighted PSEG s best-in-class storm response, our commitment to service excellence and the customer value of our investments in energy infrastructure and resilience. PSE&G successfully
managed some of the most challenging storm restorations in our history this past July and again in September, when a nor easter brought severe wind gusts and heavy rain to our service area. Our utility crews safely restored service to nearly
all affected customers in New Jersey within a 24-hour period. At PSEG Nuclear, we supplied the
regional grid with 8.2 TWh of reliable, carbon-free baseload energy during the third quarter, operating at a capacity factor of 95.5%. Salem unit #1 recently entered its fall refueling following a 491-day 
breaker to breaker run matching Salem unit #2 s back-to-back, breaker to breaker run posted earlier this year. PSEG Power continues to generate stable cash
flows that help fund our regulated investments while pursuing high-value, regional growth opportunities. LaRossa added, We continue to
execute our long-term strategy to grow PSEG s non-GAAP Operating Earnings by a compound annual rate of 6% to 8% through 2030 while prioritizing customer affordability and maintaining a solid financial
position. Our financial strength enables us to fund our five-year capital investment plan totaling $24 billion to $28 billion without the need to issue new equity or sell assets and provides the opportunity for consistent and sustainable
dividend growth. PSEG Results by Segment (unaudited)

 About PSEG Public
Service Enterprise Group (PSEG) (NYSE: PEG) is a predominantly regulated infrastructure company operating New Jersey s largest transmission and distribution utility, serving approximately 2.4 million electric and 1.9 million natural
gas customers. PSEG also owns an independent fleet of 3,758 MW of carbon-free, baseload nuclear power generating units in NJ and PA. PSEG is a member of the S&P 500 Index and has been named to the Dow Jones Best in Class North America Index
for 18 consecutive years. PSEG s businesses include Public Service Electric and Gas Co. (PSE&G), PSEG Power and PSEG Long Island. 
 ( https://corporate.pseg.com ) Non-GAAP Financial Measures Management uses non-GAAP Operating Earnings
in its internal analysis, and in communications with investors and analysts, as a consistent measure for comparing PSEG s financial performance to previous financial results. Operating Earnings is a
 non-GAAP financial measure that differs from Net Income/(Loss). Non-GAAP Operating Earnings exclude the impact of gains (losses) associated with the Nuclear
Decommissioning Trust (NDT), Mark-to-Market (MTM) accounting and other material infrequent items.
