 Document Exhibit 99.1 Media Relations Consolidated Edison, Inc. 212 460 4111 (24 hours) 4 Irving Place New York, NY 10003 www.conEdison.com FOR IMMEDIATE RELEASE Contact Allan Drury November 5, 2026 212-460-4111 CON EDISON REPORTS 2026 THIRD QUARTER EARNINGS NEW YORK - C onsolidated Edison, Inc. (Con Edison) (NYSE ED) today reported 2026 third quarter net income of $766 million or $2.07 a share compared with $688 million or $1.91 a share in the 2025 third quarter. Adjusted earnings (non-GAAP) were $766 million or $2.07 a share in the 2026 period compared with $686 million or $1.90 a share in the 2025 period. Adjusted earnings and adjusted earnings per share in the 2025 period exclude accretion of the basis difference of Con Edison s equity interest in Mountain Valley Pipeline, LLC (MVP). For the first nine months of 2026, net income was $1,998 million or $5.44 a share compared with $1,726 million or $4.84 a share in the first nine months of 2025. Adjusted earnings were $1,864 million or $5.08 a share in the 2026 period compared with $1,718 million or $4.82 a share in the 2025 period. Adjusted earnings and adjusted earnings per share in the 2026 period exclude transaction costs associated with the strategic alternatives review of Con Edison s equity interests in MVP and Honeoye Storage Corporation (Honeoye), the gain on the sale of Con Edison s equity interest in MVP and the effects of hypothetical liquidation at book value (HLBV) accounting for tax equity investments. Adjusted earnings and adjusted earnings per share in the 2026 and 2025 periods exclude accretion of the basis difference of Con Edison s equity interest in MVP. Adjusted earnings and adjusted earnings per share in the 2025 period exclude adjustments to the gain and other impacts related to the sale of all of the stock of Con Edison s former subsidiary, Con Edison Clean Energy Businesses, Inc. (the Clean Energy Businesses), in 2023. Con Edison is a durable, long-term investment, supported by consistent performance, financial strength, and our ability to navigate an evolving landscape, said Tim Cawley, Chairman and CEO of Con Edison. We re at the center of one of the nation s largest electrification efforts, serving a region with unique needs, complex infrastructure, and vital national assets. We remain focused on delivering world-class reliability to our customers while building the capacity and resilience New York needs to grow. We have a responsibility to protect our equipment and customers from increasingly extreme weather and plan to invest approximately $797 million in climate resilience from 2026 through 2030, Cawley added. Our robust capital investments modernize our system for customers changing energy needs while balancing customer costs and supporting stable returns for investors. We see a bright future as we expand our earnings base with strategic upgrades to our energy delivery systems that help us maintain our industry-leading reliability and provide attractive, steady earnings opportunities, said Kirk Andrews, Senior Vice President and CFO.
