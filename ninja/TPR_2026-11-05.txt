 Tapestry, Inc. Strategic Highlights 
 &#xa0;
 
 Tapestry advanced its Amplify growth strategy , which is focused on four key pillars that underpin durable growth:
 &#xa0;
 
 
 &#x25cf;
 
 
 Build Emotional Connection with Consumers 
 
 &#xa0;
 
 
 &#x25cf;
 
 
 Fuel Fashion Innovation and Product Excellence 
 
 &#xa0;
 
 
 &#x25cf;
 
 
 Deliver Compelling Experiences to Drive Global Growth 
 
 &#xa0;
 
 
 &#x25cf;
 
 
 Ignite the Power of our People 
 
 &#xa0;
 
 This strategy is driving the Company&#x2019;s results and deepening the competitive advantages that support long-term growth.
 &#xa0;
 
 Highlights from the fiscal first quarter of 2027 included:
 &#xa0;
 
 
 &#x25cf;
 
 
 Drove strong new customer acquisition globally , welcoming 2.6 million new consumers in the quarter, of which approximately 35% were Gen Z; at the same time, demand from existing customers increased, demonstrating the Company&#x2019;s ability to create lasting consumer relationships;
 
 &#xa0; 
 3 
 
 &#xa0; 
 
 &#x25cf;
 
 
 Achieved strong growth in the core leathergoods offering , led by handbag revenue gains at Coach, where handbag AUR increased at a low-double-digit percentage rate and units rose low-single digits, underscoring the brand's desirability, innovation, and value proposition;
 
 &#xa0;
 
 
 &#x25cf;
 
 
 Delivered broad-based growth across regions, gaining market share, and expanding the overall category . On a pro forma constant currency basis in the first quarter, North America revenue grew 4%, Europe revenue increased 22%, and APAC revenue rose 28%, which included Greater China growth of 39%. Coach delivered 16% growth, including 7% growth in North America and 30% growth internationally; 
 
 &#xa0;
 
 
 &#x25cf;
 
 
 Drove 9% Direct-to-Consumer revenue growth on a pro forma basis, exceeding expectations , &#xa0; with store revenue growing low-double-digits and digital increasing mid-single-digits. In addition, wholesale growth of approximately 30% was driven by international markets; strong profitability in each channel allows the Company to meet consumers where they are, delivering brand-accretive experiences while generating compelling returns.
 
 &#xa0;
 
 Overall, Tapestry grew pro forma revenue by 12%, non-GAAP operating profit by 24%, and non-GAAP earnings per diluted share by 32%, demonstrating the compounding advantages of its brand-building capabilities, global scale, and disciplined execution.

 Overview of Fiscal 2027 First Quarter Financial Results 
 &#xa0;
 
 
 &#x25cf;
 
 
 Net sales totaled $1.89 billion, increasing 11% on a reported and constant currency basis. Excluding Stuart Weitzman sales in the prior year period, pro forma net sales increased 12% on a reported and constant currency basis.
 
 &#xa0;
 
 
 &#x25cf;
 
 
 Gross profit totaled $1.49 billion on a GAAP basis&#xa0;compared to $1.30 billion in the prior year. On a non-GAAP basis, gross profit totaled $1.49 billion, &#xa0; while gross margin was 78.7%, representing an expansion of 220 basis points versus prior year, driven by operational improvements of approximately 130 basis points, a 70-basis point net benefit from tariffs and duties, and a 20-basis point benefit from foreign currency.
 
 &#xa0;
 
 
 &#x25cf;
 
 
 SG&A expenses totaled $1.06 billion on a GAAP basis&#xa0;compared to $973 million in the prior year. On a non-GAAP basis, SG&A expenses totaled $1.05 billion, &#xa0; representing 55.5% of sales, 10 basis points below prior year, including a 140-basis point increase in marketing investment.
 
 &#xa0; 
 5 
 
 &#xa0; 
 
 &#x25cf;
 
 
 Operating income was $424 million on a GAAP basis, compared to prior year operating income of $328 million. On a non-GAAP basis, operating income was $439 million, an increase of 24% versus the prior year, while operating margin was 23.2%, representing an expansion of approximately 230 basis points versus the prior year.
 
 &#xa0;
 
 
 &#x25cf;
 
 
 Net interest expense was $13 million in both the current and prior year.
 
 &#xa0;
 
 
 &#x25cf;
 
 
 Other expense was less than $1 million versus other income of $4 million in the prior year.
 
 &#xa0;
 
 
 &#x25cf;
 
 
 Tax rate was 12.5% on a GAAP basis and 12.7% on a non-GAAP basis versus the prior year tax rate of 13.8% on a GAAP basis and 13.7% on a non-GAAP basis.
 
 &#xa0;
 
 
 &#x25cf;
 
 
 Earnings per diluted share &#xa0;was $1.76 on a GAAP basis compared to $1.28 in the prior year, an increase of 38%. &#xa0; Non-GAAP EPS was $1.82 compared to $1.38 in the prior year, an increase of 32%.

 Management utilizes non-GAAP and constant currency measures to conduct and evaluate its business during its regular review of operating results for the periods affected and to make decisions about Company resources and performance.  The Company also presents Adjusted Free Cash Flow, which is a non-GAAP measure, and is calculated as Net cash provided by (used in) operating activities less Purchases of property and equipment, adjusted for the cash impacts of Items affecting comparability included in operating cash flows related to Distribution Network Optimization Costs, Acquisition and Divestiture Costs and Organizational Efficiency Costs, and Changes in operating assets and liabilities of items affecting comparability. The Company believes that Adjusted Free Cash Flow is an important liquidity measure of the cash that is available after capital expenditures for operational expenses, investment in our business and items affecting comparability. The Company believes that Adjusted Free Cash Flow is useful to investors because it measures the Company&#x2019;s ability to generate or use cash. Once our business needs and obligations are met, cash can be used to maintain a strong balance sheet, invest in future growth and return capital to stockholders. 
 &#xa0;
 
 The Company also presents Leverage Ratio, which is a non-GAAP metric, and is calculated as total debt, which includes Current debt and Long-term debt, divided by Adjusted EBITDA for the trailing twelve months. Adjusted EBITDA is calculated as Net Income (Loss), excluding: Interest expense, net; Provision for income taxes; Depreciation and amortization; Cloud Computing amortization; Share-based compensation; and Items affecting comparability including Distribution Network Optimization Costs, Acquisition and Divestiture Costs, Organizational Efficiency Costs and IEEPA tariff refunds. The Company believes that the Leverage Ratio is an important metric to assess the strength of our balance sheet and credit quality and demonstrates our commitment to maintaining an investment-grade credit rating.
