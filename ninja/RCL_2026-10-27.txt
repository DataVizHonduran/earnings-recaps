 Document Exhibit 99.1 FOR IMMEDIATE RELEASE ROYAL CARIBBEAN GROUP REPORTS THIRD QUARTER RESULTS ABOVE EXPECTATIONS AND RAISES FULL YEAR GUIDANCE MIAMI October 27, 2026 Royal Caribbean Group (NYSE RCL) today reported third quarter Earnings per Share ( EPS ) of $6.45 and Adjusted EPS of $6.57. These results were better than the company's guidance, driven by strong close-in demand and lower costs due to timing. The company now expects full year Adjusted EPS to be in the range of $17.85 to $17.95. The increase in earnings expectations reflects the stronger-than-expected third quarter performance partially offset by higher than anticipated fuel costs (based on current at-the-pump rates) and currency exchange rates. Our third quarter results reflect continued strong consumer demand for our vacation experiences and the accelerated momentum across our industry leading brands, said Jason Liberty, Chairman and CEO, Royal Caribbean Group. Our strategy to turn the vacation of a lifetime into a lifetime of vacations is propelling our business by leveraging our leading brands, differentiated experiences, loyalty platform and technology capabilities to deepen guest relationships, increase vacation frequency and capture a greater share of the growing global vacation market. Taking further advantage of our leading vacation platform, the recently announced partnership with Sandals and Beaches Resorts is intended to drive significant shareholder value as we expand our vacation ecosystem, giving guests more ways to vacation with us across more occasions. Demand for our leading vacation experiences remains strong, and while it is still early, 2027 bookings are pacing ahead of historical levels at higher rates, Liberty continued. 2027 also marks the final year of our Perfecta target period. The underlying business is performing well ahead of our initial expectations, and based on the forward fuel curve, we remain on track to achieve our Perfecta goals of delivering 20% Adjusted EPS CAGR from 2024 through 2027, or a $20-handle in 2027. We are currently already above our ROIC target. Third Quarter 2026 Total revenue was $5.6 billion, a 9% increase year over year. Load factor in the third quarter was 112%. Gross Margin Yields decreased 0.8% as-reported. Net Yields increased 0.9% as-reported and 0.6% in Constant Currency, driven by better-than-expected close-in demand in Europe and the Caribbean. Gross Cruise Costs per Available Passenger Cruise Days ( APCD ) decreased 0.7% as-reported. Net Cruise Costs ( NCC ), excluding Fuel, per APCD decreased 1.9% as-reported and 1.8% in Constant Currency, due to the timing of expenses that shifted into the fourth quarter. Net Income was $1.7 billion or $6.45 per share, Adjusted Net Income was $1.8 billion or $6.57 per share, and Adjusted EBITDA was $2.5 billion. Full Year 2026 Outlook Revenue is expected to grow 9% year over year. Net Yields are expected to increase approximately 2.7% as-reported and approximately 2.1% in Constant Currency. NCC, exclud ing Fuel, per APCD are expected to increase approximately 0.4% as-reported and be approximately flat in Constant Currency. Adjusted EPS is expected to be in the range of $17.85 to $17.95. Sandals and Beaches Resorts Partnership In September, Royal Caribbean Group announced an agreement to acquire a 50% interest in Sandals and Beaches Resorts, expanding the company s vacation platform into the all-inclusive resort category. The Sandals and Beaches Resorts' portfolio includes 18 resorts across the Caribbean and three resorts under development. It also includes over 1,000 acres of undeveloped land in the Caribbean, including acreage adjacent to existing resorts, creating a meaningful runway for continued growth. Sandals and Beaches are leading brands in the all-inclusive resort category, with high brand awareness, strong guest satisfaction and a loyal customer base. The portfolio has benefited from significant investment in recent years, including physical upgrades across the resorts and continued enhancements to the guest experience. The partnership will preserve the identity, operating leadership, and Caribbean character of the brands, while providing additional resources and capabilities to support continued value creation. Approximately 30% of Royal Caribbean Group guests also take all-inclusive vacations, while a similar percentage of Sandals and Beaches guests also cruise, creating a significant opportunity to introduce each portfolio to new guests and capture a greater share of the vacations they already take. Our mission is to deliver the best vacation experiences responsibly. Sandals and Beaches Resorts extend our platform into an attractive adjacent category, unlocking more ways to deliver on that mission, said Jason Liberty, Chairman and CEO, Royal Caribbean Group. Underpinned by the strength and significant growth opportunities of our core business, this investment accelerates our strategy to capture a greater share of the rapidly growing vacation market. By bringing together Sandals and Beaches hospitality and Caribbean expertise with our global commercial reach, loyalty and technology capabilities, we see meaningful opportunities to reach new guests, increase vacation frequency, and leverage our combined scale to create additional synergies and value over time. The investment is expected to close in early 2027, subject to customary closing conditions and regulatory approvals. The company's share of earnings from Sandals and Beaches Resorts is expected to be immediately accretive, with contribution in 2027 dependent on the timing of the transaction close. The investment is expected to be accounted for under the equity method, with results reported on a one-quarter lag. The expected contribution from Sandals and Beaches Resorts will be incorporated into the company s 2027 guidance, along with related detail, upon closing. Third Quarter 2026 Results Net Income for the third quarter of 2026 was $1.7 billion or $6.45 per share compared to Net Income of $1.6 billion or $5.74 per share for the same period in the prior year. Adjusted Net Income was $1.8 billion or $6.57 per share for the third quarter of 2026 compared to Adjusted Net Income of $1.6 billion or $5.75 per share for the same period in the prior year. The company also reported total revenues of $5.6 billion and Adjusted EBITDA of $2.5 billion. Capacity for the third quarter was up 8% year over year and the company delivered memorable vacations to 2.7 million guests, a 9% increase year over year. Total revenue increased 9% year over year. Gross Margin Yields decreased 0.8% as-reported, and Net Yields increased 0.9% as-reported (0.6% in Constant Currency), when compared to the third quarter of 2025. Load factor for the quarter was 112%. Net Yield growth exceeded the company s guidance primarily driven by better than expected close-in demand in Europe and the Caribbean. Gross Cruise Costs per APCD decreased 0.7% as-reported, compared to the third quarter of 2025. NCC, excluding Fuel, per APCD decreased 1.9% as-reported (and decreased 1.8% in Constant Currency), when compared to the third quarter of 2025. The better-than-expected cost performance in the third quarter was due to the timing of expenses that shifted into the fourth quarter. Demand, Bookings and Vacation Platform Since the last earnings call, booking volumes have increased in the mid-teens across all major channels, with bookings for 2027 sailings up in the high teens, demonstrating strong demand as booking activity builds for 2027. The company continues to see strong engagement across the vacation journey. Third quarter pre-cruise and onboard revenue rates were ahead of prior year, and fourth quarter pre-cruise revenue rates are also booked ahead of last year. The company s expanding range of products and services, together with its technology capabilities, is enabling more relevant and personalized engagement by connecting guests with the right experiences at the right time. Fourth Quarter 2026 In line with the company's prior expectation, Net Yields are expected to be 4.4% to 4.9% as-reported and 4.2% to 4.7% in Constant Currency as compared to 2025. Net Yield growth is driven by strong demand, a more favorable year-over-year comparison, deployment mix, and the timing of dry docks. Total revenue is expected to grow 9%. NCC, excluding Fuel, per APCD is expected to decrease 1.5% to 1.0% as-reported and 1.4% to 0.9% in Constant Currency as compared to 2025. Definitions Selected Operational and Financial Metrics Adjusted Earnings per Share ( Adjusted EPS ) is a non-GAAP measure that represents Adjusted Net Income attributable to Royal Caribbean Cruises Ltd. (as defined below) divided by basic weighted average shares outstanding or by diluted weighted average shares outstanding, as applicable. We believe that this non-GAAP measure is meaningful when assessing our performance on a comparative basis. Adjusted EBITDA is a non-GAAP measure that represents EBITDA (as defined below) excluding certain items that we believe adjusting for is meaningful whe n assessing our profitability on a comparative basis. For the periods presented , these items included (i) other (income) expense (ii) restructuring charges and other initiative expenses and (iii) equity investment impairment, (recovery) of losses and other. Adjusted EBITDA Margin is a non-GAAP measure that represents Adjusted EBITDA (as defined above) divided by total revenues. Adjusted Gross Margin represents Gross Margin, adjusted for payroll and related, food, fuel, other operating, and depreciation and amortization expenses. Gross Margin is calculated pursuant to GAAP as total revenues less total cruise operating expenses, and depreciation and amortization expenses. Adjusted Net Income attributable to Royal Caribbean Cruises Ltd. is a non-GAAP measure that represents Net Income attributable to Royal Caribbean Cruises Ltd., excluding certain items that we believe adjusting for is meaningful when assessing our performance on a comparative basis. For the periods presented, these items included (i) loss on extinguishment of debt and inducement expense (ii) restructuring charges and other initiative expenses (iii) the amortization of the Silversea intangible assets resulting from the Silversea acquisition (iv) gain on sale of noncontrolling interest and (v) equity investment impairment, (recovery) of losses, and other. Adjusted Operating Income is a non-GAAP measure that represents operating income including income from equity investments and provision for income taxes but excluding certain items for which we believe adjusting for is meaningful when assessing our operating performance on a comparative basis. We use this non-GAAP measure to calculate ROIC (as defined below). Available Passenger Cruise Days ( APCD ) is our measurement of capacity and represents double occupancy per cabin multiplied by the number of cruise days for the period, which excludes canceled cruise days and cabins not available for sale. We use this measure to perform capacity and rate analysis to identify our main non-capacity drivers that cause our cruise revenue and expenses to vary. Constant Currency is a significant measure for our revenues and expenses, which are denominated in currencies other than the U.S. Dollar. Because our reporting currency is the U.S. Dollar, the value of these revenues and expenses in U.S. Dollar will be affected by changes in currency exchange rates. Although such changes in local currency prices are just one of many elements impacting our revenues and expenses, it can be an important element. For this reason, we also monitor our revenues and expenses in Constant Currency - i.e., as if the current period's currency exchange rates had remained constant with the comparable prior period's rates. We calculate Constant Currency by applying the average of the prior period exchange rates for each of the corresponding months, of the reported and or forecasted period, so as to calculate what the results would have been had exchange rates been the same throughout both periods. We do not make predictions about future exchange rates and use current exchange rates for calculations of future periods. It should be emphasized that the use of Constant Currency is primarily used by us for comparing short-term changes and or projections. Over the longer term, changes in guest sourcing and shifting the amount of purchases between currencies can significantly change the impact of the purely currency-based fluctuations. EBITDA is a non-GAAP measure that represents Net Income attributable to Royal Caribbean Cruises Ltd. excluding (i) interest income (ii) interest expense, net of interest capitalized (iii) depreciation and amortization expenses and (iv) provision for income taxes. We believe that this non-GAAP measure is meaningful when assessing our operating performance on a comparative basis. Gross Cruise Costs represent the sum of total cruise operating expenses plus marketing, selling and administrative expenses. Gross Margin Yield represent Gross Margin per APCD. Invested Capital represents the most recent five-quarter average of total debt (i.e., Current portion of long-term debt plus Long-term debt) plus the most recent five-quarter average of Total shareholders' equity. We use this measure to calculate ROIC (as defined below). Net Cruise Costs and Net C ruise Costs excluding Fuel are non-GAAP measures that represent Gross Cruise Costs excluding commissions, transportation and other expenses, and onboard and other expenses and, in the case of Net Cruise Costs excluding Fuel, fuel expenses. In measuring our ability to control costs in a manner that positively impacts net income, we believe changes in Net Cruise Costs and Net Cruise Costs excluding Fuel to be the most relevant indicators of our cost performance. For the periods presented, Net Cruise Costs and Net Cruise Costs excluding Fuel exclude restructuring charges and other initiative expenses . Net Yields represent Adjusted Gross Margin per APCD. We utilize Adjusted Gross Margin and Net Yields to manage our business on a day-to-day basis as we believe that they are the most relevant measures of our pricing performance because they reflect the cruise revenues earned by us net of our most significant variable costs, which are commissions, transportation and other expenses, and onboard and other expenses. Occupancy ( Load factor ) , in accordance with cruise vacation industry practice, is calculated by dividing Passenger Cruise Days (as defined below) by APCD. A percentage in excess of 100% indicates that three or more passengers occupied some cabins. Passenger Cruise Days ( PCD ) represent the number of passengers carried for the period multiplied by the number of days of their respective cruises. Perfecta Program refers to the multi-year Adjusted EPS and ROIC goals we are seeking to achieve by end of 2027. Under our Perfecta Program, we are targeting 20% compound annual growth rate in Adjusted EPS compared to 2024 and ROIC of 17% or higher by the end of 2027. Return on Invested Capital ( ROIC ) represents Adjusted Operating Income divided by Invested Capital. We believe ROIC is a meaningful measure because it quantifies how efficiently we generated operating income relative to the capital we have invested in the business. For additional information see Adjusted Measures of Financial Performance below. About Royal Caribbean Group Royal Caribbean Group is a leading global vacation company spanning cruise, exclusive destinations, and land-based vacation experiences. The company operates 71 ships sailing to more than 1,000 destinations across all seven continents through its three wholly owned brands - Royal Caribbean, Celebrity Cruises, and Silversea - and a 50% joint venture interest in TUI Cruises which operates the Mein Schiff and Hapag-Lloyd brands . The Group is expanding its portfolio of private destinations through its Perfect Day and Royal Beach Club collections, and the company will enter river cruising in 2027 with Celebrity River Cruises. Powered by innovative brands, advanced technology, and an industry-leading loyalty program, the company has built a connected vacation ecosystem, turning the vacation of a lifetime into a lifetime of vacations. Named to the Fortune World s Most Admired Companies 2026 list and to Forbes 2026 Best American Companies lists, Royal Caribbean Group is guided by its mission to deliver the best vacations responsibly. For more information, visit www.royalcaribbeangroup.com.
