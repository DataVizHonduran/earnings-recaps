 Document Exhibit 99.1 IQVIA Reports Third-Quarter 2026 Results Revenue $4,428 million, up 8.0% year-over-year, up 8.6% at constant currency GAAP Net Income $350 million, Adjusted EBITDA $1,032 million, up 8.7% year-over-year GAAP Diluted Earnings per Share $2.10, Adjusted Diluted Earnings per Share $3.34, up 11.3% year-over-year Commercial Solutions Revenue $1,789 million, up 6.9% year-over-year, up 7.9% at constant currency R D Solutions Revenue $2,639 million, up 8.7% year-over-year, up 9.1% at constant currency R D Solutions Net New Bookings $3.2 billion, up 15% year-over-year Operating Cash Flow $825 million, and Free Cash Flow $667 million, representing 120% of Adjusted Net Income Raising full-year 2026 guidance for Revenue, Adjusted EBITDA and Adjusted Diluted Earnings per Share Investor Day to be held on December 1, 2026 RESEARCH TRIANGLE PARK, N.C. (BUSINESS WIRE) October 27, 2026 IQVIA Holdings Inc. ( IQVIA ) (NYSE IQV), a leading global provider of clinical research services, commercial insights and healthcare intelligence to the life sciences and healthcare industries, today reported financial results for the quarter ended September 30, 2026. Year-Over-Year Comparisons As previously disclosed, effective January 1, 2026, the company implemented a new, simplified organizational model to strengthen collaboration, enhance efficiency, and support continued growth. As a result, the company reports its results within two segments Commercial Solutions and Research Development Solutions (R DS). In conjunction with this change, prior period segment amounts have been recast to conform to this reporting structure. Third-Quarter 2026 Operating Results IQVIA delivered another record quarter, with revenue, Adjusted EBITDA and Adjusted Diluted EPS all exceeding the high end of our expectations, said Ari Bousbib, chairman and CEO of IQVIA. Multiple demand indicators continue to validate the strengthening market environment. EBP funding, RFP flow, clinical trial starts, FDA approvals, outsourcing trends, and pipeline growth across clinical and commercial are all favorable. At the same time, the team's ongoing excellent execution delivered margin expansion, strong free cash flow, higher win rates that resulted in our strongest clinical quarterly bookings since 2022, increased penetration of AI-powered solutions with over 400 AI agents deployed to date, and organic growth acceleration year-over-year to double the rate for commercial and nearly triple the rate for clinical. Revenue for the third quarter of $4,428 million increased 8.0% on a reported basis and 8.6% at constant currency, compared to the third quarter of 2025. Commercial Solutions revenue of $1,789 million increased 6.9% on a reported basis and 7.9% at constant currency. Research Development Solutions revenue of $2,639 million grew 8.7% on a reported basis and 9.1% at constant currency. Excluding reimbursed expenses, R DS revenue grew 6.8% on a reported basis. Third quarter net new bookings were $3.2 billion, up 15% year-over-year, representing a book-to-bill ratio of 1.2x. Last-twelve-month net new bookings were $11.7 billion, up 14% year-over-year. As previously discussed, the company conducted a detailed review of inactive trials in our backlog estimates. This review resulted in an adjustment to backlog of approximately $2.1 billion. The adjustment has no impact on historical financial results, 2026 guidance, book-to-bill, or next-twelve-month revenue from backlog estimates. As of September 30, 2026, R DS contracted backlog was $32.6 billion. The company expects approximately $9.4 billion of this backlog to convert to revenue in the next twelve months, representing growth of 10% year-over-year. 1 Third-quarter GAAP Net Income was $350 million and GAAP Diluted Earnings per Share was $2.10. Adjusted EBITDA was $1,032 million, up 8.7% year-over-year. Adjusted Net Income was $557 million and Adjusted Diluted Earnings per Share was $3.34, up 11.3% year-over-year. Year-to-Date 2026 Operating Results Revenue for the first nine months of 2026 was $12,947 million, up 8.4% on a reported basis and 7.7% at constant currency, compared to the first nine months of 2025. Commercial Solutions revenue of $5,336 million increased 9.0% on a reported basis and 8.3% at constant currency. Research Development Solutions revenue of $7,611 million increased 8.0% on a reported basis and 7.4% at constant currency. GAAP Net Income was $880 million and GAAP Diluted Earnings per Share was $5.24. Adjusted EBITDA was $2,958 million, up 7.9% year-over-year. Adjusted Net Income was $1,576 million and Adjusted Diluted Earnings per Share was $9.38, up 10.4% year-over-year. Financial Position As of September 30, 2026, cash and cash equivalents were $2,249 million and debt was $16,067 million, resulting in net debt of $13,818 million. IQVIA s Net Leverage Ratio was 3.45x trailing twelve-month Adjusted EBITDA. For the third quarter, Operating Cash Flow was $825 million, and Free Cash Flow was $667 million, representing 120% of Adjusted Net Income. Full-Year 2026 Guidance The company is raising its full-year 2026 guidance for revenue to be between $17,350 million and $17,500 million, for Adjusted EBITDA to be between $4,025 million and $4,055 million and for Adjusted Diluted Earnings per Share to be between $12.90 and $13.00. This revenue guidance now anticipates 250 basis points of contribution from acquisitions, including acquisitions the company expects to complete by the end of the year, and approximately 20 basis points of tailwind from foreign exchange. All financial guidance assumes foreign currency exchange rates as of October 26, 2026 remain in effect for the forecast period.
