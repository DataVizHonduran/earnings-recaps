 Document GRAINGER REPORTS RESULTS FOR THE THIRD QUARTER 2026 Continued strong results across the business Company increases full year 2026 earnings outlook Third Quarter Highlights Delivered sales of $5.2 billion, up 11.3%, or 14.9% on a daily, organic constant currency basis Achieved operating margin of 16.0% on a reported basis, up 500 basis points, or 16.1% on an adjusted basis, up 90 basis points, which excludes certain non-recurring items, notably the loss related to the Company's exit of the U.K. market in the prior year period Generated diluted EPS of $12.42 on a reported basis, up 102.9%, or $12.54 on an adjusted basis, up 22.8% Produced $661 million in operating cash flow and returned $396 million to Grainger shareholders through dividends and share repurchases Increasing full year 2026 earnings outlook, including diluted adjusted EPS range of $47.00 to $48.00 CHICAGO, November 4, 2026 - Grainger (NYSE GWW) today reported results for the third quarter of 2026 with sales of $5.2 billion, up 11.3%, or 14.9% on a daily, organic constant currency basis, and adjusted diluted EPS of $12.54, up 22.8% compared to the third quarter of 2025. The third quarter performance reflects our consistent execution and an improving demand environment, said D.G. Macpherson, Chairman and CEO. As customers continue to turn to us for the products and solutions they need, both our High-Touch Solutions and Endless Assortment segments delivered impressive growth. Revenue Sales in the quarter increased 11.3% compared to the third quarter of 2025. When normalizing for the Company's exit from the U.K. market and the impact of foreign currency exchange, sales on a daily, organic constant currency basis increased 14.9% compared to the third quarter of 2025. In the High-Touch Solutions - N.A. segment, sales were up 13.8% on both a reported and daily, constant currency basis compared to the third quarter of 2025. Results for the segment were driven by double-digit volume growth and continued price inflation as cost increases were passed. In the Endless Assortment segment, sales were up 11.9% compared to the third quarter of 2025, or up 19.3% on a daily, organic constant curre ncy basis. Growth for the segment was driven by strong performance at both MonotaRO and Zoro. Gross Profit Margin Gross profit margin was 38.8% in the third quarter of 2026, up 20 basis points compared to the third quarter of 2025, driven by expansion across the Endless Assortment segment and a benefit related to the Company's exit from the U.K. market. In the High-Touch Solutions - N.A. segment, gross profit margin was 40.8%, down 30 basis points compared to the prior year quarter due to unfavorable freight expense and mix. In the 2 Endless Assortment segment, gross profit margin increased by 90 basis points from the third quarter of 2025 due to improvement at both MonotaRO and Zoro. Earnings For the third quarter of 2026, total Company reported operating earnings were $828 million, up 62.0% compared to the third quarter of 2025. Operating margin was 16.0%, a 500 basis point increase compared to the third quarter of 2025. On an adjusted basis, operating earnings were $836 million, up 18.2% compared to the third quarter of 2025. Adjusted operating margin was 16.1%, a 90 basis point increase compared to the third quarter of 2025. This increase in adjusted operating margin was driven by sales leverage in both segments and a benefit related to the Company's exit from the U.K. market. These adjusted results for the quarter exclude transaction costs associated with the acquisition of technology assets in the current year period and the loss related to the Company's exit of the U.K. market, including the sale of the Cromwell business and closure of Zoro U.K., in the prior year period. Diluted earnings per share for the third quarter of 2026 were $12.42 on a reported basis, up 102.9% compared to the third quarter of 2025. On an adjusted basis, diluted EPS was $12.54, up 22.8% compared to the third quarter of 2025. The increase was due primarily to strong operating performance, fewer shares outstanding, and a lower effective tax rate. Tax Rate For the third quarter of 2026, the effective tax rate was 24.3%, compared to 34.7% in the third quarter of 2025. On an adjusted basis, the effective tax rate was 24.3% compared to 24.8% in the prior year period. The variance for the adjusted tax rate was primarily due to additional tax credit benefits recorded in the third quarter of 2026. Cash Flow During the third quarter of 2026, the Company generated $661 million of cash flow from operating activities driven by strong net earnings. The Company invested $115 million in capital expenditures, resulting in free cash flow of $546 million. TO NON-GAAP FINANCIAL MEASURES (Unaudited) The Company supplements the reporting of financial information determined under U.S. generally accepted accounting principles (GAAP) with the non-GAAP financial measures as defined below. The Company believes these non-GAAP financial measures provide meaningful information to assist investors in understanding financial results and assessing future performance as they provide a better baseline for analyzing the ongoing performance of its business by excluding items that may not be indicative of core operating results. Basis of presentation The Company has a controlling ownership interest in MonotaRO, which is part of the Endless Assortment segment. MonotaRO s results are fully consolidated, reflected in U.S. GAAP, and reported one-month in arrears. Results will differ from MonotaRO s externally reported financials which follow Japanese GAAP. Adjusted gross profit , adjusted SG A , adjusted operating earnings , adjusted operating margin , adjusted net earnings , adjusted diluted EPS Exclude certain non-recurring items, like restructuring charges, asset impairments, gains and losses associated with business divestitures or closures and other non-recurring, infrequent or unusual gains and losses (together referred to as non-GAAP adjustments ), from the Company s most directly comparable reported U.S. GAAP figures (reported gross profit, SG A, operating earnings, net earnings and EPS). The Company believes these non-GAAP adjustments provide meaningful information to assist investors in understanding financial results and assessing future performance as they provide a better baseline for analyzing the ongoing performance of its business by excluding items that may not be indicative of core operating results. Free cash flow (FCF) Calculated using total cash provided by operating activities less capital expenditures. The Company believes the presentation of FCF allows investors to evaluate the capacity of the Company's operations to generate free cash flow. Daily sales Refers to sales for the period divided by the number of U.S. selling days for the period. Daily, constant currency sales Refers to daily sales adjusted for changes in foreign currency exchange rates. Daily, organic constant currency sales Refers to daily sales excluding the sales of certain divested or closed businesses in the comparable prior year period post date of divestiture or closure and changes in foreign currency exchange rates. Foreign currency exchange Calculated by dividing current period local currency daily sales by current period average exchange rate and subtracting the current period local currency daily sales divided by the prior period average exchange rate.
