 Document Exhibit 99.1 NVR, INC. ANNOUNCES THIRD QUARTER RESULTS October 22, 2026, Reston, VA—NVR, Inc. (NYSE NVR) , one of the nation s largest homebuilding and mortgage banking companies, announced net income for its third quarter ended September 30, 2026 of $248.6 million, or $89.45 per diluted share. For the third quarter ended September 30, 2026, net income and diluted earnings per share decreased 27% and 20%, respectively, when compared to 2025 third quarter net income of $342.7 million, or $112.33 per diluted share. Consolidated revenues for the third quarter of 2026 totaled $2.40 billion, compared to $2.61 billion in the third quarter of 2025. For the nine months ended September 30, 2026, consolidated revenues were $6.61 billion, a 13% decrease from $7.61 billion reported for the same period of 2025. Net income for the nine months ended September 30, 2026 was $683.4 million, a decrease of 30% when compared to net income for the nine months ended September 30, 2025 of $976.0 million. Diluted earnings per share for the nine months ended September 30, 2026 was $240.54, a decrease of 24% from $315.33 per diluted share for the same period of 2025. Homebuilding New orders in the third quarter of 2026 decreased by 7% to 4,391 units, when compared to 4,735 units in the third quarter of 2025. The average sales price of new orders in the third quarter of 2026 was $442,100, a decrease of 5% when compared to the third quarter of 2025. The cancellation rate in the third quarter of 2026 was 21% compared to 19% in the third quarter of 2025. Settlements in the third quarter of 2026 decreased by 7% to 5,268 units, compared to 5,639 units in the third quarter of 2025. The average settlement price in the third quarter of 2026 was $445,900, a decrease of 2% when compared to the third quarter of 2025. Our backlog of homes sold but not settled as of September 30, 2026 increased on a unit basis by 10% to 10,121 units and increased on a dollar basis by 4% to $4.59 billion when compared to the respective backlog unit and dollar balances as of September 30, 2025. Homebuilding revenues of $2.35 billion in the third quarter of 2026 decreased by 8% compared to homebuilding revenues of $2.56 billion in the third quarter of 2025. Gross profit margin in the third quarter of 2026 decreased to 18.3%, from 21.0% in the third quarter of 2025. Gross profit margin was negatively impacted by higher lot costs, pricing pressure due to continued affordability challenges and weak consumer sentiment, and by contract land deposit impairments totaling approximately $24.8 million. Income before tax from the homebuilding segment totaled $299.7 million in the third quarter of 2026, a decrease of 27% when compared to the third quarter of 2025. Mortgage Banking Mortgage closed loan production in the third quarter of 2026 totaled $1.38 billion, a decrease of 10% when compared to the third quarter of 2025. Income before tax from the mortgage banking segment totaled $35.0 million in the third quarter of 2026, an increase of 7% when compared to $32.7 million in the third quarter of 2025. About NVR NVR, Inc. operates in two business segments homebuilding and mortgage banking. The homebuilding segment sells and builds homes under the Ryan Homes, NVHomes and Heartland Homes trade names, and operates in thirty-seven metropolitan areas in sixteen states and Washington, D.C. For more information about NVR, Inc. and its brands, see www.nvrinc.com, www.ryanhomes.com, www.nvhomes.com and www.heartlandluxuryhomes.com.
