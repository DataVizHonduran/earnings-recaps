 Document Exhibit 99.1 Kimberly-Clark Announces Third Quarter Results, Reaffirms 2026 Standalone Outlook Well-positioned to capitalize on a generational value creation opportunity and drive the next phase of durable growth DALLAS, November 5, 2026 - Kimberly-Clark Corporation (Nasdaq KMB) today reported third quarter 2026 results reflecting continued innovation-driven momentum and industry-leading productivity, despite several significant, discrete unfavorable impacts. The completion of our Kenvue transaction earlier this week marks a defining moment in the 154 year history of Kimberly-Clark. We embark on an exciting next chapter focused on delivering better care, in more hands, and enabling smarter health. said Kimberly-Clark Chairman and CEO Mike Hsu. Our third quarter results reflect a dynamic operating environment and several discrete impacts. Our team continues to execute with agility, and the underlying momentum of our business remains strong. We re excited to join forces with our Kenvue colleagues and apply our playbook and capabilities across the combined company. We are confident that our proven growth engine, and our continued industry-leading productivity gains, will enable us to build on the momentum we have achieved through Powering Care. Hsu continued, I want to thank all of our Kimberly-Clark employees for their contributions to our transformation, and extend a warm welcome to our Kenvue colleagues. Our teams are energized and focused on realizing the unique, generational value creation opportunity in front of us. As one company, we are well positioned to deliver extraordinary care and lead the future of consumer health. Third Quarter 2026 Results Net sales of $4.1 billion were relatively flat compared to the prior year, as favorable impacts from sales recognized under supply agreements with the Arbex Joint Venture and favorable currency impacts were largely offset by a -2- decline in organic sales. Organic sales declined 1.3 percent, including an approximately 270 basis point headwind from the China social media disruption, as well as additional headwinds from retailer year-over-year inventory movements in North America. Gross margin was 34.9 percent compared to 36.0 percent in the prior year, inclusive of $29 million, or approximately 70 basis points, and $34 million, or approximately 80 basis points, respectively, of charges related to the 2024 Transformation Initiative. Excluding these charges, adjusted gross margin was 35.5 percent, a decrease of 130 basis points versus the prior year, driven by unfavorable pricing net of cost inflation and supply chain related investments, partially offset by strong productivity savings. Third quarter operating profit was $516 million compared to $621 million in the prior year. Current quarter results included charges of $55 million related to the 2024 Transformation Initiative and $106 million related to the Kenvue Acquisition. Prior year results included $62 million of charges related to the 2024 Transformation Initiative. Excluding these items, adjusted operating profit was $677 million compared to $683 million, a decrease of 0.9 percent, driven by unfavorable pricing net of cost inflation and headwinds from the China social media disruption of approximately 530 basis points, partially offset by lower marketing, research and general expenses. Net interest expense was $32 million versus $59 million in the prior year primarily driven by lower interest expense. The third quarter effective tax rate was 20.2 percent, compared to 45.4 percent in the prior year. On an adjusted basis, the effective rate was 19.5 percent compared to 26.5 percent in the p rior year. The year-on-year impact was driven primarily by a combination of transitory impacts in the prior year from the One Big Beautiful Bill Act ( OBBBA ) and benefits in the current year from certain tax initiatives. Net income of equity companies was $29 million compared to $46 million in the prior year. The decrease was primarily driven by our share of the Arbex Joint Venture's net loss of $20 million, which was inclusive of $51 million pre-tax ($38 million after-tax) of acquisition accounting and restructuring charges. Income from discontinued operations, net of income taxes was $1.4 billion compared to $110 million in the prior year. Current year results included a pre-tax gain of approximately $1.4 billion recognized upon completion of the IFP Transaction and $52 million of pre-tax separation costs. Prior year results included $17 million of pre-tax separation costs. Excluding these items, adjusted income (loss) from discontinued operations was $(1) million compared to $123 million in the prior year, reflecting the completion of the IFP Transaction on July 1, 2026. Diluted EPS from continuing operations were $1.19 compared to $1.01 in the prior year. These reported amounts are inclusive of net charges of $0.51 and $0.44, respectively, for certain items discussed above that management does not believe reflect our underlying and ongoing operations. Excluding these items, adjusted EPS from continuing operations were $1.70, up 17.2 percent, primarily driven by lower net interest expense and a lower adjusted effective tax rate. Diluted EPS attributable to Kimberly-Clark were $5.37 compared to $1.34 in the prior year. These reported amounts are inclusive of a net benefit of $3.67 and net charges of $0.48, respectively, for certain items discussed above that management does not believe reflect our underlying and ongoing operations. These measures include Organic Sales Growth, Adjusted Gross Profit, Adjusted Operating Profit, Adjusted Earnings per Share from Continuing Operations, Adjusted Earnings per Share Attributable to Kimberly-Clark, Adjusted Effective Tax Rate, and Adjusted Income from Discontinued Operations. Unless specifically stated, all discussions regarding non-GAAP financial measures reflect results from our continuing operations for all periods presented. Organic Sales Growth is defined as the change in Net Sales, as determined in accordance with U.S. GAAP, excluding the impacts of currency translation and divestitures and business exits. Adjusted Gross and Operating Profit, Adjusted Earnings per Share from Continuing Operations, Adjusted Earnings per Share Attributable to Kimberly-Clark, Adjusted Effective Tax Rate, and Adjusted Income from Discontinued Operations are defined as Gross Profit, Operating Profit, Diluted Earnings per Share from Continuing Operations, Diluted Earnings per Share Attributable to Kimberly-Clark, Effective Tax Rate, and Income from Discontinued Operations, Net of Income Taxes, respectively, as determined in accordance with U.S. GAAP, excluding the impacts of certain items that management believes do not reflect our underlying operations, and which are discussed in further detail below. The income tax effect of these non-GAAP items on the company's Adjusted Earnings per Share from Continuing Operations, Adjusted Earnings per Share Attributable to Kimberly-Clark and Adjusted Income from Discontinued Operations is calculated based upon the tax laws and statutory income tax rates applicable in the tax jurisdiction(s) of the underlying non-GAAP adjustment. The impact of these non-GAAP items on the company s effective tax rate represents the difference in the effective tax rate calculated with and without the non-GAAP adjustment on pre-tax income and provision for income taxes. We use these non-GAAP financial measures to assist in comparing our performance on a consistent basis for purposes of business decision making by removing the impact of certain items that we do not believe reflect our underlying and ongoing operations. We believe that presenting these non-GAAP financial measures is useful to investors because it (i) provides investors with meaningful supplemental information regarding financial performance by excluding certain items, (ii) permits investors to view performance using the same tools that management uses to budget, make operating and strategic decisions, and evaluate historical performance, and (iii) otherwise provides supplemental information that may be useful to investors in evaluating our results. These non-GAAP financial measures are not meant to be considered in isolation or as a substitute for the comparable GAAP measures, and they should be read only in conjunction with our consolidated financial statements prepared in accordance with GAAP.
