 Document Exhibit 99.1 Quest Diagnostics Reports Third Quarter 2026 Financial Results Raises Revenue and EPS Guidance for Full Year 2026 Third quarter revenues of $3.10 billion, up 10.0% from 2025, on strong organic revenue growth Third quarter reported diluted earnings per share ( EPS ) of $2.78, up 28.7% from 2025 and adjusted diluted EPS of $3.00, up 15.4% from 2025 Full year 2026 revenues now expected to be between $12.065 billion and $12.105 billion Full year 2026 reported diluted EPS now expected to be between $10.21 and $10.31 and adjusted diluted EPS expected to be between $11.25 and $11.35 SECAUCUS, N.J., October 22, 2026 - Quest Diagnostics Incorporated (NYSE DGX), a leading provider of diagnostic information services, today announced financial results for the third quarter ended September 30, 2026. We delivered revenue growth of 10% and adjusted EPS growth of over 15% on continued strength across our physician, hospital and consumer channels, complemented by productivity gains from automation and AI, said Jim Davis, Chairman, CEO and President. Our robust organic revenue growth demonstrates that our solutions are fulfilling the expanding clinical care needs of an aging population as well as the increasing number of people focused on prevention and wellness. With these growth trends and our strong performance, we are raising our revenue and EPS guidance for the full year. Recent Highlights Announced the ability for iPhone and iPad users in the United States to conveniently purchase a tailored Quest laboratory test panel with over 50 biomarkers directly through the Apple Health app, available to the public later this year. Finalized a definitive agreement to acquire select outreach lab assets from a leading health system and long-time Co-Lab Solutions customer in the Northeastern U.S. Continued to advance our Co-Lab Solutions implementation and joint venture lab with Corewell Health in Michigan. Extended our AD-Detect menu to include the FDA-cleared Roche Elecsys Phospho-Tau (217P) Plasma (pTau217) blood test and launched a new AD-Detect test panel with pTau217, amyloid beta and APOE to help guide diagnosis and treatment decisions for Alzheimer s disease. Advanced Project Nova from the design phase to the solutions-build phase and continue to expect to deploy Epic solutions in our first lab in the fall of 2027. The company will hold its 2027 Investor Day on March 25, 2027, at the New York Stock Exchange.
