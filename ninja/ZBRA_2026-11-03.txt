 Document Exhibit 99.1 Zebra Technologies Corporation 3 Overlook Point Lincolnshire, IL 60069 USA +1 847 634 6700 www.zebra.com Zebra Technologies Announces Third Quarter 2026 Results Strong growth across segments and regions reflects customer investment in automation and productivity Raises full year outlook Lincolnshire, Ill., November 3, 2026 — Zebra Technologies Corporation (NASDAQ ZBRA), a global leader in digitizing and automating workflows to deliver intelligent operations, today announced results for the third quarter ended October 3, 2026. Third-Quarter Financial Highlights Net sales of $1,614 million year-over-year increase of 22.3% Net income of $164 million and net income per diluted share of $3.43 Non-GAAP diluted EPS increased year-over-year to $4.95 Adjusted EBITDA increased year-over-year to $349 million Share repurchases of $75 million We delivered another record quarter underpinned by strong execution and broad-based demand trends with Elo Touch contributing to our double-digit growth as customers prioritize automating their frontline operations. Our efforts to strengthen memory supply, implement pricing actions and capture operating leverage enabled us to support our growth while maintaining adjusted EBITDA margin year-over-year, said Bill Burns, Chief Executive Officer of Zebra Technologies. These results reinforce our confidence in the durability of demand and the strength of our business model as we execute on our strategy to drive profitable growth. We are putting our strong cash generation and balance sheet to work, investing in growth while returning capital to shareholders, said Nathan Winters, Chief Financial Officer of Zebra Technologies. Net sales in the Connected Frontline ( CF ) segment were $949 million compared to $705 million in the prior year. Asset Visibility Automation ( AVA ) segment net sales were $665 million compared to $615 million in the prior year. Consolidated organic net sales increased 11.3% year-over-year, with a 15.0% increase in the CF segment and a 7.0% increase in the AVA segment. Gross profit was $738 million compared to $634 million in the prior year. Gross margin decreased to 45.7% compared to 48.0% in the prior year primarily due to increased memory component costs and business mix partially offset by favorable foreign currency. Adjusted gross margin was 46.0% compared to 48.2% in the prior year. Operating expenses increased to $501 million from $451 million in the prior year primarily due to expenses associated with acquired businesses including amortization of intangible assets. Adjusted operating expenses increased to $413 million from $368 million in the prior year. Net income was $164 million, or $3.43 per diluted share, compared to net income of $101 million, or $1.97 per diluted share, in the prior year. Non-GAAP net income increased to $237 million or $4.95 per diluted share, compared to $198 million, or $3.88 per diluted share for the prior year. Adjusted EBITDA increased to $349 million, or 21.6% of adjusted net sales, compared to $285 million, or 21.6% of adjusted net sales in the prior year. Balance Sheet and Cash Flow As of October 3, 2026, the Company had cash and cash equivalents of $182 million and total debt of $2,578 million. For the first nine months of 2026, net cash provided by operating activities was $711 million and the Company invested $50 million in capital expenditures, resulting in free cash flow of $661 million. The Company also made share repurchases of $643 million. Outlook Mr. Burns added, We remain confident in the growth drivers supporting our business, and our ability to capture the opportunity ahead. We are driving operating productivity as we scale to meet the strong demand we are seeing for -2- our solutions. Together, these efforts support our increased full-year outlook and position us well for sustainable profitable growth as we continue to benefit from trends in automation and Physical AI. The Company expects fourth quarter sales growth between 10% and 14% compared to the prior year. This expectation includes approximately 150 basis points favorable impact from foreign currency. Adjusted EBITDA margin for the fourth quarter is expected to be approximately 22%. Non-GAAP diluted earnings per share are expected to be in the range of $5.00 to $5.40. This assumes an adjusted effective tax rate of approximately 19%. Free Cash Flow for the full year is expected to be at least $1 billion.
