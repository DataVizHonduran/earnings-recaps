 EX-99.1 PRESS RELEASE DATED OCTOBER 22, 2026
 
 
 
 
 Document Allegion (NYSE ALLE) Reports Q3-2026 Financial Results High-Single Digit Revenue Growth, Strength in Americas Non-Residential Raising Full-Year 2026 Outlook Quarterly Financial Highlights (All comparisons are year-over-year, unless otherwise noted) Net earnings per share (EPS) of $2.59, up 18.8% compared with $2.18 Adjusted EPS of $2.60, up 13.0% compared with $2.30 Revenues of $1,172.6 million, up 9.6% on a reported basis and up 7.2% on an organic basis Operating margin of 24.3%, compared with 21.8% Adjusted operating margin of 24.7%, compared with 24.1% DUBLIN (Oct. 22, 2026) Allegion plc (NYSE ALLE), a leading global security products and solutions provider, today reported financial results for its third quarter (ended Sept. 30, 2026). I am pleased with our Q3 performance as we delivered high-single digit organic revenue growth and low teens adjusted EPS growth led by our Americas non-residential business, Allegion President and CEO John H. Stone said. With steady momentum in our demand generation engine and a proven, resilient business model, we are raising our full-year 2026 revenue and adjusted EPS outlook. Q3 2026 Company Results (All comparisons are year-over-year, unless otherwise noted) Allegion reported third-quarter 2026 net revenues of $1,172.6 million and net earnings of $220.9 million, or $2.59 per share. Adjusted net earnings were $221.0 million, or $2.60 per share, up 13.0%. (Details of the adjustments are included in the footnotes of the tables in this press release.) Third-quarter 2026 net revenues increased 9.6%. On an organic basis, which excludes impacts of acquisitions, divestitures and foreign currency movements, net revenues increased 7.2%, led by the Americas region. The organic revenue increase was driven by volume growth and price realization. Reported revenue reflects a 2.6% net positive impact from acquisitions and divestitures, as well as a 0.2% headwind from foreign currency. Third-quarter 2026 operating income was $284.6 million, an increase of $50.8 million or 21.7%. Adjusted operating income in third-quarter 2026 was $289.7 million, an increase of $32.3 million or 12.5%. Third-quarter 2026 operating margin was 24.3%, compared with 21.8%. The adjusted operating margin in third-quarter 2026 was 24.7%, compared with 24.1%. The adjusted operating margin increase is attributable to favorable volume leverage as well as price and productivity net of inflation and investment (PPII), inclusive of transactional foreign currency, which was positive on a dollar basis and a tailwind to margin rate. Q3 2026 Segment Results (All comparisons are year-over-year, unless otherwise noted) The Americas segment revenues were up 11.7% (up 9.1% on an organic basis). The non-residential business was up low-double digits organically, driven by volume growth and price realization. The residential business was up mid-single digits organically, driven by price realization. The reported revenue reflects a 2.6% positive impact from acquisitions. Adjusted operating margin in the region 1 increased 20 basis points to 30.1%. The adjusted operating margin increase is attributable to favorable volume leverage as well as PPII, inclusive of transactional foreign currency headwinds, which was positive on a dollar basis and a 20-basis point tailwind to margin rate. Acquisitions were a 40-basis point headwind to margin rate. The International segment revenues were up 1.8% (down 0.2% on an organic basis). The slight organic revenue decrease was driven by volume declines, which more than offset favorable pricing. Reported revenue reflects a 2.5% net positive impact from acquisitions and divestitures and a 0.5% headwind from foreign currency. Adjusted operating margin in the region decreased 40 basis points to 13.9%, driven by volume declines and unfavorable mix offset partially by a PPII tailwind and favorable impacts from acquisitions. IEEPA Tariff Refunds Third-quarter 2026 reported results include the impact of IEEPA tariff refunds (tariff refunds), which benefited operating income by $19.9 million and interest income by $0.9 million. These benefits are excluded from adjusted operating income and adjusted EPS and reflect the impact from refunds received and expected to be received from the U.S. government. Additional Items (All comparisons are year-over-year, unless otherwise noted) Interest expense for third-quarter 2026 was $24.2 million, a decrease of $2.4 million. Other income, net for third-quarter 2026 was $7.4 million, compared to other income, net of $1.7 million. Other income, net for third-quarter 2026 includes a $4.1 million gain on non-operating investments and $0.9 million of interest income related to tariff refunds. The company s effective tax rate for third-quarter 2026 was 17.5%, compared with 9.8%. The company s adjusted effective tax rate for third-quarter 2026 was 17.5%, compared with 14.9%. Cash Flow and Liquidity Year-to-date available cash flow for 2026 was $539.4 million, an increase of $54.2 million versus the prior-year period. The company ended third-quarter 2026 with cash and cash equivalents of $388.1 million and total debt of $2,032.0 million. Share Repurchase and Dividends In the third quarter of 2026, the company repurchased approximately 0.8 million shares for approximately $120 million and paid quarterly dividends of $0.55 per ordinary share or approximately $46 million. 2026 Full-Year Outlook (All comparisons are year-over-year, unless otherwise noted) The company is raising its full-year 2026 reported revenue growth outlook to a range of 9.5% to 10.5% and is raising its organic growth outlook to a range of 5.0% to 6.0%, after excluding the expected impacts of acquisitions, divestitures and foreign currency movements. The company is raising the outlook for full-year 2026 adjusted EPS to be in the range of $9.05 to $9.10. The company is updating the outlook for full-year 2026 reported EPS and expects it to be in the range of $8.35 to $8.40. Adjustments to 2026 EPS include estimated impacts of approximately $0.60 per share for acquisition-related amortization, $0.28 per share for restructuring, M A and other adjustments as well as $0.18 per share benefit for tariff refunds. 2 The outlook continues to assume a full-year adjusted effective tax rate of approximately 18% to 19%. The outlook assumes an average diluted share count for the full year of approximately 85.6 million shares.
