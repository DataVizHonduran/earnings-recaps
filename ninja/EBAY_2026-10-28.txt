Reports Third Quarter 2026 Results Revenue of $3.2 billion, up 14% on an as-reported basis and up 13% on an FX-Neutral basis Gross Merchandise Volume of $23.2 billion, up 15% on an as-reported basis and up 16% on an FX-Neutral basis GAAP and Non-GAAP earnings per diluted share of $1.11 and $1.51, respectively, on a continuing operations basis GAAP and Non-GAAP operating margins of 19.2% and 26.3%, respectively Returned $827 million to stockholders in Q3, including $689 million of share repurchases and $138 million paid in cash dividends San Jose, California, October 28, 2026 eBay Inc. (Nasdaq EBAY), a global commerce leader that connects millions of buyers and sellers around the world, today reported financial results for its third quarter ended September 30, 2026. eBay s strong third quarter results reflect continued momentum across our business priorities and customer segments as more consumers embrace recommerce, said Jamie Iannone, Chief Executive Officer at eBay. Our Focus Categories, C2C marketplace, and Recommerce leadership are driving strong, sustained growth, while innovations across eBay Live, Shipping, and our portfolio are making the platform more trusted, seamless, and compelling for buyers and sellers around the world. Our third quarter results demonstrate that eBay s strategy is delivering strong GMV growth and meaningful earnings expansion, said Peggy Alford, Chief Financial Officer at eBay. By investing with discipline in our highest strategic priorities and focusing on the areas where we have a clear competitive advantage, we are positioning eBay for durable, profitable growth and long-term shareholder value. Third Quarter 2026 Business Highlights eBay continued to enhance its C2C selling experience with comprehensive improvements in France and Italy, bringing together free selling, buyer protection, enhanced local discovery, eBay Balance and simplified shipping. The company expanded the latest generation of its AI-powered Magical Listing technology to all new and reactivated listers in the U.S., U.K., Germany, Australia, France and Italy. eBay Live delivered another record quarter and is generating Gross Merchandise Volume ( GMV ) at a multibillion-dollar annualized run rate. GMV growth remained well into the triple digits year over year, similar to recent quarters, as sellers, buyers, content hours and viewership continued to expand across all seven markets. eBay continued to enhance its Live shopping experience by introducing multistreaming capabilities that allow sellers to broadcast simultaneously across eBay Live and social platforms, and strengthened trust by introducing new features for sellers, including AI-powered moderation of chat, audio and video and enhanced content screening. Depop further strengthened its buying and selling experience by enabling faster personal discovery on the homepage, simplifying its AI-assisted listing flow and offering more affordable shipping options in the U.S. eBay created standout moments at New York and London Fashion Weeks with Endless Runway activations, showcasing rare archival and pre-loved fashion, celebrating iconic designers and leveraging eBay Live to bring the collection to buyers worldwide. In Motors Parts Accessories, eBay expanded Easy and Free Returns to Germany following successful U.S. and U.K. launches and introduced Certified Recycled badging in the U.S., helping buyers shop for compatible, quality parts with greater confidence. eBay s Vehicle Purchase Protection now covers eligible transactions up to $500,000. The company also facilitated several vehicle transactions exceeding $1 million, including the sale of a 2005 Porsche Carrera GT for $5 million. eBay expanded Authenticity Guarantee to eligible collectible coins in the U.S. through a partnership with Professional Coin Grading Service, a leading authority in coin grading and authentication. The company also extended trading card authentication services to a broader range of collectors by lowering the mandatory price thresholds in the U.S. and U.K. eBay expanded its Authenticity Guarantee service for luxury fashion across Europe, enabling buyers in 23 European Union countries to shop for authenticated Luxury Fashion from sellers in Germany, France and Italy. In Australia, eBay introduced authentication for watches, further strengthening trust and confidence in its global luxury resale marketplace. TCGplayer surpassed $1 billion in GMV over the trailing 12 months and began onboarding international sellers in the U.K. and Canada. Goldin achieved another quarter of record GMV and hosted an auction commemorating the 250th anniversary of the United States, highlighted by the $3.65 million sale of a rare 1776 Exeter Broadside copy of the Declaration of Independence. eBay expanded Managed Shipping to all new and reactivated listers in the U.S. for domestic transactions and launched eBay International Shipping in the U.K. and Germany, which will enable sellers to reach buyers in more than 190 markets by shipping to a domestic eBay hub. Impact In October, eBay released its sixth annual Recommerce Report, which found that 47% of consumers surveyed purchase secondhand goods at least monthly, up from 35% in 2025, underscoring the growing adoption of recommerce worldwide. eBay for Charity enabled buyers and sellers on the platform to raise $59 million worldwide in the third quarter. eBay Foundation s Global Give Program granted nearly $3 million to over 30 nonprofits around the world that are supporting entrepreneurs in geographies where eBayers live and work. eBay was recognized with numerous awards, including Forbes World s Best Employers 2026, TIME s World s Best Companies 2026, Newsweek s America's Best Online Platforms 2026 and Business Insider s America's Most Innovative Businesses 2027. Non-GAAP Measures of Financial Performance To supplement the company's condensed consolidated financial statements presented in accordance with generally accepted accounting principles, or GAAP, the company uses non-GAAP measures of certain components of financial performance. These non-GAAP measures include non-GAAP net income, non-GAAP earnings per diluted share, non-GAAP operating income and margin, non-GAAP effective tax rate, free cash flow and figures in this press release presented on an FX-Neutral basis. These non-GAAP financial measures are presented on a continuing operations basis. These non-GAAP measures are not in accordance with, or an alternative to, measures prepared in accordance with GAAP and may be different from non-GAAP measures used by other companies. In addition, these non-GAAP measures are not based on any comprehensive set of accounting rules or principles. Non-GAAP measures have limitations in that they do not reflect all of the amounts associated with the company's results of operations as determined in accordance with GAAP. These measures should only be used to evaluate the company's results of operations in conjunction with the corresponding GAAP measures. Reconciliation to the nearest GAAP measure of all non-GAAP measures included in this press release, except for figures in this press release presented on an FX-Neutral basis, can be found in the tables included in this press release. For figures in this press release reported on an FX-Neutral basis, the company calculates the year-over-year impact of foreign currency movements using prior period foreign currency rates, excluding hedging activity, applied to current year transactional currency amounts. These non-GAAP measures are provided to enhance investors' overall understanding of the company's current financial performance and its prospects for the future. Specifically, the company believes the non-GAAP measures provide useful information to both management and investors by excluding certain expenses, gains and losses, or net purchases of property and equipment, as the case may be, that may not be indicative of its core operating results and business outlook. In addition, because the company has historically reported certain non-GAAP results to investors, the company believes that the inclusion of non-GAAP measures provides consistency in the company's financial reporting. For its internal budgeting process, and as discussed further below, the company's management uses financial measures that do not include stock-based compensation expense, employer payroll taxes on stock-based compensation, amortization or impairment of acquired intangible assets, impairment of goodwill, amortization of deferred tax assets associated with the realignment of its legal structure and related foreign exchange effects, significant gains or losses from the disposal acquisition of a business, certain gains and losses on investments including changes in fair value, changes in foreign currency exchange rates and the impact of any related foreign exchange derivative instruments, gains or losses associated with a warrant agreement that the company entered into with Adyen, restructuring-related charges and the income taxes associated with the foregoing. In addition to the corresponding GAAP measures, the company's management also uses the foregoing non-GAAP measures in reviewing the financial results of the company. The company excludes the following items from non-GAAP net income, non-GAAP earnings per diluted share, non-GAAP operating income and margin and non-GAAP effective tax rate Stock-based compensation expense and related employer payroll taxes. This expense consists of expenses for stock options, restricted stock and employee stock purchases. The company excludes stock-based compensation expense from its non-GAAP measures primarily because they are non-cash expenses that management does not believe are reflective of ongoing operating results. The related employer payroll taxes are dependent on the company's stock price and the vesting of restricted stock by employees and the timing and size of stock option exercises, over which management has limited to no control, and as such management does not believe it correlates to the company's operation of the business. Amortization or impairment of acquired intangible assets, impairment of goodwill, certain amortization of deferred tax assets and related foreign exchange effects, and certain gains or losses on investments. The company incurs amortization or impairment of acquired intangible assets and goodwill in connection with acquisitions and excludes these amounts from its non-GAAP measures. The company also excludes certain gains and losses on investments. The company excludes the non-cash amortization of deferred tax assets associated with the realignment of its legal structure, which is not reduced by the effects of the Tax Cuts and Jobs Act, and related foreign exchange effects. The company excludes these items because management does not believe they correlate to the ongoing operating results of the company's business. Restructuring. These charges consist of expenses for employee severance and other exit and disposal costs. The company excludes significant restructuring charges primarily because management does not believe they are reflective of ongoing operating results. Transaction related costs . The company may incur significant gains or losses and incremental costs related to the acquisition or disposal of a business, unsolicited proposals and shareholder activism matters. These costs include third party fees such as banker fees, legal and advisory fees and other professional services. The company excludes these items from its non-GAAP measures as management does not believe they correlate to the company's ongoing operating results of the business. Other certain significant gains, losses, or charges that are not indicative of the company s core operating results. These are significant gains, losses, or charges during a period that are the result of isolated events or transactions which have not occurred frequently in the past and are not expected to occur regularly or be repeated in the future. The company excludes these amounts from its results primarily because management does not believe they are indicative of its current or ongoing operating results. These amounts include changes in fair value and the related change in foreign currency exchange rates of equity securities with readily determinable fair values, globally. This rate could change for various reasons including significant changes in our geographic earnings mix or fundamental tax law changes in major jurisdictions in which we operate. In addition to the non-GAAP measures discussed above, the company also uses free cash flow. Free cash flow represents operating cash flows less purchases of property and equipment. The company considers free cash flow to be a liquidity measure that provides useful information to management and investors about the amount of cash generated by the business after the purchases of property, buildings, and equipment, which can then be used to, among other things, invest in the company's business, make strategic acquisitions, repurchase stock and pay dividends.
