(NYSE EL) today reported its financial results for the first quarter ended September 30, 2026. Our first quarter of fiscal 2027 marks another powerful step forward for The Est e Lauder Companies. Growth is accelerating, profitability is strengthening significantly, and the impact of Beauty Reimagined is becoming increasingly evident across our business. We are bringing more breakthrough innovation to consumers, amplifying the desirability of our brands with bold, high impact campaigns, and building a faster, more agile organization that is converting momentum into stronger financial performance, said St phane de La Faverie, President and CEO. We have moved beyond restoring growth. We are building diverse drivers for sustained profitable growth. The energy and determination of our teams, combined with the strength of our brands and the progress of our transformation, give me greater confidence than ever in the opportunities that lie ahead. We are raising our fiscal 2027 outlook, reflecting both our strong start and our conviction in the future we are creating. In addition to the Operational Highlights above, below are the drivers of the Company s performance. Skin Care Skin Care net sales increased 4%, primarily driven by growth from La Mer and The Ordinary. Net sales increased from La Mer, benefitting from innovation, including the new The Concentrate Serum, as well as continued success of The Treatment Lotion product franchise. The Ordinary net sales increased, reflecting targeted expanded consumer reach, along with growth in existing distribution supported by key activations, by the success of its Glycolic Acid 7% Exfoliating Toner and the launch of Lacto PDRN 4% + B9 Eye Cream. Skin Care operating income increased 40%, primarily due to the increase in net sales as well as the reduction in non-consumer-facing expenses, partially offset by the net increase in consumer-facing investments, including to support new product launches, key activations and distribution expansion. Makeup Makeup net sales grew 1%, primarily driven by M A C, partially offset by the decline from Est e Lauder. Net sales increased from M A C, benefitting from targeted expanded consumer reach—including the fiscal 2026 second-half launches in select U.S. and Middle East Sephora locations, in Sephora at Kohl s and online in these markets—which, along with the launches of Lustreglass Sheer-Shine Lipstick and Lustreglass Stainglass Lip Tint, fueled the growth in the lip subcategory. Est e Lauder net sales declined, reflecting lower net sales in the face subcategory, including the timing of fiscal 2027 new product launches, partially offset by incremental sales from the next-generation of Double Wear Stay-in-Place Longwear Matte Foundation that launched in the second half of fiscal 2026. Makeup operating results improved to income from a loss in the prior year, primarily due to the increase in net sales as well as the reduction in non-consumer-facing expenses. This was partially offset by the net increase in consumer-facing investments—net of the strategic reduction in selling expenses—including those to support distribution expansion, key activations and new product launches. Fragrance Fragrance net sales increased 20%, driven by double-digit growth from the Company s Luxury Brands—led by Le Labo, TOM FORD, Jo Malone London and KILIAN PARIS—and from Est e Lauder. Page 7 of 19 Net sales increased from Le Labo, primarily due to the ongoing success of its Classic Collection, including Another 13 and Santal 33, and its City Exclusives—including Gaiac 10 and the launch of Shiu 25, the Beijing City Exclusive. This reflects growth from both targeted expanded consumer reach and existing distribution. TOM FORD net sales increased, benefitting from innovation across both the Private Blend and Signature product franchises—including Tobacco Chocolat and Ombr Leather Reserve—which created a halo effect that supported existing product sales growth. Net sales from Jo Malone London increased, driven by innovation—such as Sea Salt Bergamot—and growth from hero product franchises, both supported by targeted expanded consumer reach. The net sales growth from Est e Lauder was driven by the launch of Glimmer, the brand s first gourmand scent. Net sales increased from KILIAN PARIS—the Company s fastest growing Luxury Brand—reflecting innovation success from The Cocktails Collection and continued strength from its hero products, along with growth from targeted expanded consumer reach. Fragrance operating income increased 93%, primarily due to the increase in net sales, partially offset by increased consumer-facing investments, including those to support new product launches, key activations and distribution expansion. Hair Care operating results improved to income from a loss in the prior year, reflecting the reduction in non-consumer-facing expenses and lower cost of sales.
