QUARTERLY FINANCIAL HIGHLIGHTS (Third-Quarter 2026 vs. Third-Quarter 2025) Third-quarter 2026 revenues totaled $16.6 billion, a decrease of $71 million, or relatively flat on a percentage basis compared to the prior-year quarter, reflecting an operational decrease of $57 million and an unfavorable impact of foreign exchange of $15 million. The operational decrease was driven by a decline in revenues from COVID-19 products, the Prevnar family and several other products across categories, largely offset by an increase in revenues for Eliquis, Padcev, the Vyndaqel family, Nurtec, Lorbrena and several other products across categories. Excluding contributions from Comirnaty and Paxlovid, revenues for the third quarter grew 7% operationally. Additionally, third-quarter revenues of our Launched and Acquired Products (1) grew 32% operationally. Third-quarter 2026 operational revenue reflected higher revenues primarily for Eliquis globally, up $513 million, or 25%, operationally, driven primarily by higher net price in the U.S. primarily due to pricing dynamics, including lower rebates and channel mix favorability, as well as higher demand globally partially offset by declines due to generic entry and price erosion in certain international markets Padcev globally, up $255 million, or 55%, operationally, driven primarily by increased market share in first-line locally advanced or metastatic urothelial cancer (la mUC), as well as launch uptake in the cisplatin-ineligible indication for muscle-invasive bladder cancer Vyndaqel family (Vyndaqel, Vyndamax, Vynmac) globally, up $193 million, or 12%, operationally, driven primarily by continued market expansion in the U.S., as well as strong demand with continuing uptake in patient diagnosis across international markets and improved access in certain international markets Nurtec ODT Vydura globally, up $135 million, or 33%, operationally, driven primarily by strong demand in the U.S., as well as launch uptake in certain international markets and Lorbrena globally, up $114 million, or 43%, operationally, driven primarily by increased patient share in the first-line ALK-positive metastatic non-small cell lung cancer (ALK+ mNSCLC) treatment setting in the U.S., China, and certain other international markets - 4 - offset primarily by lower revenues for Paxlovid globally, down $771 million, or 63%, operationally, driven primarily by lower COVID-19 infections across the U.S. and international markets, partially offset by timing of government purchases in certain emerging markets Comirnaty globally, down $339 million, or 29%, operationally, driven primarily by a lower favorable adjustment to the U.S. returns provision, lower utilization in the U.S. primarily resulting from narrower vaccine recommendations, as well as lower contractual deliveries in certain international markets and Prevnar family globally, down $180 million, or 10%, operationally, driven primarily in the U.S. by lower vaccination rates in the adult and pediatric indications, as well as market share erosion for the adult indication and by lower demand in the pediatric indication in certain international markets. RECENT NOTABLE DEVELOPMENTS (Since August 4, 2026) Product Developments Product Project Milestone Recent Development Link Comirnaty (COVID-19 Vaccine, mRNA) Regulatory August 2026. Pfizer and BioNTech announced U.S. Food and Drug Administration (FDA) approval for the companies 2026-2027 COVID-19 vaccine formula targeting the XFG variant for use in adults ages 65 years and older, as well as in individuals ages 5 through 64 years with at least one underlying condition that puts them at high risk for severe outcomes from COVID-19. Full Release - 6 - Product Project Milestone Recent Development Link Elrexfio (elranatamab) Phase 3 Results October 2026. Announced topline results from the investigational Phase 3 MagnetisMM-32 study evaluating Elrexfio as monotherapy in adults with relapsed or refractory multiple myeloma (RRMM) who received at least one prior line of treatment with anti-CD38 therapy and lenalidomide. At the pre-specified interim analysis for efficacy, the study met its primary endpoint of progression-free survival (PFS) as assessed by blinded independent central review (BICR). Elrexfio demonstrated a statistically significant and clinically meaningful reduction in the risk of disease progression or death versus investigator s choice of elotuzumab, pomalidomide and dexamethasone (EloPd) pomalidomide, bortezomib and dexamethasone (PVd) or carfilzomib and dexamethasone (Kd). The safety profile of Elrexfio in MagnetisMM-32 was consistent with its known profile and no new safety signals were identified. Full Release Litfulo (ritlecitinib) Phase 3 Results October 2026. Presented results from two investigational Phase 3 trials -- TRANQUILLO 2 and TRANQUILLO in which once-daily oral Litfulo significantly improved facial and total body repigmentation at Week 52 compared to placebo in patients with nonsegmental vitiligo (NSV). The safety profile of Litfulo in NSV was consistent with the established safety profile in alopecia areata. Pfizer intends to submit these data to regulatory authorities globally. Full Release Talzenna (talazoparib) Regulatory October 2026. Announced FDA expanded approval of Talzenna in combination with Xtandi expanding use to the treatment of men with metastatic androgen pathway modulation-na ve or -sensitive (mAPMN S) prostate cancer with homologous recombination repair (HRR) gene mutations, including both BRCA and non-BRCA mutations. Approval was based on the pivotal TALAPRO-3 data, offering a potential new standard of care earlier in the course of disease. Full Release Tukysa (tucatinib) Regulatory October 2026. Announced FDA approval of Tukysa in combination with trastuzumab and pertuzumab for the maintenance treatment of adults with unresectable locally advanced or metastatic human epidermal growth factor receptor 2-positive (HER2+) breast cancer following induction treatment. The approval expands the use of Tukysa to frontline, an earlier stage of metastatic disease, offering a new chemotherapy-free maintenance treatment option that may help further delay disease progression. Full Release Pipeline Developments A comprehensive update of Pfizer s development pipeline was published today and is now available at www.pfizer.com science drug-product-pipeline. It includes an overview of Pfizer s research and a list of compounds in development with targeted indication and phase of development, as well as mechanism of action for some candidates in Phase 1 and all candidates from Phase 2 through registration. - 7 - Product Project Milestone Recent Development Link 25-valent pneumococcal conjugate vaccine candidate (PF-07872412) Phase 2 Results October 2026. Announced detailed results across serotypes from a Phase 2 study (NCT06524414) investigating 25-valent pneumococcal conjugate vaccine candidate (25vPnC) in infants. In the study, a four-dose series of 25vPnC elicited robust immune responses against all 25 serotypes and showed safety and tolerability comparable to four doses of Prevnar 20. These data support the ongoing Phase 3 development program for 25vPnC. Full Release Lyme disease vaccine candidate (PF-07307405) Regulatory August 2026. Pfizer and Valneva SE announced the European Medicines Agency (EMA) validated the Marketing Authorization Application (MAA) for the companies 6-valent outer surface protein A (OspA)-based Lyme disease vaccine candidate and will begin its assessment of the application. The submission was based on favorable efficacy and safety results from the Phase 3 VALOR Vaccine Against Lyme for Outdoor Recreationists trial. Full Release Tilrekimig (PF-07275315) Phase 2 Results October 2026. Presented detailed results from an ongoing Phase 2 study investigating the potentially first-in-class trispecific antibody tilrekimig in adults with moderate-to-severe atopic dermatitis (AD). The study met its primary endpoint, demonstrating a statistically significant increase in the percentage of participants achieving EASI-75 ( 75% reduction in the Eczema Area and Severity Index) at Week 16 across all evaluated doses compared to placebo. Tilrekimig was well-tolerated, with no dose-dependent safety signals, with treatment-emergent adverse event rates comparable between treatment and placebo groups. The Phase 3 pivotal program is underway with ongoing studies in AD and asthma. Tilrekimig is also being studied in an ongoing Phase 2b 3 study in chronic obstructive pulmonary disease (COPD).
