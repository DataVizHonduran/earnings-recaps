Revenue Revenue in the third quarter of 2026 increased $2.4 billion to $6.4 billion as compared to the third quarter of 2025, primarily due to the acquisition of IPG, which closed on November 26, 2025, and constant currency revenue growth. Revenue in the third quarter of 2026 includes $412.1 million from dispositions and assets held for sale. Expenses Operating expenses increased $1.9 billion to $5.4 billion in the third quarter of 2026 compared to the third quarter of 2025, primarily due to the acquisition of IPG. Included in operating expenses in the third quarter of 2026 are $40.4 million of integration and transaction costs related to the acquisition of IPG, $84.7 million of repositioning costs, primarily related to real estate and other exit charges, and a $106.8 million gain on the disposition of a subsidiary. Page 2 Salary and service costs increased $1.7 billion to $4.6 billion, primarily due to the IPG acquisition and constant currency revenue growth. These costs tend to fluctuate with changes in revenue and are comprised of salary and related costs, which include employee compensation and benefits costs, freelance labor, third-party service costs, and third-party incidental costs. Salary and related costs increased $1.1 billion to $2.9 billion, due to revenue growth and the acquisition of IPG. Third-party service costs increased $552.4 million to $1.5 billion, primarily due to growth in our Integrated Media discipline and the acquisition of IPG. Third-party incidental costs increased $24.3 million to $211.7 million, primarily due to revenue growth and the acquisition of IPG. Occupancy and other costs, which are less directly linked to changes in revenue than salary and service costs, increased $176.6 million to $499.3 million, primarily due to the acquisition of IPG. SG A expenses increased $37.4 million to $200.9 million, primarily due to the acquisition of IPG. Included in SG A expenses in the third quarter of 2026 are $40.4 million of integration and transaction costs related to the acquisition of IPG, compared to $60.8 million in the third quarter of 2025. Operating Income Operating income increased $461.6 million to $991.7 million in the third quarter of 2026 compared to the third quarter of 2025, primarily as a result of revenue growth and the IPG acquisition, partially offset by costs primarily related to the integration of IPG. Interest Expense, net Net interest expense in the third quarter of 2026 increased $61.0 million to $104.2 million compared to the third quarter of 2025, primarily due to debt assumed as part of the IPG acquisition and the refinancing activities in the first quarter of 2026. Interest expense increased $72.4 million to $132.8 million. Interest income increased $11.4 million to $28.6 million. Income Taxes Our effective tax rate for the third quarter of 2026 was 24.3% compared to 27.2% for the third quarter of 2025. The 2026 effective tax rate included the impacts of favorable settlements of uncertain tax positions. The effective tax rates for 2025 reflect the impact of the lower tax benefit associated with integration costs, severance, and repositioning charges related to the acquisition of IPG. Excluding these items, our Non-GAAP adjusted effective tax rate for the third quarter of 2026 was 25.0% compared to 26.0% for the third quarter of 2025. Net Income Omnicom Group Inc. and Diluted Net Income per Share Net Income - Omnicom Group Inc. for the third quarter of 2026 increased $314.7 million to $656.0 million compared to the third quarter of 2025. Weighted-average diluted shares outstanding for the third quarter of 2026 increased to 275.4 million from 194.9 million, primarily as a result of shares issued for the IPG acquisition, partially offset by net share repurchases, including shares purchased pursuant to the accelerated stock repurchase agreement. Diluted net income per share of $2.38 increased by $0.63 from $1.75 in the prior year period. Non-GAAP Adjusted Net Income per Share - Diluted for the third quarter of 2026 increased $0.49, or 21.9%, to $2.73 from $2.24. Non-GAAP Adjusted Net Income per Share - Diluted for the third quarters of 2026 and 2025 excluded $88.9 million and $15.9 million, respectively, of after-tax amortization expense. Non-GAAP Adjusted Net Income per Share - Diluted for the third quarter of 2026 also excluded $13.3 million of after-tax integration and transaction costs, $63.5 million of after-tax severance and repositioning costs, and a $69.5 million after-tax gain on the disposition of a subsidiary. In 2025, Non-GAAP Adjusted Net Income per Share - Diluted excluded $50.8 million of costs related to the acquisition of IPG and $28.4 million of after-tax Page 3 severance and repositioning costs. We present Non-GAAP Adjusted Net Income per Share - Diluted to allow for comparability with the prior year period. EBITA EBITA increased $560.2 million to $1,111.8 million in the third quarter of 2026 compared to the third quarter of 2025. Adjusted EBITA increased $479.1 million, or 73.6%, to $1,130.1 million in the third quarter of 2026 compared to the third quarter of 2025, and the related margin increased to 17.6% from 16.1%. EBITA and Adjusted EBITA excluded amortization expense of $120.1 million and $21.5 million in the third quarters of 2026 and 2025, respectively. Adjusted EBITA in the third quarter of 2026 also excluded $40.4 million of costs related to the integration of IPG, severance and repositioning costs of $84.7 million, and a $106.8 million gain on the disposition of a subsidiary. Adjusted EBITA in the third quarter of 2025 also excluded $60.8 million of costs related to the acquisition of IPG and $38.6 million of severance and repositioning costs. Organic growth calculated by subtracting the foreign exchange rate impact and the acquisition revenue from total revenue growth, which is equal to the current period revenue from Core Operations minus the prior period revenue from Core Operations. Foreign exchange rate impact on core operations calculated by translating the current period s local currency revenue using the prior period average exchange rates to derive current period constant currency revenue. The foreign exchange rate impact is the difference between the current period revenue in U.S. Dollars and the current period constant currency revenue. Percentage change Calculated by dividing the individual component amount by the prior period Core Operations revenue base.
