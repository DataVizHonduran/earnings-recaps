Exec Summary

Lockheed Martin is navigating a period of stagnant quarterly revenue growth paired with a strategic pivot toward scaled munitions production via new multi-year framework agreements with the U.S. government (LMT, 2026-04-23). While the company faces a significant short-term decline in free cash flow and net earnings compared to the previous year, it maintains a bullish full-year 2026 outlook with projected operating profit growth of approximately 25% (LMT, 2026-04-23). The macro picture is characterized by high demand for layered missile defense and fifth-generation fighter jets amidst a transition toward "commercially inspired" contracting models (LMT, 2026-04-23).

Consensus vs Outliers

  • Confirmation: The report confirms a high-demand environment for advanced defense technology, specifically "layered missile defense architecture" and "fifth generation fighter jets" (LMT, 2026-04-23).
  • Deviation: While revenue remained flat year-over-year, the company is deviating from traditional contracting by utilizing "groundbreaking" framework agreements to scale production rates by "3-4 times current rates," signaling a shift in how the industry interacts with government procurement (LMT, 2026-04-23).

Key Findings

  • First quarter 2026 sales were $18.021 billion, remaining nearly flat compared to $17.963 billion in the first quarter of 2025 (LMT, 2026-04-23).
  • Net earnings decreased to $1.488 billion from $1.712 billion in the prior year period (LMT, 2026-04-23).
  • Free cash flow turned negative at $(291) million, a sharp decline from $955 million in Q1 2025, primarily driven by "higher working capital largely as a result of timing of billing activities" (LMT, 2026-04-23).
  • The company signed framework agreements to accelerate production of "advanced Patriot Missile, THAAD, and PrSM" (LMT, 2026-04-23).
  • Full-year 2026 guidance anticipates sales growth of approximately 5% and operating profit growth of approximately 25% (LMT, 2026-04-23).
  • Segment performance was mixed: Missiles and Fire Control and Space saw higher sales, while RMS and Aeronautics experienced lower sales (LMT, 2026-04-23).
