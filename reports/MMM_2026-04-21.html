Exec Summary

3M is navigating a "volatile environment" by focusing on a value creation framework centered on operational simplification and portfolio reshaping (MMM, 2026-04-21). While GAAP EPS declined 40% year-on-year, adjusted metrics show growth in EPS and operating margins (MMM, 2026-04-21). The company maintains a stable outlook, reiterating its full-year 2026 guidance for sales growth and margin expansion (MMM, 2026-04-21).

Consensus vs Outliers

The report confirms a trend of margin expansion, with adjusted operating income margin increasing by 30 bps to 23.8% (MMM, 2026-04-21). However, it reveals a divergence between GAAP and Adjusted organic sales; while adjusted organic sales grew 1.2%, GAAP organic sales were down 1.4% (MMM, 2026-04-21).

Key Findings

  • Q1 GAAP sales reached $6.0 billion, representing a 1.3% increase year-on-year (MMM, 2026-04-21).
  • Adjusted EPS rose to $2.14, a 14% increase compared to the previous year (MMM, 2026-04-21).
  • The company returned $2.4 billion to shareholders through share repurchases and dividends (MMM, 2026-04-21).
  • Full-year 2026 guidance expects adjusted total sales growth of 1 to 4 percent and adjusted EPS between $8.50 and $8.70 (MMM, 2026-04-21).
  • Significant litigation and PFAS-related costs continue to impact GAAP results, with "Manufactured PFAS products" contributing 0.18 to special items (MMM, 2026-04-21).

Manufactured PFAS products These amounts relate to sales and income (loss) and associated activity regarding manufactured PFAS products that 3M exited by the end of 2025.
