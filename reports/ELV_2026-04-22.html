Exec Summary

Elevance Health demonstrates underlying business strength in 1Q 2026, characterized by revenue growth and an upward revision of full-year adjusted EPS guidance (ELV, 2026-04-22). While the company is facing "expected elevated medical cost trend" in Medicaid and membership declines in specific risk-based segments, these are being offset by higher premium yields and growth in CarelonRx (ELV, 2026-04-22). The macro picture is further complicated by a significant accrual related to a "CMS matter," though the company maintains a strong cash flow position (ELV, 2026-04-22).

Consensus vs Outliers

The report confirms a broader industry trend of rising medical costs, specifically within Medicaid, but deviates from a purely negative narrative by raising full-year adjusted EPS guidance to "at least $26.75" based on "increased visibility" and "underlying business strength" (ELV, 2026-04-22).

Key Findings

  • Financial Performance: 1Q 2026 operating revenue reached $49.5 billion, a 1.5% increase over 1Q 2025 (ELV, 2026-04-22).
  • Guidance Update: FY 2026 adjusted diluted EPS guidance was raised to "at least $26.75," while diluted EPS guidance is set at "at least $19.85," the latter including the financial impact of the "CMS matter" (ELV, 2026-04-22).
  • Medical Cost Trends: The benefit expense ratio rose 40 basis points to 86.8%, driven by "expected elevated medical cost trend in our Medicaid business," though this was partially offset by "improved performance in Medicare" (ELV, 2026-04-22).
  • Regulatory Exposure: The company recorded a $935 million accrual as its "current best estimate of the identified potential exposure related to the CMS notice" (ELV, 2026-04-22).
  • Membership Dynamics: Total medical membership was approximately 45.4 million as of March 31, 2026; growth in commercial fee-based membership was offset by "anticipated reductions in our Medicare Advantage and Employer Group risk membership" (ELV, 2026-04-22).
  • Segment Growth: Carelon operating revenue grew 7.9% year-over-year to $18.0 billion, fueled by "CarelonRx product revenue" and "scaling of Carelon Services risk-based solutions" (ELV, 2026-04-22).
  • Capital Allocation: The company returned $1.5 billion to shareholders in 1Q 2026, including $1.1 billion in share repurchases (ELV, 2026-04-22).
